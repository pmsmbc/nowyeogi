Search for a room in Dongdaemun and you get a mix of big hotels facing DDP and tiny guesthouses down side streets. Counting nine neighbourhoods (*dong*) with Seoul's official licensing data gives a clearer picture:

- **88 tourist lodgings**, of which only 11 are hotels and **77 are hostels**
- **61 of those 77 hostels were registered in 2025 or 2026**, 51 of them this year alone
- **Changsin-dong (19 hostels) ranks second of all Seoul neighbourhoods, and Jongno 5-ga (17) third**
- **231 urban homestays for foreign tourists**, mostly in Sindang-dong, Changsin-dong, Sungin-dong and Gwanghui-dong 2-ga

In short, Dongdaemun's big hotels sit **south-east of DDP (Gwanghui-dong 2-ga, Euljiro 6-ga)** and **next to Heunginjimun Gate (Jongno 6-ga)**. Almost everything else is **small hostels and old inns in Changsin-dong, Sungin-dong and Jongno 5-ga**. If you are still choosing between Seoul neighbourhoods, [where to stay in Seoul](/en/posts/seoul-where-to-stay-2026-09/) compares them side by side.

## How these were counted

The figures come from four licensing datasets on the Seoul Open Data Plaza: tourist lodging, general lodging, urban homestays for foreign tourists, and hanok (traditional house) stays. Only businesses marked as operating and with a legal address in the nine *dong* below were counted. The tourist and general lodging data were updated on 24 September 2026, the homestay data on 27 September and the hanok data on 23 September.

| Area | Legal neighbourhoods |

| DDP side (Jung-gu) | Gwanghui-dong 1-ga and 2-ga, Euljiro 6-ga and 7-ga, Sindang-dong |
| Heunginjimun and Dongmyo side (Jongno-gu) | Jongno 5-ga and 6-ga, Changsin-dong, Sungin-dong |

Dongdaemun straddles two districts, Jung-gu and Jongno-gu, split by the Cheonggyecheon stream. **Euljiro 5-ga, Ojang-dong and Ssangnim-dong**, just west of Dongdaemun History & Culture Park Station, overlap with the [Euljiro accommodation](/en/posts/euljiro-hotels-2026-09/) guide, so they are left out of the table and their hotels are listed separately below. By registration year, the 77 hostels in the nine neighbourhoods break down as **16 before 2025, 10 in 2025 and 51 in 2026 (to September)**.

- **All of Jongno 5-ga's 17 hostels except one (March 2025) were registered in 2026.** Most of those with a listed room count have **1-4 rooms**, often one or two floors of a building on Jong-ro. 225 Jong-ro has three different hostels on floors 2, 3 and 4; the GLO STAR building at 24 Dongho-ro 38-gil has two, on floors 4 and 5
- **14 of Changsin-dong's 19 were registered since 2025.** They cluster on Jong-ro 46-gil and 54-gil and along Jong-ro itself (nos. 294-334). The oldest are 24 Guesthouse Dongdaemun Market (2014) and Dongdaemun Seoul Dalbit (2016)

- **All four in Gwanghui-dong 1-ga were registered between April and August 2026**

Hostels are tourist lodging, so unlike homestays they **take Korean nationals as well as foreigners**. With 80% opened since 2025, many will have few reviews yet, and some "hostels" are mostly private rooms, so check the room types on the booking site.

## Changsin-dong and Sungin-dong: old inns and hanok

Changsin-dong, east of the gate, and Sungin-dong around Dongmyo shrine have the most lodging outside the tourist category: **25 and 31 places respectively**. Most are **inns licensed in the 1960s to 80s**.

- Of **Sungin-dong's** 31, 26 are *yeogwan* inns and 4 are *yeoinsuk* (the simplest category of inn). They cluster on Bomun-ro 1-gil, Nangye-ro 29-gil and Jong-ro 58-gil, and many now trade under guesthouse-style names such as Gaga House Dongdaemun, One More Hostel and Cloud Stay Jongno
- **Changsin-dong** has 15 *yeogwan* and 7 *yeoinsuk*, concentrated around Jong-ro 46ga-gil and 48-gil
- Closed lodging records are also numerous: **41 in Changsin-dong and 33 in Sungin-dong**

**All 11 hanok stays are on the Jongno-gu side.** There are **three on Naksanseonggwakseo-gil in Jongno 6-ga**, below the Naksan section of the city wall, two in Changsin-dong and six in Sungin-dong; Migamjae and Jayeonjae in Sungin-dong were both registered on 3 September 2026. Under the Tourism Promotion Act a hanok stay offers overnight experiences in a traditional house, and unlike foreign-tourist homestays it is not limited to foreign guests.

## 231 homestays

Korea's *urban homestay for foreign tourists* licence lets **a resident host foreign tourists in the home they live in**, under the Tourism Promotion Act Enforcement Decree. **Korean nationals cannot normally stay**, and the host must live there. The rules are explained in more detail in the [Yeonnam-dong and Mangwon-dong accommodation](/en/posts/yeonnam-hotels-2026-09/) guide.

That is small next to Hongdae (Seogyo-dong alone has 356 of Seoul's 7,365), but **135 of the 231 (58%) were registered since 2025**, and **32 of Sindang-dong's 67 were registered this year**.

- **30 of Gwanghui-dong 2-ga's 38 homestays have a single room.** They are in the residential lanes south of DDP, on Toegye-ro 66-gil and Jangchungdan-ro 10-gil
- **Changsin-dong** has many detached houses (19) and multi-household houses (21), and five homestays on **Naksanseonggwakdong-gil**, the hillside lane along the wall
- **Gwanghui-dong 1-ga, Euljiro 6-ga and Jongno 5-ga have no homestays at all.** They are mostly shops and main roads

Homestays often have no lift and no 24-hour reception, and Changsin-dong and Sungin-dong are hilly. If you are arriving late with a suitcase, ask for check-in instructions in advance and look at the slope on a map.

## A market that works at night

What sets Dongdaemun apart from other neighbourhoods is that **its wholesale trade runs through the night**.

- According to Jung-gu's tourism site, the **Dongdaemun special tourist zone was designated on 23 May 2002**, and its **30-odd shopping buildings with around 30,000 stores** welcome customers from around the world **24 hours a day**
- Seoul's city magazine Seoul Sarang (March 2014) described Dongdaemun Fashion Town as **getting busier towards dawn, with the streets filling up as people carry and load goods bound for the provinces**

So a room in the same block as a wholesale mall may sit on a street that is **busy with people and freight vehicles from night until dawn**. For a quiet night, choose **a higher floor in a large hotel or somewhere a few blocks from the mall buildings**, and search recent reviews for mentions of noise before booking. No public block-by-block noise measurements or complaint statistics could be found.

## Getting to the airport

**The AREX airport railroad does not reach Dongdaemun, but two airport bus routes do.** [[bus:6002]] is closer to Heunginjimun and Dongmyo, and [[bus:6001]] to the south side of DDP. Both cost ₩17,000 (about $12.75) for adults. ## Which to choose

- **A big hotel by the airport bus**: Novotel, Splaisir or Toyoko Inn II south-east of DDP (6001), or JW Marriott beside Heunginjimun (6002)
- **A dawn flight**: near Dongdaemun Station, where the N6002 stops at Dongdaemun (Heunginjimun)
- **A late-evening flight**: the last 6001 leaves before 20:00, so plan on [[line:4]] from Dongdaemun History & Culture Park to Seoul Station and AREX
- **A tight budget, travelling solo**: hostels in Changsin-dong, Jongno 5-ga or Sungin-dong. With 61 of 77 registered since 2025, many have few reviews yet
- **A night in a hanok**: the 11 hanok stays in Jongno 6-ga and Sungin-dong, below the Naksan city wall
- **A foreign visitor who wants a residential lane**: homestays in Sindang-dong, Changsin-dong or Sungin-dong (not open to Korean nationals)
- **A quiet night**: a few blocks from the wholesale malls, or a higher floor in a large hotel

## Summary

- The nine Dongdaemun neighbourhoods have **88 tourist lodgings with 2,777 rooms**: 11 hotels and 77 hostels.
- The biggest hotels are **Novotel (523), Skypark Kingstown (408), Splaisir (215), Toyoko Inn II (207), Mangrove (177) and JW Marriott (170)**.
- **61 of the 77 hostels were registered since 2025**; Changsin-dong ranks second in Seoul and Jongno 5-ga third.
- Mercure, Travelodge, Nine Tree and Ramada Dongdaemun are **just outside the boundary, in Euljiro 5-ga**.
- There are **231 homestays**, concentrated in Sindang-dong, Changsin-dong, Sungin-dong and Gwanghui-dong 2-ga, and all 11 hanok stays are on the Jongno-gu side.
- Airport buses: **6002 from Dongdaemun (Heunginjimun), last 20:39**; **6001 from DDP, last 19:56**; and the late-night **N6002**.
- For AREX, **change at Seoul Station** to [[line:4]] or [[line:1]].
