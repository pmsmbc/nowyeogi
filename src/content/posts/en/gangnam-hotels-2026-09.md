Search for a hotel in Gangnam and you get everything at once: the big towers wrapped around COEX, small business hotels in the back streets of Yeoksam, and one-room homestays in apartment blocks. Counting Gangnam-gu with Seoul's own licensing data gives this picture.

- **84 tourist accommodation businesses** (tourist hotels, hostels and the like). That is 10% of Seoul's 815, third after Jung-gu and Jongno-gu
- **8,646 tourist-hotel rooms**, 15% of Seoul's 58,306 and second only to Jung-gu
- **70 ordinary lodging businesses** that are not on the tourist list (35 inns, 27 general hotels and a few others)
- **407 urban homestays for foreign tourists**, and **243 of them (60%) were licensed in 2026**

In short: **Gangnam's hotels cluster around COEX in Samseong-dong and along Teheran-ro in Yeoksam-dong, and hostels and homestays have multiplied since 2025.** This guide breaks the numbers down by neighbourhood (*dong*, the smallest administrative district), covers hotels that have changed names or closed, and lists first and last airport buses stop by stop.

![The Teheran-ro and Yeongdong-daero crossing in Samseong-dong at dusk, office towers lit up and streams of car lights between them](/images/gangnam-hotels-2026-09/01.webp)

## How these were counted

The count takes every business **with a Gangnam-gu address** listed as operating in four licensing datasets on Seoul Open Data Plaza. Hotels and lodging use the 24 September 2026 update, homestays the 27 September update and hanok stays the 23 September update. The method is the same as in the [Itaewon](/en/posts/itaewon-hotels-2026-09/) and [Myeongdong](/en/posts/myeongdong-hotels-2026-09/) accommodation guides.

| Dataset | Law | What it covers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tourist accommodation | Tourism Promotion Act (registration) | Tourist hotels, hostels, family hotels, small hotels |
| Lodging | Public Health Control Act (report) | Inns, general hotels, residence-style lodging, and tourist hotels a second time |
| Urban homestay for foreign tourists | Tourism Promotion Act (designation) | A resident renting rooms in their own home to foreign visitors |
| Hanok experience | Tourism Promotion Act (registration) | Stays in traditional Korean houses |

| Dong | Tourist accommodation | Lodging only | Homestays |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Yeoksam-dong | **31** | **42** | **134** |
| Nonhyeon-dong | 18 | 6 | 92 |
| Samseong-dong | 14 | 10 | 76 |
| Sinsa-dong (Garosu-gil, part of Apgujeong Rodeo) | 9 | 4 | 46 |
| Daechi-dong | 6 | 5 | 23 |
| Cheongdam-dong | 5 | 1 | 19 |
| Gaepo, Dogok, Irwon, Yulhyeon | 1 | 2 | 17 |
| **Gangnam-gu total** | **84** | **70** | **407** |

Three things to keep in mind.

- **Big hotels appear in both datasets.** The 56 "tourist hotels" in the lodging data overlap with the 84 tourist businesses, so the lodging column only counts places not on the tourist list. Many hostels registered in 2025 and 2026 also appear a second time as residence-style lodging, and were removed the same way
- **Room counts come from the lodging data.** The tourist dataset leaves the room field blank for several large hotels. Where the two disagree, this guide says so
- **Gangnam Station straddles two districts.** Hotels on the west side of Gangnam-daero are in Seocho-dong, Seocho-gu. They are not in these numbers and are listed under "Just outside" below. The [Gangnam guide](/en/posts/gangnam-guide-2026-09/) explains where the boundary runs

Gangnam-gu has only **one** hanok stay (Suseo-dong, registered January 2026), so it is left out of the table. Jamsil in Songpa-gu and Seongsu-dong are not covered here; Seongsu has its own [accommodation guide](/en/posts/seongsu-hotels-2026-09/).

## Homestays are for foreign visitors only

Article 2 of the Enforcement Decree of the Tourism Promotion Act defines an urban homestay for foreign tourists as a business in which **a city resident** offers lodging **in the home they live in** **to foreign tourists**.

1. **Korean nationals generally cannot stay in them.** If you are travelling with Korean friends or family, they will need one of the 84 tourist businesses or 70 lodging businesses above
2. **The host must live there.** Renting out an empty flat or officetel as a whole is not legal under this licence

In an apartment or multi-unit building, **the neighbours are real residents**. Ask in advance about quiet hours and rubbish rules (Seoul sorts waste strictly by type). There is no 24-hour front desk, so if you arrive late, get the check-in instructions before you fly.

## Getting to the airport

No Airport Railroad line runs through Gangnam-gu. The main airport bus is K Limousine [[bus:6703]] (Gangnam/COEX), which stops all over the district. CALT's [[bus:6103]] starts at COEX, and the late-night [[bus:airport:N6703]] stops at Samseong Station. All three cost ₩18,000 (about $13.50) for adults and ₩12,000 for children at the time of writing.

### Routes 6103 and N6703

- [[bus:6103]], run by Korea City Air Terminal (CALT): towards the airport it leaves from the **2nd-floor limousine platform at COEX City Airport, between 04:10 and 21:00**. From the airport it departs Terminal 1 from 06:00 to 23:30 and Terminal 2 from 05:40 to 23:10, and drops off at COEX City Airport and at Samseong Station (in front of the Grand InterContinental). The operator gives about 65 minutes to Terminal 1 and about 85 minutes to Terminal 2
- [[bus:airport:N6703]], the late-night route: towards the airport it calls at Samseong Station (Grand InterContinental Parnas) six times a night, **from 22:45 to 03:40**. From the airport it leaves Terminal 2 from 23:20 to 04:30 and Terminal 1 from 23:40 to 04:50; in Gangnam it drops off at **COEX East Gate**

**There is no longer any check-in or immigration at the COEX City Airport Terminal.** According to Travel Times (25 April 2023), the service was suspended in April 2020 because of COVID-19 and ended for good on 4 January 2023. Today the building is simply where the 6103 departs.

### Route 6703 from the airport

From Incheon Airport the 6703 leaves **Terminal 2 from 05:55 to 22:35 and Terminal 1 from 06:15 to 22:55**, 21 times a day. After drop-off-only stops at Isu Chemical and Banpo (Seorae Village entrance) it calls at the stops above, in the same order from Sinnonhyeon Station. The operator does not publish arrival times for each stop.

## Which to choose

- **A COEX trade show or event**: The Westin Seoul Parnas (602 rooms) and Grand InterContinental (546) in Samseong-dong connect to COEX. All three airport buses (6703, 6103, N6703) stop around Samseong Station
- **Business on Teheran-ro**: Yeoksam-dong, from Josun Palace (254 rooms) to twelve small tourist hotels, plus 42 general hotels and inns in the back streets
- **Shopping on Garosu-gil and around Apgujeong Rodeo**: Andaz, Four Points, Anteroom and Sunshine on Dosan-daero in Sinsa-dong, or voco and Aiden in Nonhyeon-dong
- **Right at Gangnam Station**: Hilton Garden Inn (208) and Hotel Peyto (186) on the Seocho side, or hostels and The Jerry's Planet on Gangnam-daero 84-gil to 94-gil on the Yeoksam side
- **On a budget**: 23 of Gangnam's 30 hostels were registered in 2025 or 2026, most of them in Yeoksam-dong (11) and Nonhyeon-dong (8)
- **A homestay in a residential street**: Yeoksam-dong and Nonhyeon-dong together have 226. Many buildings hold several, so check photos and the exact address
- **An early flight**: the 6703 starts at 04:00 (Sinnonhyeon Station) and the 6103 at 04:10 from COEX. For a late-night departure, the N6703 leaves Samseong Station from 22:45

## What I got wrong

The earlier version of this guide (13 September 2026) contained mistakes and weakly sourced claims.

**One: it said Gangnam has "almost no hostels or guesthouses".** The licensing data shows **30 hostels (third in Seoul) and 407 homestays**.

**Two: it grouped Josun Palace with Samseong Station.** Josun Palace is at **231 Teheran-ro in Yeoksam-dong**, and its official directions point to Yeoksam Station (exit 8, a ten-minute walk).

**Three: it called a hotel "InterContinental Seoul COEX".** The hotel at 524 Bongeunsa-ro is now **The Westin Seoul Parnas**.

**Four: it placed the ibis Ambassador "near Sinnonhyeon, same area as the Novotel".** The registered name is **ibis Styles Ambassador Gangnam**, and the address is **431 Samseong-ro, Daechi-dong**.

**Five: it said chains such as Ramada cluster in Yeoksam and Seolleung.** Gangnam's Ramada Seoul (410 Bongeunsa-ro) **closed in December 2021**. Its name survives only on a bus stop.

**Six: the airport details were wrong.** The old version said the 6103 cost ₩17,000 and ran "to the COEX City Airport Terminal". The operator's fare is **₩18,000**, and the terminal's check-in and immigration **ended on 4 January 2023**. It also said to take the Airport Railroad to Seoul Station and change to Line 9, but Line 9 does not stop at Seoul Station. The Airport Railroad and Line 9 meet at **Gimpo Airport**.

**Seven: unsourced numbers are gone.** Price bands (₩350,000 and up, ₩150,000 to ₩250,000, ₩80,000 to ₩140,000), "35 to 50 m² rooms", "breakfast ₩30,000 to ₩50,000" and "taxi ₩70,000 to ₩90,000, 60 to 80 minutes" came from price-comparison averages or no stated source. Signiel Seoul in Songpa-gu has also been dropped as outside the area.

**Eight: unverified claims are gone.** "Gangnam has Seoul's biggest rooms", "Park Hyatt is said to have the best design in the area" and "the Sinbundang Line does not accept the Climate Card" could not be confirmed from official sources this time.

## Summary

- Gangnam-gu has **84 tourist accommodation businesses** (third in Seoul) and **8,646 tourist-hotel rooms** (second). Yeoksam-dong leads with 31, then Nonhyeon-dong 18 and Samseong-dong 14.
- The largest hotels are **The Westin Seoul Parnas (602 rooms) and Grand InterContinental Seoul Parnas (546)** in Samseong-dong. Park Hyatt (185) has a Daechi-dong address.
- Since 2020 Le Méridien, Ramada Seoul (Ramid) and Glad Live have closed, while Anteroom, Josun Palace and voco have opened.
- **243 of the 407 homestays were licensed in 2026.** They are **for foreign visitors only**, in a home where the host lives.
- Airport buses: **6703 (last departures 18:38 to 19:42), 6103 (from COEX, 04:10 to 21:00) and the late-night N6703 (Samseong Station, 22:45 to 03:40)**. Check-in at the COEX City Airport Terminal ended in 2023.
