Search for a room in Hongdae and hotels, hostels, guesthouses and Airbnbs all come back in one long list. Count only the four neighbourhoods around Hongik University Station (Seogyo-dong, Donggyo-dong, Hapjeong-dong and Sangsu-dong) in Seoul's official licensing data, and the picture gets much clearer.

- **12 tourist hotels.** Of the five with more than 200 rooms, four are on **Yanghwa-ro**, the main road between Hongik University and Hapjeong stations
- **17 hostels**, and **14 of them were registered in 2025 or 2026**
- **833 urban homestays for foreign tourists.** Seogyo-dong (356) ranks **second of all Seoul neighbourhoods**, and Donggyo-dong (256) fourth

In short, Hongdae's real hotels line one main road, and almost everything else is **a homestay or small hostel in a residential lane**. One more thing: the Marigold Hotel, a name you will still see recommended, is recorded as **closed since July 2022**.

## How I counted

I downloaded four licensing datasets from the Seoul Open Data Plaza and kept businesses whose legal address is **Seogyo-dong, Donggyo-dong, Hapjeong-dong or Sangsu-dong in Mapo-gu**. The tourist accommodation and lodging data are the 24 September 2026 update; the homestay data is from 27 September. Yeonnam-dong, north of Exit 3, is counted separately in [Where to stay in Yeonnam-dong and Mangwon](/en/posts/yeonnam-hotels-2026-09/).

| Neighbourhood | Tourist accommodation (Tourism Promotion Act) | Registered rooms | Lodging only | Homestays (rooms) | Hanok stay |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Seogyo-dong | 13 (7 hotels, 6 hostels) | 1,045 | 2 inns | **356 (716)** | 1 |
| Donggyo-dong | 14 (3 hotels, 11 hostels) | 1,016 | 0 | **256 (512)** | 0 |
| Hapjeong-dong | 2 (2 hotels) | 108 | 6 (3 hotels, 3 inns) | 155 (242) | 0 |
| Sangsu-dong | 1 (family hotel) | 30 | 0 | 66 (83) | 0 |
| **Total** | **30** | **2,199** | **8** | **833 (1,553)** | 1 |

Most tourist hotels and hostels also appear in the general lodging dataset, so "lodging only" counts just the **inns and ordinary hotels that are not registered as tourist accommodation**.

### Closed: the Marigold Hotel

The 186-room **Marigold Hotel at 112 Yanghwa-ro is recorded as closed on 6 July 2022** in the tourist accommodation data, and as closed in the lodging data too. Some booking sites still show the name, so **confirm directly that it is operating** before you book anything under it. Seogyo-dong also lost Hotel Yaja (42 rooms, November 2020) and the NEO SEOUL hostel (18 rooms, February 2022).

## 17 hostels, 14 of them new

Only three of the 17 hostels predate 2025: **Pillowsopher (2014, 217 Donggyo-ro), Nabi Hostel (2016, 177-1 Yanghwa-ro) and Cleo (2018, 27 Yeonhui-ro)**. The other 14 were registered in 2025 or 2026, including **three on a single day, 2 September 2026** (Grida Hongdae, Sunny Ville Hostel and Valueplace Hongdae Hapjeong).

- **Several share a building.** 19 World Cup buk-ro 2-gil in Donggyo-dong holds Stay the Eeroom (floors 2-5, 7 rooms), Stay Here Again (6 rooms) and Look N Stay (8th floor, 6 rooms). At 118 Yanghwa-ro in Seogyo-dong, Hongdae Ongi (4th floor, 14 rooms) sits under Slow-on Hostel (5th floor, 15 rooms)
- **Sizes run from 2 to 27 rooms**, and 11 of the 17 have ten rooms or fewer

Hostels are tourist accommodation, so unlike homestays they **can take Korean guests as well as foreigners**. Some "hostels" are mostly private rooms, so check the room types on the booking site.

**Sangsu-dong** has one piece of tourist accommodation: **MyMe, a family hotel (97 Dongmak-ro, 30 rooms)** registered on 18 June 2026. A "family hotel" is a category of tourist accommodation under Korea's Tourism Promotion Act. Seogyo-dong's two inns are Nido Nado (29 Hongik-ro, licensed 1983) and Donggyeong Motel (34 Hongik-ro 5an-gil, licensed 1969).

## 833 homestays

Under the Tourism Promotion Act's enforcement decree, an urban homestay for foreign tourists is **a home where the resident offers rooms to foreign visitors**. Two consequences: **Korean nationals generally cannot book them**, and the host must actually live there. The rules, and Airbnb's licence-number requirement, are covered in more detail in [the Yeonnam-dong post](/en/posts/yeonnam-hotels-2026-09/).

Of Seoul's 7,365 operating homestays, **Seogyo-dong ranks 2nd (356), Donggyo-dong 4th (256) and Hapjeong-dong 8th (155)** by neighbourhood. Split by the year they were licensed, each neighbourhood looks different.

| Year licensed | Seogyo | Donggyo | Hapjeong | Sangsu |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2012-2020 | 60 | 43 | 11 | 2 |
| 2021-2023 | 72 | 74 | 19 | 6 |
| 2024 | 79 | 55 | 27 | 26 |
| 2025 | 78 | 45 | 36 | 12 |
| **2026 (to September)** | 67 | 39 | **62** | 20 |

- **62 of Hapjeong-dong's 155 homestays (40%) were licensed this year**, the fastest growth of the four
- **Seogyo-dong also has the most closures**: 202 closed or cancelled records, 41 of them in 2020
- Single-room homestays number **152 in Seogyo, 127 in Donggyo, 92 in Hapjeong and 50 in Sangsu**. Sangsu-dong's median declared floor area is about 37 m², the smallest of the four
- Multi-household houses (*dagagu*) are the most common building type in Seogyo, Donggyo and Hapjeong; in Sangsu it is small multi-unit villas (29)

The streets with the most homestays are **Wausan-ro 29-gil (49) and Wausan-ro 33-gil (26)** in Seogyo, **Sinchon-ro 2an-gil (32) and Wausan-ro 38-gil (22)** in Donggyo, **World Cup-ro 5-gil (18) and 7-gil (16)** in Hapjeong, and **Wausan-ro 3an-gil (26)** in Sangsu. If your address is on one of these, expect a residential lane, often without a lift or a 24-hour desk, so get check-in instructions in advance if you land late.

## Where the crowds are: the Red Road and the club street

The stretch Mapo-gu actively manages for crowds is the **Red Road**, about 2 km from the Gyeongui Line Forest Park to the Dangin-ri power plant, divided into sections R1 to R6 (Seoul Shinmun, 6 December 2023).

- **Since 16 May 2025, R1-R6 has been a "no e-scooter street" every day from 12:00 to 23:00.** Seoul says those hours were chosen from **big-data analysis of when the crowds peak**
- Seoul's real-time city data recorded a **momentary peak of about 134,000 people in the Hongdae tourist zone on 25 December 2024**, and Hongik University Station on Line 2 handles more than 167,000 passengers a day (Herald Business, 22 October 2025)
- For **Halloween 2025**, Mapo-gu ran safety management from 24 October to 2 November, with a concentrated operation from **20:00 on 31 October to 05:00 the next morning**, when the crowd peaked at about 140,000. Staff were concentrated on **R1-R6 and the club street** (Korea Economic Daily, 3 November 2025)

**31 October 2026 falls on a Saturday.** If you are staying in Hongdae that weekend, expect crowds and possible controls around the Red Road and club street late into the night, and avoid wheeling luggage through them. Mapo-gu's 2026 Halloween plan had not been published when this was written.

If you want a quiet night, **avoid rooms right on the Red Road or club street** and check the distance on a map. There are no public block-by-block noise complaint figures, so recent reviews are the only real guide to how loud a particular room is.

## Getting to the airport

Hongdae's biggest practical advantage is that **both the airport train and the airport bus run here with no transfer**.

### AREX airport railroad

The AREX [[line:arex]] all-stop train calls at Hongik University. **The Express train runs nonstop between Seoul Station and the airport, so it does not stop here.**

| Route | Fare (transit card) | First | Last |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hongik Univ. → Incheon Airport T1 | ₩4,650 (about $3.50) | 05:28 | 23:48 |
| Hongik Univ. → Incheon Airport T2 | ₩5,250 | 05:28 | **23:48** |

Weekday and weekend times are the same. Trains after 23:48 terminate at Geomam, so don't board them by mistake.

**Coming from the airport**, the last all-stop trains to Seoul Station leave **T1 at 23:38 and T2 at 23:32**. The later 23:56 (T1) and 23:50 (T2) trains terminate at Digital Media City and do not reach Hongik University. For **Sangsu or Hapjeong**, change at Hongik University to [[line:2]] for one stop to Hapjeong, then [[line:6]] one more stop to Sangsu.

![Exit 9 of Hongik University Station, its canopy marked for Line 2, the Gyeongui-Jungang Line and AREX, with a yellow Gyeonggi bus on Yanghwa-ro beside it](/images/hongdae-hotels-2026-09/01.webp)

### Airport bus

**Towards the airport**, airport limousine [[bus:6002]] stops three times along Yanghwa-ro. The adult fare is ₩17,000 (about $13).

| Stop (number) | Where | First | Last |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hongik Univ. (14016) | median bus lane on Yanghwa-ro, by the Mercure and L7 | 04:26 | **21:11** |
| Seogyo-dong RYSE Hotel (14014) | median bus lane | 04:28 | 21:14 |
| Hapjeong Station (14895) | Hapjeong Station Exit 1 | 04:31 | **21:17** |

The Hongik Univ. stop sees 77 departures a day, roughly every 13 minutes in the daytime. Seats are first come, first served with no online booking; pay by transit card or cash.

The **late-night [[bus:airport:N6002]]** stops only at Hongik Univ. (14016) in this area, leaving for the airport at **23:00, 23:50, 02:20, 02:50, 03:20 and 04:00**, which makes it useful for flights earlier than the first train (05:28).

**From the airport**, the 6002 leaves T1 on weekdays from 05:34 to **23:29** and T2 from 05:14 to 23:09. In the city it drops off at **Hapjeong Station (Exit 7, median lane), then Seogyo-dong (median lane), then Hongik Univ. (kerbside, in front of Olive Young)**, so Hapjeong guests get off first.

## Which to choose

- **A big hotel right by the airport bus**: the L7 or Mercure (Hongik Univ. stop) or RYSE (Seogyo-dong stop) on Yanghwa-ro
- **Right on top of the airport train**: Holiday Inn Express at 188 Yanghwa-ro
- **A tight budget, travelling solo**: the Donggyo and Seogyo hostels. With 14 of 17 registered since 2025, many will have few reviews yet
- **A foreign visitor who wants a residential lane**: a Seogyo or Donggyo homestay (not open to Korean nationals)
- **A quiet night**: a few blocks away from the Red Road and club street; check on a map
- **An early-morning flight**: near Hongik University Station, where you can use both the N6002 and the first train
- **The weekend around 31 October**: somewhere outside the Red Road crowd zone

## Summary

- Seogyo, Donggyo, Hapjeong and Sangsu-dong have **30 tourist accommodation businesses with 2,199 rooms**, 12 of them tourist hotels.
- The five hotels over 200 rooms are **L7 (340), Creatortown Seogyo (296), Holiday Inn Express (294), RYSE (274) and Mercure (270)**.
- **The Marigold Hotel is recorded as closed since July 2022.**
- **14 of the 17 hostels were registered in 2025 or 2026.**
- There are **833 homestays**; Seogyo-dong ranks second in Seoul and Hapjeong-dong added **62 this year alone**.
- The Red Road (R1-R6) is a **no e-scooter street from 12:00 to 23:00 daily**; Halloween 2025 peaked at about 140,000 people.
- AREX **last train to the airport 23:48**, airport bus **6002 last departure 21:11** at Hongik Univ., plus the late-night **N6002**.
