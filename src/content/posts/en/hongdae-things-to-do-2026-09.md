People say Hongdae has no sights, and they are half right: no palaces, no big museums. What it has instead is **a set of rules for using the street**. Where buskers may play and until when, which hours e-scooters are banned, which day each shrine closes. Know those and a day in Hongdae gets a lot easier.

Our [Hongdae overview](/en/posts/hongdae-guide-2026-09/) covers how the neighbourhood grew; this post sticks to **what to see and do**. The Yeonnam-dong stretch of the Gyeongui Line Forest Park ("Yeontral Park"), Mangwon Market and Mangwon Hangang Park are in [things to do in Yeonnam-dong and Mangwon-dong](/en/posts/yeonnam-things-to-do-2026-09/), so they are not repeated here. All hours and prices are **as of late September 2026**.

## The Red Road: 2km in seven sections

![A Hongdae side street at night, people walking along a road painted with a red stripe, a pink neon PINK'S sign glowing on a rooftop ahead and a brightly lit souvenir shop at street level](/images/hongdae-things-to-do-2026-09/13.webp)

The red line painted on Hongdae's streets is the **Red Road**, a themed tourist street Mapo District created in 2023. According to the Seoul city media hub it runs **about 2km from the Gyeongui Line Forest Park, past Hongik University, to the Danginri power plant**, and takes **about 25 minutes on foot** from start (R1) to end (R7). Think of it as one line joining Hongik Univ. Station on [[line:2]] and Sangsu Station on [[line:6]].

The surrounding 1.13km² of Seogyo-dong, Donggyo-dong, Hapjeong-dong and Sangsu-dong was designated the **Hongdae Culture and Arts Special Tourist Zone in December 2021**.

Each section has its own role. Here is how Mapo described them when the Red Road launched.

| Section | Role | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| R1-R2 | Busking and performance | R1 is by Hongik Univ. Station Exit 7, with a 300m² pavement artwork, "Pixel Travel", by artist Chu Mi-rim |
| R2 | The Walking Street | Where Mapo's official busking zone is |
| R3-R4 | Shopping street | At launch R4 was pitched as a live-commerce space for merchants. Mapo has set up its Halloween command post at R4 |
| R5 | Road Gallery | Open-air exhibition space built in 2023 |
| R6 | Festival plaza | Where an air dome hosted the New Year countdown |
| R7 | Danginri junction to the power plant | Walkway leading toward the Han River |

### The R5 Road Gallery

An open-air gallery you simply walk past. According to Munhwa Ilbo, this spring it showed 72 works by 24 artists with the Mapo Art Association until 30 April, and the district planned to rotate works with a monthly theme. We could not find what is on now.

### No e-scooters from noon to 11pm

**Red Road sections R1 to R6 became Korea's first "no e-scooter street"** on 16 May 2025.

- Ban runs **12:00-23:00** and covers personal mobility devices such as e-scooters
- Fine on an ordinary road: **₩30,000 (about $22) and 15 penalty points**
- If you ride a shared scooter into Hongdae, drop it off outside the Red Road.

## Busking on the Walking Street: a new system from September

![A pedestrian street in Hongdae on an overcast day, young people with umbrellas walking on red-patterned paving between clothing shops and restaurants crowded with Korean signs](/images/hongdae-things-to-do-2026-09/16.webp)

The **Hongdae Walking Street** (Red Road R2) is a short walk from **Exits 8 and 9** of Hongik Univ. Station ([[line:2]] [[line:arex]] [[line:gyeongui]]) toward the university. Korea Tourism Organization (KTO) data says the city rebuilt about 500m of Eoulmadang-ro between October 2016 and July 2017 to create it, with a **busking zone** on one side and a plaza stage, travel stage and meeting square on the other. Tourist guides in red vests walk the street.

### How performers apply now

Money Today reported on 9 August 2026 that Mapo District would switch **from a registration system to an application system from September 2026**.

| Period | System |
| --- | --- |
| 2010s | Fines over noise complaints |
| 2018 | Application system introduced |
| 2024 | Only pre-registered performers could apply (285 solo acts and 382 groups registered at the time of the report) |
| September 2026 | Apply without pre-registration. Weekdays first come, first served; weekends and holidays by lottery |

Under the new system there is no audition video or content screening, and the report says **foreign performers and primary-school teams are no longer excluded**. If you have always wanted to busk in Hongdae, you can now apply through the "outdoor stage application" page on Mapo's culture and tourism site.

### Rules worth knowing as an audience

Mapo's application page lists rules for performers, but they explain what you see on the street.

- **No performances outside booked spots and none after 22:00.** The page says this is because the area is both residential and packed with shops.
- Each slot is **1 hour 50 minutes**, wrapping up 10 minutes early for the next act.
- Performers must keep crowds from **blocking shop entrances or the walkway**, so leave doorways clear when you gather round.
- Excessive sound, lighting and filming rigs that bother neighbours are restricted.

In short, **official busking on the Walking Street ends at 10pm**. The Gyeongui Line Forest Park in Yeonnam-dong has its own permitted busking slots that end at 7pm; see [things to do in Yeonnam-dong and Mangwon-dong](/en/posts/yeonnam-things-to-do-2026-09/).

## Live clubs: one ticket, several venues

Hongdae's reputation was built in basement venues. The easiest way in is **Live Club Day**.

According to Newsis (February 2025):

- The original **Club Day began in 2001**, included dance clubs, and **stopped in January 2011**.
- It returned in **2015** as Live Club Day, focused on live music.
- **One ticket lets you move between several venues** and see many acts.
- For the 71st edition on 28 February 2025 (its 10th anniversary) a ticket was **₩35,000 (about $26)**, and blind tickets sold out first.

The most recent edition we confirmed was on **27 March 2026**, a collaboration for the CJ Cultural Foundation's 20th anniversary. NewsPim reports 20 acts across **six venues**: CJ Azit, Club Evans, Veloju, Musinsa Garage, KT&G Sangsangmadang and Prism Hall. Both 28 February 2025 and 27 March 2026 were the last Friday of the month. **We could not confirm the October date or line-up**, so check the official social accounts.

### Rolling Hall: a 30-year-old stage

**Rolling Hall** (35 Eoulmadang-ro) is one of Hongdae's longest-running live venues.

- Started in **June 1995** as "Rolling Stones" in Sinchon
- KTO lists **about 200 seats, or up to 400 standing**
- In a Newsis interview the venue says it hosts around 100 planned shows a year; BTS's RM played a 200-person show here in December 2022.
- Hours and prices vary by show. 02-325-6071

## KT&G Sangsangmadang Hongdae: building tours

![The design shop at KT&G Sangsangmadang Hongdae, white shelves packed with postcards, stickers, stationery and character plush toys](/images/hongdae-things-to-do-2026-09/02.webp)

At the end of the Walking Street, **KT&G Sangsangmadang** (65 Eoulmadang-ro) opened in September 2007 as an arts complex. KTO lists a **cinema, live hall, design square (design shop), gallery, academy, photo darkroom and café** under one roof. It is a good place to spend a rainy afternoon.

- Open **11:00-21:00** per KTO's accessible-travel listing; closing days and prices depend on the program.
- Step-free entrance with automatic doors, an accessible lift and accessible toilets are listed.

A notice from April 2026 announced **building tours**.

| | Basic tour | Signature tour |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Content | Main spaces and the brand story | Basic tour plus docent talk tied to a current show or exhibition |
| Length | About 40-60 min | About 60-90 min |
| Price | **Free** | **₩10,000** per person plus a discounted show/exhibition ticket |

- Apply by **phone (02-2014-2003) or email**; they confirm after an internal review.
- **Age 14 and over**, up to 15 people. **Foreign groups must bring their own interpreter.**
- Paid tours must be paid three days ahead; cancel three or more days ahead for a full refund.

## Gyeongui Line Book Street: bookshops on the old tracks

![Old railway tracks and a platform kept under an overpass, a small mint-green station-style building and a Book Street station sign beside them, a footpath continuing behind](/images/hongdae-things-to-do-2026-09/05.webp)

Right outside **Exit 6** of Hongik Univ. Station is a book-themed street laid out on the old Gyeongui Line track bed. It runs the opposite way from the Yeonnam-dong park, toward Wawoo Bridge. KTO says it was **completed in October 2016**, inspired by the Joseon-era *chaekgado* (bookshelf painting) culture of King Jeongjo's time.

- **11:00-20:00, closed Mondays**, free to enter
- No parking
- 02-324-6200

When the Seoul city media hub covered it in 2021 there were **ten themed bookshops** in train-carriage-style booths. We could not confirm the current line-up.

![The Gyeongui Line Book Street at night seen from above, a lit footpath and stepped seating lined with low buildings, city towers glowing in the distance](/images/hongdae-things-to-do-2026-09/08.webp)

The old level crossing under Wawoo Bridge at the end of the street is "Ttaengttaeng-geori"; its story is in [our Yeonnam-dong and Mangwon-dong overview](/en/posts/yeonnam-guide-2026-09/).

## Alternative Space LOOP: free contemporary art

**Alternative Space LOOP**, on a lane near Hongik University, opened in **1999** as **Korea's first alternative art space**. It is a non-profit that gives exhibition space to young artists, and KTO notes it became a non-profit corporation in 2019.

- **Free**; closing days vary by exhibition
- 02-3141-1377

Mapo's free neighbourhood tour **"Hidden Pictures in Hongdae"** links places like this; see the walking tours section below.

## Sangsu: a park on top of a power plant

Follow the end of the Red Road (R7) down toward the river and you reach the old **Danginri power plant**.

### Mapo Saebit Culture Forest

According to Seoul& (January 2022), the plant, Korea's first coal-fired power station, was **replaced by an LNG combined-cycle plant built underground**, and a park was laid out on top.

- **78,867m²**, opened **9 April 2021**
- 56 Tojeong-ro, **open 24 hours, every day**
- About **890m** from Exit 4 of Sangsu Station on [[line:6]]
- Han River views from the paths, and remnants of the old Dangin rail line reused as seating
- A **3m-wide link path and a 21-person lift** now connect it to Mangwon Hangang Park. Before that, a retaining wall meant a 700m detour, the city says.

### Danginri Cultural Creation Power Plant: opening targeted for late 2027

Next to the park, the retired **Units 4 and 5** are being turned into an arts complex. NewsPim (1 June 2026) reports **25,532m² of floor space over two basement and six above-ground floors**; work began in May 2023 with a 2025 opening in mind, but **the target is now the second half of 2027**. **You cannot go inside yet.**

## Hapjeong: two riverside shrines that close on different days

Walk from Exit 7 of Hapjeong Station ([[line:2]] [[line:6]]) toward the river and two sacred sites sit side by side on a bluff. **They close on different days**, so plan around that.

| | Jeoldusan Martyrs' Shrine | Yanghwajin Foreign Missionary Cemetery |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What | Catholic martyrs' shrine | Protestant missionaries' cemetery |
| Hours | Museum 09:30-17:00 | Mon-Sat 10:00-17:00 |
| Closed | Museum **Mondays** | **Sundays** |
| Price | Museum by voluntary donation | Free |

### Jeoldusan Martyrs' Shrine

![A white statue of the Virgin Mary in a rock grotto at Jeoldusan Martyrs' Shrine, rows of votive candles inside a green glass booth in front, pine trees and shrubs around](/images/hongdae-things-to-do-2026-09/11.webp)

The bluff was once called **Jamdubong**, "silkworm head peak", for its shape. KTO explains that during the 1866 Byeongin persecution many Catholics were executed here, after which it became known as **Jeoldusan**, "beheading mountain". A memorial hall opened in October 1967 for the centenary.

Official visiting hours:

| Place | Hours |
| --- | --- |
| Byeongin Martyrdom Centenary Church | 07:00-17:00 (Mondays until 13:00) |
| Relic chapel of the martyr saints | 07:00-17:00, **no entry during Mass** |
| Korean Catholic Martyrs' Museum | Tue-Sun 09:30-17:00. Closed Mondays, 1 January, Lunar New Year and Chuseok holidays |
| Shrine car park | 07:00-18:00 |

- Museum admission is a **voluntary donation**.
- Mass is at **10:00 on Mondays** and **10:00 and 15:00 Tuesday to Sunday**.
- The basement special exhibition on Yanghwa Ferry and Jamdubong closed on 27 September; check the notices for the next one.
- No flash or tripods in the museum, and no bikes or scooters in the outdoor exhibition area.
- **About 450m, a 10-minute walk** from Hapjeong Station Exit 7. The shrine asks visitors to use public transport on weekends and holidays.

### Yanghwajin Foreign Missionary Cemetery

Right next to Jeoldusan. KTO says **417 missionaries from 15 countries** are buried here, naming Ernest Bethell, Homer Hulbert, Horace Underwood and Henry Appenzeller among the best known. The Seoul city media hub notes that John Heron was the first to be buried, on 28 July 1890. The Centennial Memorial Church manages it today.

- **Mon-Sat 10:00-17:00, closed Sundays**, free
- **Free guided visits**: book online **by 18:00 the day before**; **no same-day bookings**.
  - Times: Mon-Fri 10:00, 11:30, 14:00; Sat 10:00, 11:30, 13:30, 15:00
  - A missionary film, a cemetery walk and the Yanghwajin Hall exhibition, 30 minutes each
  - For a foreign-language guide, choose the English, Japanese or Chinese option in the region field; visitors without a Korean phone number can give an email address. A QR self-guide is also available.
  - No guided visits on 1 January, Lunar New Year, Chuseok or Christmas Day.

The rules are strict. **No food, litter or loudspeakers in the grave area**, and **photos only from the paths between graves**. Walk the path in single file.

Mapo was reported in late 2023 to be studying an expansion of the tourist zone to include these sites and the power-plant park; we could not confirm whether that has happened.

### Halloween week

Hongdae is among Seoul's busiest spots at Halloween and New Year. Munhwa Ilbo reports that the tourist zone hit **a peak of 88,000 people** on 31 December 2025, more than Myeongdong (58,000) at the same moment. For Halloween 2024 Mapo ran a **command post at R4** and AI crowd-density monitoring at nine points. **We could not find the 2026 Halloween safety plan yet.** Crowd safety is covered in the [Hongdae overview](/en/posts/hongdae-guide-2026-09/).

Hongdae's big music showcase, **Zandari Festa**, already took place on 4-6 September this year.

## A day by time of day

**Morning: Hapjeong**
From Hapjeong Station Exit 7 to Jeoldusan (museum closed Mondays), then next door to Yanghwajin (closed Sundays). Book the guided visit by 18:00 the day before.

**Around lunch: Sangsu**
Along the river to Mapo Saebit Culture Forest. Head up toward Sangsu Station and you are at the start of Red Road R7.

**Afternoon: up the Red Road**
Walk from R7 toward R1, past the R5 Road Gallery to Sangsangmadang (phone ahead for a building tour). On a rainy day you can stay inside here.

**Late afternoon: Gyeongui Line Book Street**
Outside Exit 6 of Hongik Univ. Station. It closes at 20:00 and on Mondays.

**Evening: the Walking Street**
Busking until 22:00, then move to a live venue such as Rolling Hall. If it is the last Friday of the month, check whether there is a Live Club Day.

**On 16-18 October**
Drop by the Wow Book street fair on Red Road R1 during the day.

## Summary

- The **Red Road** runs about 2km from Hongik Univ. Station to the Danginri power plant in seven sections; **e-scooters banned on R1-R6 from 12:00 to 23:00**.
- **Walking Street busking** switched to an application system without pre-registration in September; performances **end by 22:00** and only in booked spots.
- **Live Club Day** lets one ticket cover several venues; recent editions fell on the last Friday.
- **Sangsangmadang Hongdae** is open 11:00-21:00, with a free basic building tour by phone.
- The **Gyeongui Line Book Street** is open 11:00-20:00, **closed Mondays**.
- **Jeoldusan's museum closes Mondays**, **Yanghwajin closes Sundays**; book Yanghwajin's free guide by 18:00 the day before.
- The **Wow Book Festival** is 16-18 October on Red Road R1 and around Sanwoollim Theatre.
