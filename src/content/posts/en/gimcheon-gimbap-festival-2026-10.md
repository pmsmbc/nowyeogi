The Gimcheon Gimbap Festival started as a joke. Gimcheon (김천), a small city in Gyeongsangbuk-do, shares its name with the shorthand for Korea's best-known gimbap diner chain, and most Koreans think of the diner first. In 2024 the city leaned into that and built a whole festival around gimbap, the seaweed-wrapped rice rolls that every Korean associates with school picnics. It expected 20,000 people and got about 100,000. In 2025 roughly 150,000 came in two days. The third edition runs **Friday 23 to Sunday 25 October 2026**, and this guide pulls together the official notices and Korean press coverage on hours, transport, what is on the menu and how to avoid the queues. Times and fares are as published on 12 October 2026.

![White sphere and rabbit sculptures on the lawn of Jikji Culture Park, with pine woods and a small waterfall behind](/images/gimcheon-gimbap-festival-2026-10/05.webp)

## What is new in 2026

The festival grows from two days to **three**, and from two parks to three: Jikji Culture Park (직지문화공원), Samyeong Daesa Park (사명대사공원) and the newly added Eco Park (친환경생태공원), all side by side below Jikjisa Temple. The address is 118 Jikjisa-gil, Daehang-myeon, and admission is free.

- **Friday 23 October** 10:00–18:00
- **Saturday 24 and Sunday 25 October** 09:00–18:00

The gimbap count has gone from 8 kinds in year one to about 40 last year and **more than 60** this year. The city's breakdown:

- **19 local gimbap** made by Gimcheon businesses, schools and community groups, sold only during the festival
- **14 unusual gimbap** curated by the city's tourism ambassador
- **About 20 regional gimbap** from well-known shops around the country, each running its own stall
- **4 special gimbap** from this year's gimbap cooking contest. The gold-prize roll is due to go on sale at CU convenience stores nationwide afterwards

New this year are gimbap-shaped breads and handmade sandwiches, a retro "gimbap and cider" zone run with a soft-drink maker, and stalls selling Gimcheon's two signature crops, grapes and walnuts. The rice is a local variety, and the city says every stall uses it.

## What is where

Each park has a job. This layout comes from guides summarising the official map, and stalls can move on the day.

- **Jikji Culture Park, "Gimbap Square"**: the open-kitchen **gimbap factory** that went viral last year, the local gimbap, Gimcheon desserts, local produce and the **main stage**
- **Samyeong Daesa Park, "Gimbap Heaven"**: regional and unusual gimbap, the curated rolls, the cooking-contest zone, a gimbap art workshop and busking
- **Around the Peace Pagoda in Samyeong Daesa Park, "Event Yard"**: Gimcheon signature gimbap, the **mascot merchandise shop**, the gimbap-and-cider zone, hanbok try-ons and a fringe stage
- **Eco Park, "Gimbap Playground"**: snack-craft workshops, a gimbap bouncy castle, an arcade, a gimbap maze and a mini train. If you have children, this is your base

![A silver cube sculpture in the middle of the circular plaza at Jikji Culture Park, green hills behind](/images/gimcheon-gimbap-festival-2026-10/07.webp)

The mascot is **Kkodari** (꼬달이), named after the end piece of a gimbap roll. There are more than 29 merchandise items this year, 16 of them new. Last year the popular items sold out three hours after the shop opened, so merchandise hunters should go there first.

![A wooden pavilion, the five-storey Peace Pagoda and a water wheel at Samyeong Daesa Park](/images/gimcheon-gimbap-festival-2026-10/08.webp)

## Concerts at 4 pm every day

The main-stage concert is at **16:00** on all three days: Bobby Kim on Friday, Jadu on Saturday and the band Cheeze on Sunday. Jadu sang a gimbap song here last year too. A bicycle-themed brass band also plays daily at 13:30 and 15:30. No seat-booking system has been announced, so treat it as first come, first served. Times can shift on the day.

![A rainbow-coloured arch and a pavilion above an artificial waterfall, seen from a wooden bridge in Jikji Culture Park](/images/gimcheon-gimbap-festival-2026-10/06.webp)

## Skipping the queue: QR ordering

The biggest operational change is **QR ordering and payment through Kakao Pay**. Scan the code at a stall, order and pay on your phone, and you do not stand in the line. Kakao Pay is a Korean mobile wallet, so overseas visitors without a Korean bank account may find it hard to set up. In that case bring cash and a card and plan for the queue. A festival-only Kakao Map, opened by QR codes on site, shows zone routes, vendors and stage times. After complaints last year the city added three more women's toilet blocks and introduced a luggage storage service.

## How to beat the sell-outs

Organisers prepared more than 100,000 servings in 2025 and still had **stalls sell out before noon on day one**, with queues of several hundred metres forming before the gates opened. The fix is simple.

- **Arrive for opening**: 09:00 on the weekend, 10:00 on Friday
- **Friday is the quiet day.** If you can, go then
- Go to the gimbap stalls you want first and do the workshops and concert afterwards
- Late October evenings at the foot of the mountain are cold. Bring a layer, and a picnic mat makes the lawns much more comfortable

## Getting there

**Take the KTX to Gimcheon (Gumi) Station**, not Gimcheon Station. The latter is the older station for slower Mugunghwa and ITX trains, and the two are a long way apart. Shuttles leave from both.

**Festival shuttle buses** number about 60 coaches. All routes end at the Jikji No. 2 public car park next to the grounds.

- **Gimcheon Sports Town** 40 buses. This is the park-and-ride lot for drivers
- **Gimcheon (Gumi) KTX Station** 14 buses
- **Gimcheon Station** 6 buses
- **Every 20 minutes**. Friday 10:00–19:00, Saturday and Sunday 08:00–19:00, with short pauses at lunch and mid-afternoon

The **Picnic Taxi** (소풍택시) is a new on-demand service. There is no fixed route or timetable: you call through a dedicated app or a call centre, name your start and end point, and the car number arrives by text or KakaoTalk. One-way fares are **₩8,000 (about $6)** from either Gimcheon station to Jikjisa and **₩5,000** from the Sports Town, for up to four passengers. For a group of three or four it can be quicker than the shuttle queue.

**Direct Kakao T coaches** run from five cities. Book in the Kakao T app under "Shuttle" by choosing a date and departure point. Fares and times vary by route and are shown in the app. Passengers get two mascot goods and one gimbap roll.

- **Seoul**: Jamsil Station, Sadang Station, Seoul Station
- **Daejeon**: City Hall Station
- **Daegu**: Banwoldang Station
- **Gwangju**: Central Bus Terminal
- **Busan**: Seomyeon Station

Details of the outer car parks had not been published at the time of writing. The city is asking visitors to leave the car at home and use the shuttles or the taxi.

## Walk up to Jikjisa Temple

Directly above the festival grounds is **Jikjisa** (직지사), a major temple on Hwangaksan said to date from 418, and the temple where the warrior-monk Samyeong Daesa served as abbot, which is where the park below gets its name. Korea abolished temple heritage-site admission fees in 2023, so entry is free. The festival already involves a fair amount of walking between three parks, but a slow climb from the first gate to the main hall after lunch takes about an hour and gets you away from the crowd. Shops and cafés at the temple gate are offering ₩1,000 off per visitor during the festival.

The photos above and below were taken in spring. In late October the cherry trees will have turned and the maples are just starting.

## Practical notes

- **Dates and hours**: Fri 23 Oct 2026 10:00–18:00, Sat 24 and Sun 25 Oct 09:00–18:00. Free admission
- **Where**: 118 Jikjisa-gil, Daehang-myeon, Gimcheon. Three adjoining parks, all on foot
- **Paying**: gimbap, snacks, desserts, merchandise and some workshops are paid on site. QR ordering via Kakao Pay. Individual gimbap prices had not been published
- **Shuttles**: Sports Town, Gimcheon (Gumi) KTX Station and Gimcheon Station to the Jikji No. 2 car park, every 20 minutes
- **Picnic Taxi**: ₩5,000–8,000 one way, up to four people
- **From other cities**: Kakao T app, "Shuttle" menu, from Seoul, Daejeon, Daegu, Gwangju and Busan
- **Facilities**: new luggage storage, festival Kakao Map, extra women's toilets
- **Language**: signage and apps are in Korean. Kakao T has an English interface, but the shuttle menu and festival site are Korean-only, so a translation app helps
- **Enquiries**: Gimcheon City +82-54-420-6714, gimbapcity.kr, Instagram @gc_gimbapcity

## Wrapping up

A festival that began as a pun was voted Korea's top regional festival by visitors in its second year, and in 2026 it was named Gyeongbuk's best festival and a candidate national culture-and-tourism festival. Crowds follow. Stick to three rules, Friday at opening time, shuttle or taxi instead of your own car, and QR ordering, and most of the queues reported last year disappear. Two rolls of gimbap and a walk up to the temple make a very good autumn day out from Daegu or Daejeon, and a feasible one from Seoul on the KTX.
