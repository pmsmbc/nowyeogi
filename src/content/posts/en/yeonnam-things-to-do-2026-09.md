Almost nothing in Yeonnam-dong and Mangwon-dong **needs a ticket.** You walk a park laid over an old railway, pass through a market, and sit by the Han River.

What these neighbourhoods do have is **rules and hours**. The park locals call "Yeontral Park" is a designated drinking-clean zone, and busking there is licensed for three slots a day. The warship museum on the river closes on Mondays, and Haneul Park shuts its gates at night. This post sets those out from official sources and adds **a route by time of day**.

Our [Hongdae things-to-do guide](/en/posts/hongdae-things-to-do-2026-09/) touches on the forest park, Mangwon Market and the river in a few lines; this one goes deeper into Yeonnam and Mangwon alone. For the neighbourhoods as a whole, see [the Yeonnam-dong and Mangwon-dong overview](/en/posts/yeonnam-guide-2026-09/).

## Gyeongui Line Forest Park, Yeonnam section: 1.2 km or 1.3 km?

The **Gyeongui Line Forest Park** (경의선숲길, Gyeonguiseon Supgil) is a **6.3 km linear park** running from Mapo-gu into Yongsan-gu. According to Seoul's parks site, it was built section by section from March 2012 and **completed in 2016**, on the ground freed when the old Yongsan Line railway was moved underground.

The **Yeonnam-dong section** is the stretch nicknamed "Yeontral Park", a pun on Central Park.

- **From the Yeonnam intersection to Hongik University Station**
- **About 1.2 km** according to Seoul Parks; Mapo-gu's tourism site says **1.3 km**
- Seoul Parks describes **rows of ginkgo trees and an 860-metre water channel**, with rest spots styled after rail tracks and small stations
- Nearest stations: Hongik Univ. [[line:2]] [[line:gyeongui]] [[line:arex]] and Gajwa [[line:gyeongui]]
- **There is no park car park.** Come by public transport.

### Busking in three slots a day

The park's licensed busking spot is the **roughly 200 m from exit 3 of Hongik Univ. Station to Yeonnam police box**.

| | Busking slot |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | 11:00-13:00 |
| 2 | 14:00-16:00 |
| 3 | 17:00-19:00 |

Performers book slots free, first come first served, through Seoul's public reservation service. So **the park's official performances end at 19:00**; music you hear later at night may not be a licensed act.

The same stretch, **exit 3 to the Yeonnam police box junction**, is also a no-filming zone. That rule is about permitted shoots such as TV and advertising, not holiday snaps.

### A "drinking-clean zone": drinking itself is not banned

The picture of people drinking convenience-store beer on the Yeonnam lawns is famous. But **the Gyeongui Line Forest Park is a designated drinking-clean zone**, one of 22 parks run directly by the Seoul city government, with enforcement since 1 April 2018.

- **What is prohibited: drinking and then causing serious noise or smells that give others offence**
- **Fine of up to ₩100,000 (about $75)**
- The city states plainly that **drinking in the park is not in itself prohibited**

In practice: remember that houses line both sides. If you need help in the crowded Yeonnam section, the park directs visitors to the **Yeonnam guard post in front of the Yeonnam police box**, which also keeps leaflets.

## Mangwon Hangang Park: 7.4 km of riverbank

![A slope in Mangwon Hangang Park on a clear day, stepped paths among pine trees and, beyond them, the wide Han River and a red arched bridge](/images/yeonnam-things-to-do-2026-09/16.webp)

The Seoul Future Hangang Headquarters, which runs the riverside parks, gives it as **7.4 km long and 422,347 m²**, from the north end of Wonhyo Bridge to the Hongjecheon Bridge in Sangam-dong. There are lawns, sports grounds, a **7.2 km cycle path**, two convenience stores and 11 toilets.

### Getting there: the underpasses

The riverside expressway, Gangbyeon-bukro, separates the neighbourhood from the park. You cross beneath it through underpasses called **nadeulmok**. The official directions sort them by where you start.

| Starting from | Use |
| --- | --- |
| Mangwon Station | Mangwon, Seongsan or Hapjeong underpass, or the Mangwon Green Road |
| Hapjeong Station exit 8 | **Mangwon Green Road** (cycle path) |
| Mangwon Post Office junction | Mangwon underpass |
| Mapo-gu Office Station exit 7 | **Hongjecheon stream path** |

By bus: from exit 1 of Mangwon Station [[line:6]] or exit 1 of Hapjeong Station [[line:2]] [[line:6]], take village bus [[bus:village:09]] Mapo 09 or [[bus:village:16]] Mapo 16 to the "Mangwon Hangang Park" stop. Village buses are small neighbourhood minibuses that take the same T-money card.

### Rules worth knowing

- **Take your rubbish home. Leaving it costs a ₩100,000 fine.**
- Shade tents only **March to November, in permitted zones** (Mangwon has two)
- **Parking outside designated spots: ₩50,000 fine.** Public transport is advised at weekends and on holidays.
- No smoking, no cooking, dogs on leads

### Bikes: use Ttareungyi

**Mangwon is not on the list of 12 Hangang park bicycle rental shops.** The nearest on the north bank is at Nanji, further downstream. At Mangwon, the practical choice is **Ttareungyi** (따릉이), Seoul's public bike share.

- **1-hour pass ₩1,000, 2-hour pass ₩2,000** (day passes)
- Going over the base time costs **₩200 per 5 minutes**
- A day pass lasts 24 hours, and **as long as each ride stays within the limit you can rent again as often as you like** (Seoul city media)
- Foreign visitors can choose the foreigner option in the app or on the site (English, Chinese or Japanese) and buy a pass with **a credit card issued abroad**, one pass per card (Ttareungyi FAQ)

### The Hangang Bus, and no swimming pool

Mangwon has a stop for the **Hangang Bus** river ferry. When full service resumed on 1 March 2026 it was split around Yeouido into an eastern route (Jamsil-Yeouido) and a western route (Magok-Yeouido); Mangwon is on the western one. The March notice lists **₩3,000 for adults** and no cash payments. Timetables have changed several times since, so **check the Hangang Bus site or Naver Map / Kakao Map on the day.**

There is **no swimming pool at Mangwon**, and the Hangang headquarters has announced that **the 2026 river pools and water play areas have closed** for the season (Yeouido's pool ended on 30 August).

## Seoul Battleship Park: three warships, closed on Mondays

![The grey frigate ROKS Seoul moored beside a riverside cycle path, missile launchers and a deck gun on board, buildings across the hazy river behind](/images/yeonnam-things-to-do-2026-09/13.webp)

Inside Mangwon Hangang Park, next to the children's baseball field, is what the city calls **Seoul's first warship theme park**. Three decommissioned vessels are kept as they were.

- **ROKS Seoul**: a 1,900-tonne frigate, retired after 30 years of service
- **Chamsuri**: a 150-tonne patrol boat
- **Dolgorae-class submarine**: 175 tonnes
- A **rooftop library** on the visitor centre

The Hangang headquarters notes that this stretch of riverbank was **a naval training ground in the Joseon era**, where kings sometimes came to watch.

| | Hours |
| --- | --- |
| Tue-Fri | 10:00-19:00 |
| Sat-Sun | 10:00-20:00 |
| Closed | **Mondays**, 1 January, Seollal and Chuseok days |

Last entry is **30 minutes before closing**. These are the March-October hours.

| Admission | Individual | Group (20+) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Adult | **₩3,000 (about $2.25)** | ₩2,000 |
| Youth (13-18) / military | ₩2,000 | ₩1,400 |
| Child (6-12) | ₩1,000 | ₩700 |

Free **docent tours run on weekends and public holidays** (13:30 and 16:00 in summer, 13:00 and 15:00 in winter). The official pages do not say which languages they are given in.

From exit 1 of Mangwon Station, take [[bus:village:09]] Mapo 09 to "Hangang Park Entrance" and walk seven minutes.

## Mangwonjeong: a pavilion with two names

The neighbourhood takes its name from this pavilion. Its registered name is **"Site of Mangwonjeong"**, a **Seoul Monument** designated on 18 June 1990.

The city's media hub tells the story like this.

- Built in **1424 by Prince Hyoryeong**, elder brother of King Sejong
- Sejong saw welcome rain fall here and called it **Huiujeong**, "the pavilion of happy rain"
- Later **Prince Wolsan**, elder brother of King Seongjong, repaired it and renamed it **Mangwonjeong**, "the pavilion for looking far"
- Lost after **the great flood of 1925** and later damage, then **rebuilt in 1989**

That is why it still carries **Mangwonjeong on the outside board and Huiujeong on the inside**.

Today the Gangbyeon-bukro expressway runs right in front of it. The media hub describes a low gate at the end of an alley and steep steps up to a two-storey pavilion. The address is **23 Donggyo-ro 8an-gil**, about 870 m from exit 8 of Hapjeong Station.

**I could not find official opening hours.** It is managed by Mapo-gu.

## Mangwon Market: just the walk

**Mangwon Market** (27 Poeun-ro 6-gil) is five minutes on foot from exit 2 of Mangwon Station. The Korea Tourism Organization lists opening hours as **varying by stall**.

In this post the market is simply **the way between the station and the river**; the city's media hub puts the park **within ten minutes' walk** of the market. The food is in [Yeonnam and Mangwon food](/en/posts/yeonnam-food-2026-09/).

## Free walking tours led by residents

Mapo-gu runs **Mapo Village Tours**, walks guided by local residents. There is one for each neighbourhood.

| | Yeonnam, One Fine Day | Mangwon in the Palm of My Hand |
| --- | --- | --- |
| About | Yeonnam lanes from Yeontral Park to local cafés and bookshops | Stories hidden in Mangwon's alleys |
| Length | About 2 hours | About 2 hours |
| Languages | Korean, English, Japanese, Chinese | Korean, English, Japanese |
| Cost | **Free** (drinks and activities at your own cost) | **Free** (same) |

- **Apply at least a week before the date you want**; no same-day bookings
- Runs only with **at least two participants**
- Travel insurance is not included

Check this year's schedule on the tour booking page of Mapo-gu's tourism site. The Korean title "내 손 안에 망원경" plays on the neighbourhood name: *mangwongyeong* means "telescope".

## Haneul Park: the Seoul Silver Grass Festival, 17-23 October

![Haneul Park in autumn, a dirt path lined with rope fencing running through silver grass in white bloom, three wind turbines in the distance](/images/yeonnam-things-to-do-2026-09/10.webp)

Two stops west of Mangwon on [[line:6]], by World Cup Stadium Station, is **World Cup Park**. It was built on **Nanjido, the island where Seoul dumped its rubbish from 1978 to 1993**, stabilised and opened as a park in May 2002. **Haneul Park** ("Sky Park") is the grassland on top of the second landfill mound.

**The 25th Seoul Silver Grass Festival is confirmed.**

- **Saturday 17 to Friday 23 October 2026, 10:00-21:00**
- Around Haneul Park, World Cup Park
- Enquiries: Western Park Leisure Center, 02-300-5574

**World Cup Park closes at night** to protect wildlife.

| | Opens | Closes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| April-September | 06:00 | 22:00 |
| October-March | 06:00 | **21:00** |

**It is a long way up.** The city suggests walking **about 40 minutes** from World Cup Stadium or Mapo-gu Office Station [[line:6]], or taking the **Maengkkongi electric shuttle** from the Nanjicheon Park car park: **₩2,000 one way, ₩3,000 return** to the Haneul Park summit. Traffic is very heavy during the festival, so use public transport.

## Days of the week: only Mondays matter

| | Mon | Tue-Fri | Sat-Sun |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Busking (licensed) | 11-13, 14-16, 17-19 | same | same |
| Mangwon Hangang Park | Open | Open | Open |
| Seoul Battleship Park | **Closed** | 10-19 | 10-20 |
| Haneul Park (October) | 06-21 | 06-21 | 06-21 |

## A route by time of day

**Morning: Yeonnam-dong**
From exit 3 of Hongik Univ. Station, follow the forest park towards the Yeonnam intersection, about 1.2 km of ginkgo trees and water channel. The first busking slot starts at 11:00.

**Midday to afternoon: Mangwon-dong**
Move to Mangwon Station [[line:6]], walk through Mangwon Market and on to the river. Mangwonjeong (Donggyo-ro 8an-gil) is a short detour on the way. In the afternoon, **Seoul Battleship Park** (not Mondays), or a Ttareungyi ride along the bank.

**Sunset: the river**
Sit on the riverside lawns, and take your rubbish home. The battleship park stays open to 20:00 at weekends and 19:00 on weekdays.

**If you are here 17-23 October**
Give the afternoon to Haneul Park. It is a 40-minute walk or the electric shuttle, and **the gates close at 21:00**.

## In short

- **The Yeonnam section is about 1.2 km**, from the Yeonnam intersection to Hongik Univ. Station. Licensed busking runs **11-13, 14-16 and 17-19**.
- It is a **drinking-clean zone**: causing a nuisance can mean **a fine of up to ₩100,000**, but drinking itself is not banned.
- **Mangwon Hangang Park is 7.4 km** long. Take rubbish home (₩100,000 fine); for bikes use **Ttareungyi**, as Mangwon has no river rental shop.
- **Seoul Battleship Park is closed on Mondays**, ₩3,000 for adults, open to 20:00 at weekends.
- **Mangwonjeong** has two name boards, Mangwonjeong outside and Huiujeong inside; rebuilt in 1989.
- **The Silver Grass Festival runs 17-23 October**, and Haneul Park **closes at 21:00** in October.
