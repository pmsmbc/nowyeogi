The first decision on a Jeju (제주) trip is not the dates or the flights — it is **which side of the island you sleep on**. Jeju is roughly three times the area of Seoul, with Hallasan mountain blocking the middle, so your base changes how much of each day you spend in a car.

Information here is as of September 2026. Fares and schedules change, so check official pages before you go.

## 1. Jeju City — convenience and late arrivals

The northern city closest to the airport, with the highest concentration of restaurants, convenience stores, pharmacies and supermarkets. It suits late-arriving or early-departing flights, and it is the easiest base if you are travelling by bus rather than car. Dongmun Market and the black-pork restaurant streets are within walking distance of most city hotels.

The trade-off is scenery. Most city hotels look out over buildings, not water, and the quiet that people come to Jeju for is elsewhere.

## 2. Aewol and Hallim — the west coast

Thirty to forty minutes west of Jeju City. Cafés, guesthouses and standalone rental houses line the coastal road, and this stretch is known for its sunsets.

![Sunset over the sea off Aewol with wind turbines lined up on the horizon](/images/jeju-hotels-2026-09/02.webp)

Many places here let you walk straight out to the water — the **Handam coastal path** in Gwakji-ri is the best-known example. Hyeopjae Beach is in the same area, which makes summer easy.

![The narrow Handam coastal path winding along the shore at dusk](/images/jeju-hotels-2026-09/01.webp)

Bus coverage is thin, so **treat a rental car as necessary here**. Fewer restaurants stay open late than in the city.

## 3. Jungmun — the resort cluster

A purpose-built tourist complex west of Seogwipo where the large hotels and resorts sit together. It fits trips built around a pool and hotel facilities, and trips with young children. The Daepo columnar joints and Cheonjeyeon Falls are close enough to fill half a day on foot.

It is also the one resort area with a direct airport bus: the [[bus:airport:600]] limousine. Official figures put the airport-to-Jungmun run at about 52 minutes, with a fare of ₩4,500 (about $3.40) as far as the Yeomiji stop.

## 4. Seogwipo city — value and bus travellers

The main town on the south coast, with its own markets and restaurants, and generally lower prices than Jeju City or Jungmun for comparable rooms. Residence-style hotels with a washing machine and microwave are common, which helps on stays of three nights or more.

![Kayakers on the Soesokkak inlet, enclosed by forest and rock](/images/jeju-hotels-2026-09/04.webp)

Hotels cluster near Seogwipo Bus Terminal, so it works without a car. Cheonjiyeon Falls is a walk from the town centre, and the Soesokkak inlet is a short drive east.

## 5. Seongsan and the east — quiet and sunrises

The eastern end, near Seongsan Ilchulbong and the ferry to Udo island. It draws fewer visitors, so it is quieter, and small guesthouses in the stone-walled villages tend to be cheaper. If you want to see the sunrise from Ilchulbong, staying here is the only comfortable way to do it.

The downside is distance: this is the furthest point from the airport, so it is a poor choice for your first or last night.

## Practical notes

- **From the airport**: the [[bus:airport:600]] limousine runs from 6:00am to 9:50pm at 18–27 minute intervals, plus late departures at 10:20pm and 10:50pm. Seogwipo (KAL Hotel) takes about 98 minutes and costs ₩5,500 (about $4.10). The [[bus:red:181]] and [[bus:red:182]] express buses reach Seogwipo Bus Terminal in roughly 50 minutes.
- **Rental car**: assume you need one for Aewol/Hallim and Seongsan. Jeju City, Seogwipo town and Jungmun are all manageable without.
- **How to split it**: for three nights or fewer, stay in one place and explore around it. For four or more, two nights west and two nights east saves a lot of driving.
- **High season**: July, August and Korean public holidays push prices up sharply and fill the popular places early. Off-season weekdays can be half the price for the same room.
- **Wind**: seafront rentals have the views and the winter wind both. Worth knowing before booking an exposed coastal house between December and February.

## Wrapping up

Choose a Jeju base by location rather than by the room itself. Jeju City or Seogwipo town for convenience, Aewol or Seongsan for the coast, Jungmun for a resort stay. Deciding whether you will rent a car first cuts the options in half.
