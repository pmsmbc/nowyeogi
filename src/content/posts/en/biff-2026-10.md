The **31st Busan International Film Festival (BIFF)** runs **6 to 15 October 2026**, so as of 8 October more than half of it is still ahead, and the free outdoor events continue through the 11th. This guide covers what is left, how a visitor buys tickets, where the venues are, and then spends the second half on the Busan Cinema Center and Centum City, because both are worth a visit in any month. Prices and hours are as of October 2026.

![The Busan Cinema Center's Big Roof and Double Cone lit with LEDs at night, with the empty Dureraum square below](/images/biff-2026-10/01.webp)

## This year in numbers, and what remains

The official selection is **247 films from 59 countries**, 93 of them world premieres, with 316 titles screened in total. The opener was a new film by Kim Jong-kwan, and this year BIFF joined Toronto on FIAPF's A-list of festivals. Dated events after 8 October:

- **7–11 October, Outdoor Stage Greetings** at the Cinema Center's Roof Theater, free
- **7–14 October, Open Cinema** at the Cinema Center's outdoor theater, 20:00 nightly
- **8–11 October, Community BIFF** around BIFF Square in Nampo-dong
- **10–13 October, Asian Contents & Film Market** at BEXCO (industry only)
- **15 October, closing ceremony** at the Cinema Center

## Free without a ticket: Outdoor Stage Greetings

Directors and cast of invited films step onto the outdoor stage for about **20 minutes** to introduce their work. It is **free with no registration**. Seating is limited, so arrive early for big names. Remaining sessions on the official schedule (subject to change on the day):

- **Friday 9 October**: 12:00 *Paradise Lost* with director Yeon Sang-ho and Kim Hyun-joo; 15:30 *Scarface Cat: Shura-do*; 16:00 *First World* with Yoon Dan-bi
- **Saturday 10 October**: 12:00 *The Remarried Empress* with Shin Min-a, Ju Ji-hoon, Lee Jong-suk and Lee Se-young; 12:30 *Nowhere to Look*
- **Sunday 11 October**: 12:00 Italian new-talent session; 12:30 *Inherit* with Banjong Pisanthanakun; 15:00 Flash Forward section directors

On the 8th itself, *Dora*, *Final Interview*, *Still Radiant* (Kim Hye-ja) and *In the Clear Moonlit Dusk* are on in the daytime.

![Festival crowds outside BIFF Hill at the Busan Cinema Center on a sunny afternoon](/images/biff-2026-10/13.webp)

## Open Cinema: the 4,000-seat outdoor theater

The festival's signature event. From **7 to 14 October at 20:00**, one film a night plays under the roof of the outdoor theater: Hirokazu Kore-eda's *Look Back*, Toledano and Nakache's *Just an Illusion*, the Korean comedy *The Sorry Generation*, the Thai horror *Inherit* and four more.

It is a **paid screening** and went on sale first, on 17 September. Right now your options are leftover seats on the official ticketing site or returned tickets at a box office. Seating is **unreserved**, with gates opening around 18:30, so turn up at the gate time for a good spot. After the afternoon stage greetings, everyone is cleared out and 20:00 ticket holders are re-admitted.

Busan residents have a separate route: the festival is giving away **1,000 free Open Cinema tickets to residents**, two per person, from 16:00 daily at the information desk by the Cinema Center's outdoor crossroads, with an ID showing a Busan address.

Food rules apply outdoors. Covered drinks, sandwiches, fried chicken and gimbap are fine; alcohol (including non-alcoholic beer), soupy food such as ramyeon and strong-smelling dishes are not. October nights by the sea are cool, so bring a layer.

## How to buy tickets

**Online sales are only through the official site**, ticket.biff.kr, open 24 hours until 15 October. You cannot buy BIFF screenings through the CGV or Lotte Cinema apps even when the film plays there. Last year's accounts do not carry over, so **register afresh** with an email address, and the limit is **two tickets per screening code**. Payment online is by credit or debit card. The English site (biff.kr/eng) runs the same process with an email sign-up, so overseas visitors book the same way.

Prices: **₩10,000 (about $7.50) for a general screening**, ₩30,000 for the opening or closing ceremony, ₩15,000 for Actors' House and Master Class talks, ₩20,000 for Midnight Passion. A ₩3,000 discount applies to over-65s and people with disabilities.

During the festival, **box offices** sell leftover and cancelled seats. There is no fixed release time for returns; refreshing the booking page is the only method.

- Cinema Center **outdoor box office**: 6–15 October, 08:30–20:30
- Cinema Center, Cine Mountain 6F: 7–15 October, from 08:30 until 15 minutes after the last screening starts
- CGV Centum City 7F, Lotte Cinema Centum City 8F, KOFIC 2F, Community Media Center 1F, Sohyang Theatre: hours follow each venue's schedule
- Megabox Busan Theater 4F (Nampo-dong): 8–11 October, 09:30–20:00, **Community BIFF only**

Cancellations are accepted up to **60 minutes before the screening**: free six or more days ahead, ₩1,000 at three to five days, ₩3,000 inside two days (₩3,000 and ₩9,000 for the ceremonies). Doors open 15 minutes before the start and close 15 minutes after. You need the mobile ticket or a paper ticket; **screenshots are refused**. Children under four are not admitted.

## Where the venues are

All of this year's official venues sit inside Centum City, within walking distance of Centum City Station on [[line:busan2]].

- **Busan Cinema Center**, 120 Suyeonggangbyeon-daero: outdoor theater, Haneulyeon Theater, Cinema 1, Cinema 2, Cinematheque
- **CGV Centum City**, 35 Centumnam-daero, 7th floor of Shinsegae
- **Lotte Cinema Centum City**, 59 Centumnam-daero, 8th floor of Lotte Department Store
- **KOFIC screening room**, 130 Suyeonggangbyeon-daero, 2F
- **Sohyang Theatre Woori Bank Hall**, 55 Centumjungang-ro
- **Busan Community Media Center**, 42 Centumjungang-ro
- Shinsegae 9F Culture Hall for Actors' House and Master Class

Only bottled water is allowed into the Haneulyeon Theater, KOFIC, Sohyang Theatre and the Media Center. For screenings inside the department stores before the 10:30 opening, follow the dedicated entrance printed on the ticket guidance.

## Nampo-dong and Community BIFF

![The Busan International Film Festival arch at the entrance to BIFF Square in Nampo-dong, with street-stall umbrellas](/images/biff-2026-10/17.webp)

The festival began in 1996 in the old cinema district of Nampo-dong, now called **BIFF Square**, where handprints of visiting filmmakers are set into the pavement. This year's **Community BIFF**, the ninth edition, runs there **8–11 October** with 69 films over 42 screenings.

The BIFF Square outdoor stage hosts **free open-air screenings** and stage greetings every evening; this year's outdoor titles are the Korean films *Jungle Story* (1996), *Lump Sugar* (2006) and *A Melody to Remember* (2016). Indoor screenings use **Megabox Busan Theater** and the **Catholic Center Space 101.1**. Highlights include a 30th-anniversary talk on *Waikiki Brothers* with director Im Soon-rye and actor Park Hae-il, Ryoo Seung-wan on *The City of Violence*, and 18 audience-voted Request Cinema programmes. Tickets are **₩10,000** for general screenings, ₩15,000 for the Cinephiles Strike Back strand and ₩25,000 for the late-night food-and-drink programme.

![BIFF Square by day, with fruit-juice and chicken-skewer carts under red and yellow umbrellas](/images/biff-2026-10/16.webp)

Walk from Jagalchi or Nampo Station on [[line:busan1]]. From Centum City it is about an hour with a change at Seomyeon, so split Centum and Nampo-dong into a daytime and an evening. The square's ssiat hotteok stalls and Jagalchi Market are covered in the [Busan food guide](/en/posts/busan-food-2026-09/).

## The Busan Cinema Center when there is no festival

For the other 355 days the Cinema Center is a city-run **cinema and concert venue**. It opened on 29 September 2011 to a design by the Austrian studio **Coop Himmelb(l)au**. The signature is the **Big Roof**, which cantilevers **85 m** from a single cone-shaped column; at 163 m long, 60 m wide and 6,376 tonnes it holds the **Guinness World Record for the longest cantilever roof**. Its ceiling carries 42,600 LEDs that change colour after dark, and the Roof Theater lighting runs **from sunset to 23:00**. The square under it is public space, so walking around and photographing costs nothing.

Inside are the **4,000-seat outdoor theater**, the 841-seat Haneulyeon Theater, Cinema 1 (413), Cinema 2 (212) and the Cinematheque (212). In normal weeks the Cinematheque shows classic and art-house programmes, the smaller cinemas run new releases and Korean independents, and the Haneulyeon stages musicals and concerts.

Regular ticket prices vary by programme. This summer's World Cinema season in the Cinematheque was **₩7,000, or ₩5,000 for members, students and seniors**, and other in-house events charged ₩8,000. On the **last Wednesday of every month there is a free screening**, and a Hollywood film-music concert on 28 October is listed as free. From May to early September the outdoor theater runs free screenings on Tuesday and Wednesday evenings at 20:00, so you can sit in the 4,000 seats without a festival.

Because of BIFF, regular screenings are **suspended from 28 September and resume on 17 October**. The cinema box office is on the 6th floor of Cine Mountain, 09:00–21:00. The car park holds 501 cars, is free for 30 minutes then ₩1,000 per 30 minutes, and **free for four hours with a film or concert ticket**.

## Around Centum City

![Main entrance of Shinsegae Centum City department store](/images/biff-2026-10/18.webp)

Centum City is a planned district built on the old Suyeong airfield, so the grid is easy to walk. The Cinema Center, two department stores and BEXCO are all within ten minutes of the station.

**Shinsegae Centum City** took the Guinness title of **the world's largest department store** from Macy's in 2009. Hours are 10:30–20:00 (20:30 on extended days), restaurants 11:00–21:00, and it is **closed on Sunday 18 October**. There is an ice rink on the 4th floor and a rooftop dinosaur park on the 9th.

**Spa Land** on the 1st floor is a large jjimjilbang, the Korean bathhouse-plus-sauna format, with 18 hot-spring baths and 13 themed sauna rooms. It opens **09:00–22:00 (last entry 21:00)** and charges **₩26,000 for adults and ₩21,000 for students**, the same on weekdays and weekends. One entry covers **four hours**, then ₩5,000 per extra hour. Big suitcases do not fit the lockers, so use the storage by the ticket desk (₩3,000 for five hours). It closes too early for after-film visits, so slot it between daytime screenings.

**Lotte Department Store Centum City** faces Shinsegae and holds the Lotte Cinema venue on its 8th floor. **BEXCO**, opened in 2001 and host of the 2005 APEC summit, is where the film market runs, and it connects to the station by an underground passage.

![Centum City at night seen across the Suyeong River, with the Cinema Center roof lit up among the towers](/images/biff-2026-10/14.webp)

Behind the Cinema Center, **APEC Naru Park** follows the Suyeong River; from the far bank the lit roof reflects in the water, which makes a good evening walk.

## Hotels, and planning for 2027

Festival week fills Centum and Haeundae with industry guests. Busan's pattern of price spikes around big events is well documented: during the BTS concert weekend in June 2026, a Fair Trade Commission survey found average room rates 2.4 times normal and hotels 2.9 times, with Haeundae and Gwangalli rising less than other districts. No equivalent survey exists for BIFF, but the lesson is the same: **book as soon as the dates are announced**. Seomyeon (about 20 minutes) and Gwangalli (about 10) on [[line:busan2]] are the fallback areas. Haeundae options are in the [Haeundae hotels guide](/en/posts/haeundae-hotels-2026-10/).

BIFF has run every year since 1996 in **early October**. Dates are usually announced in the spring or summer before, the timetable three to four weeks before opening, and ticket sales two to three weeks before (this year 17 and 21 September). For 2027: check the dates in spring and book a room, register on the site a month out, and be online at the sale time.

## Practical notes

- **Getting there**: [[line:busan2]] Centum City Station exit 12 (or 6), **five minutes** on foot. About 15 minutes from BEXCO Station on [[line:donghae]]. Buses stop at Shinsegae Department Store; from Busan Station take [[bus:40]], [[bus:139]] or [[bus:1001]].
- **From Gimhae Airport**: the airport limousine (Haeundae route) stops at Shinsegae, about **50 minutes**. Or take the light rail to Sasang and change to [[line:busan2]].
- **Tickets only from the official site**, two per screening, mobile ticket by default. Cards work online and at box offices.
- **Free things**: stage greetings until 11 October, outdoor screenings at BIFF Square, the Cinema Center square and its night lighting.
- **English**: biff.kr/eng and an English booking page exist; venue staff and volunteers handle the queues.
- Schedules and guest line-ups change on the day, so check the official site before heading out.

## Wrapping up

With a ticket, spend a day in Centum City; without one, do the stage greetings and then Nampo-dong. Either way, see the Cinema Center roof after dark. Once the festival packs up, Spa Land, the Cinematheque and the free outdoor screenings keep Centum City worth a stop at any time of year.
