Tanks tearing through mud, special forces jumpers drifting down under smoke trails, and fighter jets overhead on one afternoon. Once a year the Korean Army opens the runway at Gyeryongdae, the joint headquarters of Korea's army, navy and air force, for the **Ground Forces Festival** (지상군페스티벌). The 2026 edition opened today, 1 October.

The short version: **entry and parking are free, and the easiest way in is a train to Gyeryong Station and a free shuttle bus.** If you can only pick one day, make it **Saturday 3 October**, the only day the Black Eagles fly.

Everything here is as of 1 October 2026. The official site notes that stage times may change.

![A K2 tank charging out of dust and smoke across a muddy track, a Korean flag on the turret](/images/gyeryong-ground-forces-festival-2026-10/02.webp)

## When and where

- **Dates** — Thursday 1 to Monday 5 October 2026
- **Venue** — Gyeryongdae runway area, Sindoan-myeon, Gyeryong, South Chungcheong Province (Chungcheongnam-do)
- **Hours** — 09:00 to 17:00, **extended to 21:00 on opening day only**
- **Cost** — free entry, free parking
- **Enquiries** — programme +82-42-550-5311, transport +82-42-550-5333 (Korean-language lines)

This is the **22nd** Ground Forces Festival, themed "the Army that guards people's everyday lives". It runs alongside the city's **16th Gyeryong Military Culture Festival** (계룡軍문화축제) on the same grounds, so for a visitor it works as one event.

Gyeryong expects **around 1 million visitors** this year. The city counted 1.14 million for the 2024 festival and 840,000 for 2025.

## What to see, and when

![The mud-spattered front of a K2 tank and its long gun, a crewman looking out of the driver's hatch](/images/gyeryong-ground-forces-festival-2026-10/01.webp)

The Army says it has expanded the line-up to **75 programmes**, up from 57 last year. The core is Korea's front-line kit, the K2 Black Panther tank and K9 self-propelled howitzer, on display, in demonstrations and with ride-along experiences, plus **Army TIGER+**, a showcase of drones and robots fighting alongside soldiers.

On the official timetable, demonstrations and hands-on sessions run **from 2 to 5 October**, at the same times each day. The 1 October daytime slot is blank.

| Time | Programme (2–5 October) |
| --- | --- |
| 09:30–11:00 | Military police motorcycle display (15 min), ride experience (1 hr) |
| 09:30–13:30 | Combat vehicle rides / demonstration ground |
| 11:50–12:00 | Army aviation flight demonstration |
| 13:30–15:00 | Military police motorcycle display, ride experience |
| 14:40–15:40 | **Army TIGER+ drone and robot combat demo, special forces freefall** |
| 16:30–17:00 | Road parade |

Vehicle rides are a morning-only thing, so **arrive in the morning** if you want one.

![Freefall jumpers trailing smoke across a blue sky under green parachutes](/images/gyeryong-ground-forces-festival-2026-10/05.webp)

**The Black Eagles, the Korean Air Force aerobatic team, fly on Saturday 3 October from 16:30 to 16:40, and only that day.** The road parade follows straight after.

## Stage shows and the opening night

The main stage rotates Army bands and honour guards, the Army taekwondo team and the Army performance troupe. On 3 and 5 October the line-up also includes bands and honour guards from the air force, the marines and the US Eighth Army.

Singers named on the official timetable:

- **2 October** 13:30 Navillera
- **3 October** 15:30 Gil Ryeo-won, 16:00 Son Tae-jin
- **4 October** 13:00 Navillera, 16:00 Dynamic Duo

**The opening ceremony is on 1 October, 18:30 to 20:40.** Local press reports a concert with singer Sohyang, a drone flight and a laser fireworks show. Shuttles run until 21:40 that night only.

On Sunday 4 October, the city says **about 730 people will march in a street parade** through downtown Gyeryong.

## Getting there

Gyeryong has its own station, **Gyeryong Station** (계룡역) on the Honam Line. KTX trains on the conventional Honam route via Seodaejeon stop here, as do ITX-Saemaeul and Mugunghwa trains, with departures from Yongsan Station in Seoul. Check times and fares on the Korail site or app for your date; they vary by train.

From the station a **free shuttle bus** runs to the venue. The official transport page lists two routes:

- **Gyeryong city route** — venue → Eomsa entrance → Gyeryong Health Centre → Gyeryong High School → LH Block 4 → Prugio Apartments → **Gyeryong Station** → Richvil Apartments → Jindeul stop → … → venue. **08:50 to 17:40, every 10 to 15 minutes.**
- **Car park loop** — venue → Friday Market → Gyeryongdae Shopping Town (bus stop) → Headquarters Church → Barracks Experience Hall → venue. **08:40 to 17:40, every 5 to 10 minutes.**

On 4 October from 13:30 to 15:30, the city route skips the Samjin Apartments and Eomsa crossroads stops because of the parade, and uses the Eomsa-ri stop instead.

## Driving

Visitor parking is free. The biggest lot is **Car Park 1** (3,178 spaces, 734 Gyeryongdae-ro). Others on the official map include Car Park 2 (790), Car Park 3 (400), Headquarters Church (494), and the Friday Market and Shopping Town lots (300 each). **The runway car park is reserved for visitors with limited mobility and pregnant women.**

The road in front of Gate 1 is closed to traffic, and the area runs as a clockwise one-way loop from the Gate 2 crossroads. If your navigation app sends you to Gate 1 you won't get in, so set a car park address as your destination instead.

## How the festival started

The Ground Forces Festival began in **2002 at Expo Science Park in Daejeon**, growing out of an Army weapons-systems seminar and a defence venture fair. After an agreement with Gyeryong in 2007, it has been held **on the Gyeryongdae runway since 2008**, together with the city's military culture festival. City records show that festival started in 2007; the 2025 edition ran from 17 to 21 September.

## Practical notes

- **Rain** may cancel some programmes; notices go up on the official site and at the venue.
- **The grounds close at 17:00** and the last shuttle leaves at 17:40. If you stay for the parade (16:30–17:00), head straight for the shuttle queue afterwards.
- **A runway is a big, open, shadeless space.** Bring a hat and water.
- The official materials say nothing specific for foreign visitors. This is held on a military base, so **carry your passport**.
- **Donghaksa Temple** on Gyeryongsan Mountain (462 Donghaksa 1-ro, Banpo-myeon) is actually in neighbouring Gongju, not Gyeryong. If you want to combine the two, check transport separately.

## Wrapping up

The Ground Forces Festival lets you stand right next to tanks and armoured vehicles, and with luck ride in one. Three things to remember: **rides in the morning**, **freefall at 14:40**, **Black Eagles on 3 October at 16:30.**
