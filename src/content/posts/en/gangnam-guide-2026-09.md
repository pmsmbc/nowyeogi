Gangnam is the half of Seoul that was rice fields fifty years ago and is now the most expensive real estate in the country. It is wide boulevards, glass towers, plastic surgery clinics, department stores and entertainment agencies. It is not where you go for old Seoul, and anyone telling you otherwise is selling something.

Stay here if you are shopping, seeing a K-pop concert, attending something at COEX, or on business. This is the hub guide; three companion guides cover [hotels](/en/posts/gangnam-hotels-2026-09/), [food](/en/posts/gangnam-food-2026-09/) and [what to see and do](/en/posts/gangnam-things-to-do-2026-09/). Information is as of September 2026.

## Gangnam is bigger than you think

![Gangnam-daero, the main boulevard through Gangnam](/images/gangnam-guide-2026-09/02.webp)

"Gangnam" literally means "south of the river", and the district covers about 40 square kilometres. Walking from one end to the other is not realistic. **Choose your station before you book anything.**

| Area | Station | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| **Gangnam Station** | [[line:2]] [[line:sinbundang]] | The busiest crossroads in Seoul. Chain restaurants, cinemas, underground shopping |
| **Samseong / COEX** | [[line:2]] | Convention centre, the mall, Starfield Library, Bongeunsa temple |
| **Sinsa / Garosu-gil** | [[line:3]] [[line:sinbundang]] | Tree-lined shopping street, cafés, boutiques |
| **Apgujeong Rodeo** | [[line:suinbundang]] | Luxury boutiques, entertainment agencies, K-Star Road |
| **Seolleung / Yeoksam** | [[line:2]] [[line:suinbundang]] | Offices, business hotels, the royal tombs |

Between them it is 10–25 minutes by subway. On foot it is often 30 minutes or more, so plan around stations rather than distances.

## Getting here from Incheon Airport

- **Airport bus [[bus:6103]]** — ₩17,000, runs to the COEX city airport terminal in Samseong. Easiest option with luggage if you are staying near COEX.
- **AREX to Seoul Station**, then [[line:9]] express or [[line:2]] via a transfer, roughly 80–90 minutes in total.
- **Taxi** — 60–80 minutes and ₩70,000–90,000 depending on traffic and tolls. Reasonable split between three or four people with bags.

Full fares and how to buy a Climate Card are in our [Incheon Airport to Seoul guide](/en/posts/incheon-airport-to-seoul-2026-09/).

## Who Gangnam suits

**It suits you if**: you are here to shop; you have a concert, convention or business meeting; you want a large, new hotel room; you are travelling with luggage and want space; you have already seen the palaces.

**It does not suit you if**: this is your first trip and you want atmosphere; you are on a budget; you want to walk everywhere; you like narrow streets and old buildings.

Honest summary: Gangnam is comfortable and expensive rather than charming. For a first visit, Myeongdong or Jongno gives you more, and our [neighborhood comparison](/en/posts/seoul-where-to-stay-2026-09/) sets all eight side by side.

## What is actually worth seeing

![A street in Yeoksam-dong lined with office buildings and shops](/images/gangnam-guide-2026-09/01.webp)

Three things justify the trip even if you stay elsewhere:

- **Starfield Library** at COEX — a 13-metre bookshelf wall in a mall atrium, free, open 10:00–22:00.
- **Bongeunsa temple** — a working Buddhist temple with towers behind it, ₩1,000, with an English temple stay programme once a month.
- **Seolleung and Jeongneung Royal Tombs** — UNESCO-listed Joseon burial mounds in a wooded park, ₩1,000, five minutes from the mall.

The last two are the surprise: quiet, old and genuinely worth an hour, in the middle of the most modern district in Korea. Details in the [things to do guide](/en/posts/gangnam-things-to-do-2026-09/).

## Where to stay, in one paragraph

Gangnam has Seoul's highest concentration of five-star hotels and the largest rooms in the city, at the highest prices. Expect ₩150,000–250,000 mid-range and ₩350,000–500,000 for the luxury tier. Budget options exist around Gangnam Station and Yeoksam but they are business hotels rather than character stays. The [hotel guide](/en/posts/gangnam-hotels-2026-09/) breaks it down by station.

## Where to eat, in one paragraph

Beef is the point. Gangnam has the best Korean barbecue in the city and charges for it, with hanwoo galbi easily reaching ₩60,000 a portion. Below that tier there is excellent office-worker lunch food at ₩10,000–15,000, a serious café scene on Garosu-gil, and a huge food court under COEX. See the [food guide](/en/posts/gangnam-food-2026-09/).

## A one-day Gangnam plan

**Morning**: Seolleung Royal Tombs, then walk to Bongeunsa.
**Midday**: COEX mall, Starfield Library, lunch in the food hall.
**Afternoon**: [[line:suinbundang]] to Apgujeong Rodeo for K-Star Road, then Garosu-gil for cafés and shopping.
**Evening**: Korean barbecue near Sinsa or Gangnam Station.

That is Gangnam in a day, which for most visitors is the right amount.

## Practical notes

- **Distances are deceptive.** Blocks are enormous. Use the subway between areas even when the map looks walkable.
- **It is the priciest part of the city** for food, coffee and taxis.
- **Rush hour on [[line:2]]** between 08:00 and 09:00 is genuinely crushing.
- **Underground shopping malls** at Gangnam Station and Express Bus Terminal are the cheapest shopping in the district.
- **Sinbundang Line** costs more than the regular subway because it is privately run. The Climate Card does not cover it.

## Wrapping up

Gangnam earns a day, not a week, unless you are here for a specific reason. Do the tombs and the temple in the morning, the mall and the library at midday, and the shopping streets in the afternoon. Then go back north for dinner.
