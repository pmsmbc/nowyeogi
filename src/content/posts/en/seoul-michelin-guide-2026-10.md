Seoul has 42 MICHELIN-starred restaurants in the 2026 guide, and the hard part is not finding their names but working out where they are and what they cook. This is the full list from the **MICHELIN Guide Seoul & Busan 2026**, announced on 5 March 2026, sorted by star level, cuisine and neighborhood. The short version: Mingles is still the city's only three-star, and 28 of the 42 sit in Gangnam-gu. Korean tasting menus and sushi cluster in Cheongdam, inventive cooking in Sinsa and Nonhyeon, and hotel dining rooms in Jung-gu and Jongno north of the river.

## What changed in the 2026 edition

This is the guide's tenth Korean edition. According to the Michelin Korea press release it covers 233 restaurants across Seoul and Busan: 46 with stars (42 in Seoul, 4 in Busan), 71 Bib Gourmands (51 in Seoul, 20 in Busan) and 4 Green Stars (3 in Seoul, 1 in Busan).

Ten restaurants gained a new or higher star across the two cities, which Michelin says is the most in a single Korean edition so far. In Seoul:

- **Two stars**: Mosu returned to the guide with two stars, and Sosuheon moved up from one.
- **New one-stars**: Collage, GiwaKang, SAN and Sushi Kanesaka.
- **Promoted to one star** from the Selected list: GAGGEN by Choi Junho, Hakusi and JUEUN.
- **Green Stars**: Gigas kept its Green Star, and Mitou and Gosari Express (a Bib Gourmand) earned new ones, bringing Seoul to three.

The special awards also went to Seoul rooms: the Sommelier Award to Lee Jeong-in of GiwaKang, the Service Award to manager Kim Il-woo of Gucci Osteria da Massimo Bottura, and Opening of the Year to chef Ian of Ian. The Young Chef Award went to Kim Chang-wook of Le DORER in Busan.

## Three stars: Mingles

**Mingles** holds Seoul's only three stars for the second year running. Chef Kang Min-goo cooks Korean-rooted dishes built on fermented sauces, vinegars, herbs and seasonal produce, as the Korea Tourism Organization listing describes it. It is at 19 Dosan-daero 67-gil in Gangnam-gu, a short walk from exit 4 of Apgujeong Rodeo Station on the [[line:suinbundang]]. The tourism listing gives hours of 12:00 to 22:00 with a break from 15:00 to 18:00, Monday closing, valet parking and bookings through CatchTable, the reservation app most Seoul tasting-menu restaurants use. Michelin's price symbol is ₩₩₩₩, the top band.

## Bib Gourmand and Selected

Below the stars, Seoul has 51 **Bib Gourmand** restaurants (good food at moderate prices) and 85 **MICHELIN Selected** entries, with five new Seoul Bib Gourmands this year. They are spread across the city, so they are not listed here; the Hongdae, Bukchon, Seochon, Seongsu and Yeonnam guides on this site cover them neighborhood by neighborhood.

## Practical notes

- **Price symbols**: Michelin uses four bands from ₩ to ₩₩₩₩ instead of figures. Among Seoul's 42 stars, 1 is ₩₩ (Légume), 10 are ₩₩₩ and 31 are ₩₩₩₩; every two- and three-star is ₩₩₩₩. Actual tasting-menu prices vary, so check before booking.
- **Booking**: Most Michelin restaurant pages carry a reserve button. Mingles takes bookings through CatchTable, according to the tourism listing. Eight-seat counters such as Sosuheon, Goryori Ken and Sushi Kanesaka fill early.
- **Closing days**: The tourism listings give Monday closing for Mingles and Sunday–Monday for Kwonsooksoo. Monday closing is common elsewhere too, so check the day first. Hours are as of the time of writing.
- **Getting around**: Cheongdam, Sinsa and Nonhyeon are close enough to walk between. Mingles is a walk from Apgujeong Rodeo Station on the [[line:suinbundang]]. The hotel dining rooms in Jung-gu and Jongno pair well with Gwanghwamun or Myeongdong hotels.
- **Parking**: The tourism listings note valet parking at Mingles and parking at Kwonsooksoo. Restaurants inside hotels use the hotel's parking.

## Wrapping up

Two-thirds of Seoul's starred restaurants are in Gangnam-gu, and within it the choice narrows to Cheongdam, Sinsa and Nonhyeon. For Korean tasting menus look at Mingles, Kwonsooksoo, La Yeon, Onjium and YUN; for sushi, Sosuheon, Sushi Matsumoto, Sushi Kanesaka and Muni; for inventive cooking, Jungsik, Evett, alla prima, Soigné and Mosu. All 70 Michelin entries in Gangnam-gu, Bib Gourmands included, are in the [Gangnam food guide](/en/posts/gangnam-food-2026-09/), and the Hannam-dong stars and Selected restaurants are in the [Hannam-dong food guide](/en/posts/hannam-food-2026-09/).
