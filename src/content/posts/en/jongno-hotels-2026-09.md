"Stay in Jongno" covers a lot of ground. The hostel lanes of Jonggak, the hanok of Ikseon-dong, the Bukchon hillside and the big hotel next to Dongdaemun Gate are all in Jongno-gu (Jongno district). This blog covers those neighbourhoods separately; this post covers **the three areas left in between**.

- **Insadong side** (Insa-dong, Gwanhun-dong, Gyeonji-dong, Gyeongun-dong, Unni-dong) — six tourist hotels with **909 rooms**, and no hostels at all
- **Jongno 2–4-ga and the Cheonggyecheon side** (Jongno 2-ga, Jongno 4-ga, Gwansu-dong, Jangsa-dong, Inui-dong, Hyoje-dong, Yeonji-dong, Wonnam-dong) — five hotels, **25 hostels** and many old inns
- **Daehak-ro** (Hyehwa-dong, Myeongnyun 1–4-ga, Dongsung-dong, Ihwa-dong, Yeongeon-dong, Chungsin-dong) — one hotel and **171 homestays**

Across the whole district, **358 of Seoul's 438 registered hanok stays (82%) are in Jongno-gu**. The "Jongno-gu as a whole" table below shows which neighbourhoods they sit in.

## How these were counted

Seoul Open Data Plaza publishes four licensing datasets: tourist accommodation (관광숙박업), general lodging (숙박업), foreign-tourist urban homestays (외국인관광 도시민박업) and hanok experience businesses (한옥체험업). From each, only entries marked "operating" whose legal-dong address is one of the **22 neighbourhoods below** were kept. Tourist accommodation and lodging are the 24 September 2026 update, homestays 27 September and hanok 23 September — the same data and method as the [Dongdaemun](/en/posts/dongdaemun-hotels-2026-10/) and [Euljiro](/en/posts/euljiro-hotels-2026-09/) posts. A "dong" here is a legal neighbourhood unit, smaller than a district.

| Area | Legal dong |
| --- | --- |
| Insadong side | Insa-dong, Gwanhun-dong, Gyeonji-dong, Gyeongun-dong, Unni-dong |
| Jongno 2–4-ga & Cheonggyecheon side | Jongno 2-ga, Jongno 4-ga, Gwansu-dong, Jangsa-dong, Inui-dong, Hyoje-dong, Yeonji-dong, Wonnam-dong (Jongno 3-ga, Yeji-dong and Hunjeong-dong have nothing operating) |
| Daehak-ro | Hyehwa-dong, Myeongnyun 1-ga, 2-ga, 3-ga, 4-ga, Dongsung-dong, Ihwa-dong, Yeongeon-dong, Chungsin-dong |

Neighbourhoods already counted elsewhere are left out, so nothing is counted twice:

- **Jongno 1-ga, Gwancheol-dong, Gongpyeong-dong, Seorin-dong, Cheongjin-dong, Susong-dong** — [Gwanghwamun, City Hall & Jonggak](/en/posts/gwanghwamun-hotels-2026-09/)
- **Ikseon-dong, Nagwon-dong, Donui-dong, Myo-dong, Gwonnong-dong, Waryong-dong, Bongik-dong** — [Ikseon-dong](/en/posts/ikseon-hotels-2026-09/)
- **Wonseo-dong, Gye-dong, Gahoe-dong, Anguk-dong** and the rest of Bukchon's 11 — [Bukchon](/en/posts/bukchon-hotels-2026-09/)
- **Jongno 5-ga, Jongno 6-ga, Changsin-dong, Sungin-dong** — [Dongdaemun](/en/posts/dongdaemun-hotels-2026-10/)
- The neighbourhoods west of Gyeongbokgung — [Seochon](/en/posts/seochon-hotels-2026-09/)

That is why **several big hotels with "Insadong" in their name are not in the main table**: their addresses are in Ikseon-dong or Nagwon-dong. They are listed under "Just outside the boundary".

### 25 hostels, mostly from 2026

Of the 25 hostels on this side, **18 were registered in 2026 and 5 in 2025**. Most of those with a room count have **2–6 rooms**, often on one or two floors of a building on the main road.

- Gwansu-dong: The Extay Lounge (Davich Tower, 4th–7th floors, **24 rooms**), Hello Hostel (14), The Elly Jongno (9), takt Jongno (4)

Hostels are tourist accommodation, so **Korean nationals can stay too**, unlike homestays. Most opened this year, so recent reviews may be few.

### The inn lanes of Gwansu-dong and Hyoje-dong

Lodging-only entries are most numerous in **Gwansu-dong (11) and Hyoje-dong (12)**.

- **Gwansu-dong** has a cluster of general hotels in the lanes of Supyo-ro 18-gil and Jongno 18-gil. Hotel The Designers Jongno (81 rooms) is a **general hotel licensed in 1965**; Hotel K-World Cheonggye (60), Hotel Lian (59), Ondo Hotel & Spa Seoul Jongno (58), The Post Hotel and Napore Hotel (52 each) are in the same lanes. Weco Stay Insadong (70 rooms) is, despite its name, **a serviced residence in Gwansu-dong**
- **Hyoje-dong**: 10 of its 12 are **inns**, clustered on Daehak-ro 2-gil and Jongno 35-gil. Star Hostel Dongdaemun (1973), About Stay Dongdaemun (1968) and JO Hostel Dongdaemun (1984) are **called hostels but licensed as inns decades ago**, and despite "Dongdaemun" in their names they are in Hyoje-dong, north of Jongno 5-ga Station
- Yeonji-dong: Five Hotel Jongno (81 rooms) is a general hotel

As in the Gwanghwamun and Dongdaemun posts: **don't read the business type from the word "hotel" or "hostel" in a name.** Check photos and recent reviews. The Gwansu-dong and Jongno 3-ga lanes also have bars and restaurants open late.

## Before booking a hanok

Most of Jongno-gu's hanok stays are in Bukchon and Seochon; this post's area has just 26. What the hanok-experience registration rules require — and don't — is laid out in the [Ikseon-dong post](/en/posts/ikseon-hotels-2026-09/). In short:

- **A private bathroom is not required.** The rules only ask for bathing or shower facilities. Check whether the bathroom is inside your room before booking
- **There is no rule that you sleep on the floor.** Some hanok stays have beds
- **There is no 24-hour front desk requirement.** If you arrive late, contact the host in advance
- Unlike foreign-tourist homestays, hanok stays are **not limited to foreign guests**

**The visiting-hour limits in Bukchon's "red zone" (Bukchon-ro 11-gil)** are outside this post's area. Whether guests staying inside the red zone can come and go in the evening is covered, with Jongno-gu's official guidance, in the [Bukchon post](/en/posts/bukchon-hotels-2026-09/).

## Summary

- This post's 22 neighbourhoods (Insadong side, Jongno 2–4-ga, Daehak-ro) have **46 tourist-accommodation businesses with 1,853 rooms**: 12 tourist hotels and 34 hostels.
- The biggest hotels are **Nine Tree Insadong (301), Amid (250), Orakai Daehakro (232), Hotel Venue G (163) and The Prima Jongno (155)**.
- Ibis Ambassador, Orakai Insadong Suites, Moxy and others named "Insadong" are at **Ikseon-dong and Nagwon-dong** addresses.
- Daehak-ro has one hotel but **171 homestays**, 75% registered since 2025.
- **358 of Seoul's 438 hanok stays are in Jongno-gu**; 26 are in this area, including the district's earliest-registered, Eugene House in Hyehwa-dong (2009).
- Airport buses: **6002 from Jongno 3-ga, last at 20:45**; **6011 from Changgyeonggung at 20:39 and Anguk at 20:45**. The late-night bus leaves from Dongdaemun or Gwanghwamun.
- From the Airport Railroad, change at Seoul Station to [[line:1]] (Jongno 3-ga) or [[line:4]] (Hyehwa), or at Gongdeok to [[line:5]] (Jongno 3-ga).
