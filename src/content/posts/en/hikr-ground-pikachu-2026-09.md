Pikachu is wearing **hanbok**, Korean traditional dress, in central Seoul until 11 October. The venue is **HiKR Ground**, a free Korea tourism showcase run by the Korea Tourism Organization in its Seoul Center, a short walk from Jonggak Station on the Cheonggyecheon Stream.

The short version: **exhibits on 2F and 3F, merchandise on 5F, stamp prize on 1F.** The fourth floor is not part of the event.

![The glass-fronted entrance of a building with a HiKR GROUND sign, beanbags and information banners on a lawn to the left](/images/hikr-ground-pikachu-2026-09/04.webp)

## Getting the name right

The official Korean title is 포켓몬 메가페스타 2026: 피카츄의 가을 나들이, which translates as **Pokémon Mega Festa 2026: Pikachu's Autumn Outing**. It marks the franchise's 30th anniversary and is organised by Pokémon Korea together with the Korea Tourism Organization and AREX, the airport railway.

A second, similar name is doing the rounds. Inside the mobile game Pokémon GO, the event running on the same dates is called **2026 Pikachu's Autumn Picnic**. "Outing" is the physical event at HiKR Ground; "Picnic" is the in-game one.

- **Dates:** 18 September – 11 October 2026
- **Hours:** 10:00–20:00 for both the 2F–3F exhibition and the 5F pop-up
- **Entry:** the official event page still reads "to be announced separately" and warns that entry may be restricted when it gets crowded.

## What's on each floor

This follows the venue map published by Pokémon Korea.

- **2F** — exhibits and photo zones featuring hanbok Pikachu, plus scheduled photo time with Pikachu
- **3F** — booths for the Pokémon Trading Card Game, the anime series and merchandise displays
- **5F** — the pop-up store, named 포켓몬 고풍 상점 (roughly "Pokémon Old-Style Shop"), selling original goods designed for the event. The official page says the line-up is announced separately.
- **1F** — one stamp station and the prize desk, in front of the information centre

Some news coverage describes the exhibition as filling floors 1 to 3. The official map is explicit that the event runs on **2F, 3F and 5F, and 4F is unrelated**. Pre-opening reports say the 5F shop sells original hanbok-Pikachu goods and capsule toys.

Reports on the Korea Tourism Organization's announcement add that the venue also shows regional travel information during the event, including autumn destinations, festivals, traditional markets and island trips.

![A long media wall showing a road lined with pink blossom trees, with queue posts running along in front](/images/hikr-ground-pikachu-2026-09/03.webp)

## Stamp rally and freebies

The **stamp rally** links HiKR Ground with AREX stations.

- **Stamp locations:** HiKR Ground 1F (in front of the information centre), 2F and 3F; Seoul Station City Airport Terminal; Incheon Airport Terminal 1 Station (HiKR Station) and Terminal 2 Station
- **Hours:** HiKR Ground 10:00–20:00; the AREX stations 10:00–19:00
- **Prize:** collect **two or more** stamps for a Pokémon 30th-anniversary sticker, handed out in front of the 1F information centre

Since HiKR Ground alone has three stamps, you can qualify without leaving the building.

Two more giveaways:

- **Sun visor** — show that you follow Pokémon Korea's official Instagram (@pokemon_korea_official) on your way in. First come, first served each day.
- **Pokémon HOME medal** — find the dedicated panel inside the venue, open the smartphone version of Pokémon HOME, go to Medals → Scan → NFC Scan, and tap your phone on the panel. One issue per day, two copies of the medal each time, and the limit resets at 09:00 Korea time. It won't work while the app is under maintenance.

## AREX and Pokémon GO

**AREX**, the rail link between Seoul Station and Incheon Airport, is running an express train with a Pokémon-decorated interior during the event, boarding at Seoul Station City Airport Terminal and Incheon Airport Terminals 1 and 2. A special event-design voucher is on sale at the same three stations; it is valid for a year and is exchanged on site for an express train ticket.

In Pokémon GO, **2026 Pikachu's Autumn Picnic** runs 10:00–20:00 on the same dates, but only in **parts of Jongno-gu and Jung-gu in Seoul, and Incheon International Airport**. This is where **Pikachu in an orange hanbok** makes its debut. The nationwide spin-off, Pikachu's Korea Tour, ran 24–26 September and is over.

The event itself is indoors. The official pages list no outdoor programme on the Cheonggyecheon walkway; the stream connection is simply that the Pokémon GO zone covers Jongno-gu and Jung-gu.

## HiKR Ground the rest of the time

It is worth a stop even without Pikachu. The name combines "Hi", "KR" for Korea and "playground", and **admission is free**.

According to the Korea Tourism Organization's accessible-travel listing, the floors normally hold:

- **1F** — HiKR Wall, a large media-art screen
- **2F** — a K-pop zone where you film your own music video in an XR studio
- **3F** — HiKR Street, Korean culture and contemporary art through AI, AR and XR
- **4F** — regional tourism exhibits
- **5F** — a photo terrace over Cheonggyecheon and a tourist information centre

![A purple-lit bus shelter photo set beside a vending machine with a HiKR logo, bus-lane markings painted on the floor](/images/hikr-ground-pikachu-2026-09/02.webp)

As of writing, **1F and 5F open daily 10:00–20:00 and 2F–4F open Tuesday to Sunday 10:00–20:00**, with last entry 20 minutes before closing. The 5F tourist information centre keeps 10:00–19:00. **The building is closed on Mondays.** There is step-free access, a lift and accessible toilets.

![Lettering sculptures shaped like regional specialities, such as snow crab and Hanwoo beef, lined up under a 'TEXT-HIP Traditional Market' sign](/images/hikr-ground-pikachu-2026-09/01.webp)

## Practical notes

- **Address:** 40 Cheonggyecheon-ro, Jung-gu, Seoul (Korea Tourism Organization Seoul Center)
- **Subway:** about two minutes on foot from Exit 5 of Jonggak Station on [[line:1]]
- **From the airport:** ride the [[line:arex]] express and you can pick up the Seoul Station and Incheon Airport stamps on the way.
- **Cost:** HiKR Ground is free and the official event pages list no admission charge. Goods on 5F are paid.
- **Mondays:** 2F–4F normally close on Mondays, and the Pokémon exhibition sits on 2F and 3F, so check before going on a Monday (see below).
- **Final weekend:** the last day, 11 October, is a Sunday.

## Wrapping up

A simple plan: see 2F and 3F, stamp on each floor, drop into the 5F shop, then pick up the sticker at the 1F information centre. Install Pokémon HOME beforehand and you can collect the medal too.
