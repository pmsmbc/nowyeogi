Search for a room in Itaewon and you get a few big hotels mixed in with hundreds of "Itaewon house" listings. Count Itaewon-dong and Haebangchon (Yongsan-dong 2-ga) in Seoul's licensing data and the picture is this:

- **8 registered tourist hotels and hostels**, all in Itaewon-dong. The five with a room count add up to 583 rooms
- **6 licensed inns (yeogwan)**, some of them with "hotel" in the name
- **225 urban homestays for foreign tourists** — 173 in Itaewon-dong (seventh among all of Seoul's neighbourhoods) and 52 in Haebangchon

The short version: **most places to sleep in Itaewon are not hotels but one- or two-room homestays in residential lanes.** Those homestays are, in principle, for foreign visitors only, so a Korean traveller is effectively left with the 8 hotels and hostels and the 6 inns. This guide shows which lanes the numbers sit in, what happened to the old Crown Hotel site, and where to catch the airport bus.

![Colourful houses packed onto the Haebangchon hillside on Namsan's southern slope under a clear blue sky, with a pointed spire on the ridge](/images/itaewon-hotels-2026-09/01.webp)

## How these were counted

The counts use four licensing datasets on Seoul Open Data Plaza, keeping only businesses marked "open" whose **legal-dong address is Itaewon-dong or Yongsan-dong 2-ga**. Tourist accommodation and lodging data are the 24 September 2026 update, homestays the 27 September update and hanok stays the 23 September update — the same sources and method as the [Bukchon](/en/posts/bukchon-hotels-2026-09/) and [Myeongdong](/en/posts/myeongdong-hotels-2026-09/) guides.

| Dataset | Law | What it covers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tourist accommodation | Tourism Promotion Act (registration) | Tourist hotels, hostels, small hotels |
| Lodging business | Public Health Control Act (report) | Inns, motels, general hotels, serviced residences |
| Urban homestay for foreign tourists | Tourism Promotion Act (designation) | Residents hosting foreign tourists in the home they live in |
| Hanok stay | Tourism Promotion Act (registration) | Stays or culture programmes in traditional houses |

| Legal dong | Tourist accommodation | Inns | Homestays | Hanok stays |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Itaewon-dong | **8** | 6 | **173** | 0 |
| Yongsan-dong 2-ga (Haebangchon) | 0 | 0 | **52** | 0 |
| **Total** | **8** | **6** | **225** | **0** |

Three things to know about these numbers:

- **The administrative districts Itaewon 1-dong and Itaewon 2-dong are both Itaewon-dong as a legal dong.** The data only carries the legal dong, so the two could not be split. Street names are used below instead
- **The area around Noksapyeong Station is mostly Itaewon-dong too.** Yongsan-gu Office (150 Noksapyeong-daero) and the old Crown Hotel site (140 Noksapyeong-daero) both have Itaewon-dong addresses
- **Big hotels appear twice**, once as tourist hotels and again in the lodging data. To avoid double counting, the inn column only counts **yeogwan** that are not in the tourist list. Three hostels registered in 2024–2025 also appear a second time in the lodging data as serviced residences

Hannam-dong and Bogwang-dong are counted in the [Hannam-dong guide](/en/posts/hannam-hotels-2026-09/). The earlier versions of this post and that one covered the same streets and their numbers overlapped; they are now split by dong (see "What I got wrong").

- **Mondrian Seoul Itaewon** describes itself on its official site as having **295 rooms and suites**, two fewer than registered. The site lists a bar with a rooftop terrace and a pool lounge, and the operating company in its footer is Hotel Capital Co. It is the biggest hotel in the neighbourhood, and **its address really is Itaewon-dong**
- **Hamilton Hotel** sits right by Itaewon Station at 179 Itaewon-ro. Its official site says it **opened in 1973 and was extensively remodelled in 2015**, and that it is a one-minute walk from exits 1 and 2 of Itaewon Station. The **outdoor pool** on the fifth floor of the main building opens from late May to early September and is **closed to anyone under 19**. The lodging data dates its licence to 1973 and the tourist registration to 1975
- **Nuber Seoul Hotel** (71 rooms; romanized from its Korean registered name) is on Usadan-ro 14-gil in the south of Itaewon-dong. It was registered in September 2023 at the same address as the I.T.W Hotel (71 rooms), which closed in 2022
- **A1 Hotel** (21 rooms) is a small hotel in a lane behind Itaewon-ro
- **Three of the four hostels were registered in 2024–2025.** Two of them, Stay Pachira and Why Not Sleep, share one lane, Bogwang-ro 59-gil, and were registered on the same day, 18 December 2025

The new building is **two blocks of 4 basement and 14 above-ground floors**, with **29 apartments and 4 officetel units**, branded "Eterno Yongsan". The reports mention no hotel. If you stay between Noksapyeong Station and Yongsan-gu Office, **expect a construction site next door until 2029**.

The lodging data also keeps **18 closed records** for Itaewon-dong, mostly inns licensed in the 1960s and 1970s, plus the Crown, the I.T.W Hotel and the 42-room "Hotel Doro" on Itaewon-ro 27-gil, licensed in 2002.

## 52 homestays in Haebangchon, mostly new

Haebangchon's legal dong is **Yongsan-dong 2-ga** (administrative dong Yongsan 2-ga-dong; the community center is at 90 Sinheung-ro). It has **no** hotels or inns; the four old inn records in the lodging data (licensed 1969–2003) are all closed. What remains is 52 homestays.

- **38 of the 52 (73%) were designated in 2025 or later**, 24 of them in 2026 alone
- **48 are on Sinheung-ro or its side lanes**: Sinheung-ro itself (12), Sinheung-ro 7-gil and 11-gil (5 each), Sinheung-ro 12-gil (4)
- Of the 47 with a room count, **23 have one room and 20 have two**
- Buildings: 21 multi-household houses, 12 multi-unit houses, 11 detached houses. Median floor area is **about 54 m²**, a little smaller than Itaewon-dong
- Only five closed records exist — **a much newer neighbourhood** than Itaewon-dong

![Inside Sinheung Market in Haebangchon, a glass-roofed lane lined with small shops and green chairs and tables](/images/itaewon-hotels-2026-09/03.webp)

Haebangchon sits on Namsan's southern slope and its lanes have plenty of stairs. With heavy luggage, **ask about the slope, the floor and whether there is a lift** before booking. Several listings use "Namsan view" or "rooftop" in their names; check the actual view in the photos.

## Homestays are for foreign visitors only

Article 2 of the Enforcement Decree of the Tourism Promotion Act (version in force from 4 August 2026) defines an urban homestay for foreign tourists as a business where **a city resident, in the house they live in**, provides lodging **to foreign tourists**.

1. **Korean nationals cannot, in principle, stay in them.** Korean travellers need one of the 8 hotels and hostels or 6 inns above, or the hotels in the [Hannam-dong guide](/en/posts/hannam-hotels-2026-09/)
2. **The host has to live there.** Renting out an empty flat is not legal under this licence

Airbnb made **business registration documents mandatory for existing listings from 16 October 2025**, and said listings without them **could not take bookings from 1 January 2026**. The platform may not stop a Korean national from booking a foreigner-only homestay, so **ask the host about the licence type before you book.**

Living with neighbours, in practice:

- **Next door is someone's home** and the host lives in the same building. Late-night chat and suitcase wheels carry in narrow lanes
- **There is no 24-hour front desk.** If you arrive late, get the check-in instructions in advance

## Just outside the boundary

Walkable from Itaewon, but not counted here:

- **Hannam-dong** — Grand Hyatt Seoul (620 rooms) and Imperial Palace Boutique Hotel (128), plus 21 homestays. The Grand Hyatt sits on the Namsan slope close to Itaewon 2-dong, but **its address is Hannam-dong**. [Hannam-dong guide](/en/posts/hannam-hotels-2026-09/)
- **Bogwang-dong** — 2 inns and 14 homestays. [Hannam-dong guide](/en/posts/hannam-hotels-2026-09/)
- **Huam-dong** — west of Haebangchon. It has **95 homestays** (15th in Seoul) and 2 inns, and is not yet covered by another guide on this blog

## Getting to the airport

**The Airport Railroad does not run through Itaewon or Haebangchon.** One airport bus, K Limousine [[bus:6702]], stops in the two dongs covered here. Fares are 18,000 won for adults and 12,000 won for children aged 6–12 (about $13.50 and $9).

### To the airport

Route 6702 starts at Jangchung-dong and runs via Dongdaemun, Yaksu and Beotigogae before calling at the Grand Hyatt, the Itaewon 2-dong Community Center and Boseong Girls' School stops. There are 25 runs a day.

| Stop (number) | Near | First | Last |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Namsan (Grand Hyatt Seoul) (03680) | Hannam-dong, in front of the Grand Hyatt | 04:26 | 20:18 |
| **Itaewon 2-dong Community Center (03343)** | **Yongam Elementary School** | 04:28 | **20:20** |
| **Boseong Girls' School entrance (03171)** | **Yongsan 2-ga-dong Community Center / Boseong Girls' School** | 04:29 | **20:21** |
| Namsan (Millennium Seoul Hilton) | Next stop | 04:33 | 20:28 |

- Stop numbers are from Seoul's bus stop location data, where the stops are named **"Yongam Elementary School / Itaewon 2-dong Community Center"** and **"Yongsan 2-ga-dong Community Center / Boseong Girls' Middle and High School"**
- The Itaewon 2-dong Community Center building is at 58 Hoenamu-ro 13-gil, and Yongsan 2-ga-dong's is at 90 Sinheung-ro. Check the exact stop position in a map app by its number
- **The last bus is around 20:20.** For a late-evening flight this bus often won't work
- Route 6702 does not stop in front of Itaewon or Noksapyeong stations

## Which to choose

- **Korean traveller who needs a hotel** — Hamilton Hotel by Itaewon Station (166 rooms) or Mondrian on Jangmun-ro (297). For more, the Grand Hyatt and Imperial Palace in the [Hannam-dong guide](/en/posts/hannam-hotels-2026-09/)
- **Walking distance to Itaewon Station, on a budget** — the four hostels behind Itaewon-ro and Bogwang-ro (H Hostel, Fellaz, Pachira, Why Not Sleep) and A1 Hotel
- **Foreign visitor who wants a residential lane** — homestays around Itaewon-ro 16-gil and Noksapyeong-daero 40-gil. Seventh-highest count in Seoul
- **Near Gyeongnidan-gil** — the Hoenamu-ro homestays (32). The likely airport bus stop is Itaewon 2-dong Community Center (6702)

## Summary

- Itaewon-dong and Haebangchon have **8 registered hotels and hostels** (all in Itaewon-dong); the five with room counts total 583 rooms. The largest are **Mondrian (297) and Hamilton (166)**.
- **Six inns**, two of them with "hotel" in the name.
- **The Crown Tourist Hotel site** (closed 2022) began construction in June 2026: two 14-storey blocks, 33 units, due October 2029.
- **173 homestays in Itaewon-dong (7th in Seoul) and 52 in Haebangchon**, 35% of Yongsan-gu. 76 and 38 respectively were designated since 2025.
- Homestays are allowed only **in the host's own home** and **for foreign visitors**.
- Airport bus **6702 at Itaewon 2-dong Community Center (last 20:20) and Boseong Girls' School (20:21)**; by rail, **change at Gongdeok to Line 6** for Noksapyeong and Itaewon.
