Our [Itaewon food guide](/en/posts/itaewon-food-2026-09/) covers halal food, world cuisines, craft beer and Hannam-dong brunch with rough price ranges. This post goes one level deeper and keeps only **places that appear on official lists**.

The short version: the area has **six MICHELIN Guide Seoul 2026 starred or Bib Gourmand restaurants**, and 18 entries once you add the Selected ones. On the other hand, no restaurant here is on Seoul's OraeGage (long-standing shops) list, and the Korea Tourism Organization's **Muslim-friendly restaurant categories were last updated with December 2021 data**. Below, what can be confirmed is kept apart from what cannot.

![A dark brick building on a Haebangchon lane, with a shop under a striped awning and a small café with a white sign side by side, a sandwich board at the door](/images/hannam-food-2026-09/01.webp)

## MICHELIN 2026: four starred, two Bib Gourmand

The MICHELIN Guide announced Seoul's 42 starred restaurants on **5 March 2026**. Checking every address on the official Seoul list against the map gives **18 entries** in Itaewon, Hannam-dong and Haebangchon: along Itaewon-ro, Hannam-daero, Hoenamu-ro, Noksapyeong-daero, Bogwang-ro, Daesagwan-ro, Dokseodang-ro and Haebangchon's Sinheung-ro.

| Distinction | Name | Cuisine | Price band |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Two stars (new) | Mosu | Modern | ₩₩₩₩ |
| One star | Soseoul Hannam | Korean | ₩₩₩₩ |
| One star (new) | Escondido | Mexican | ₩₩₩ |
| One star | Soul | Contemporary | ₩₩₩ |
| Bib Gourmand | Youhan | Thai | ₩₩ |
| Bib Gourmand | ALT.a | Vegan (Chinese) | ₩ |

**The ₩ symbols are bands, not amounts.** The English pages label ₩ "On a budget" and ₩₩₩₩ "Spare no expense". A Bib Gourmand means you can eat for **₩45,000 (about $34) or less per person**.

### Two on Hannam-daero 20-gil

**Soseoul Hannam** and **Escondido** sit on the same lane, both in basements.

- **Soseoul Hannam** — The MICHELIN write-up describes contemporary Korean food built on everyday techniques such as seasoned salads, pan-fried and braised dishes. Kelp chips, soybean paste stew, raw fish with gomchwi and aged kimchi, and chargrilled webfoot octopus are named as signatures, with a wide range of traditional liquors.
- **Escondido** — The chef learned to cook in Mexico, and the kitchen is described as centred on tortillas and mole. It gained **its first star in 2026**.

### Mosu

**4 Hoenamu-ro 41-gil.** Listed as a **new two-star** in 2026. The MICHELIN write-up names abalone taco, sesame tofu and burdock tarte as signatures and notes that each floor has its own atmosphere. It is in the top price band.

### Soul, in Haebangchon

**B1, 35 Sinheung-ro 26-gil.** Run by chef Yoon Dae-hyun with his wife, chef Kim Hee-eun. Upstairs in the same building is **Egg & Flour**, a fresh-pasta restaurant on the MICHELIN Selected list, so one building in Haebangchon holds two entries.

### Bib Gourmand: Youhan and ALT.a

- **Youhan** (10-5 Daesagwan-ro 5-gil) — A small Thai bistro by chef Kim Yu-han, who went to culinary school in Thailand. The write-up warns that it is **hidden deep in the Hannam-dong alleys**, and mentions natural wine pairings.
- **ALT.a** (109 Bogwang-ro) — A **fully plant-based Chinese restaurant**. Vegan jjamppong (spicy seafood-style noodle soup, here without seafood), tangsuyuk and yurinyuk are named as signatures, and MICHELIN rates it as simply a good Chinese restaurant, vegan or not.

**None of the eight new Bib Gourmands for 2026 are in this area.** Both of these were carried over from earlier selections.

### A budget pick among the Selected

Among the **Selected** entries (listed in the guide, no star or Bib), only **Kyoyang Siksa** (238 Itaewon-ro) has the ₩ band. According to MICHELIN it grills Australian lamb under 10 months old, Sapporo style, and seating is limited, so book ahead. The other Selected entries are Bawi, Borgo Hannam, Chez Nous Private Kitchen, Chez Simon, Egg & Flour, Manao, Mr. Ahn's Craft Makgeolli, Osteria Orzo, Table for Four, A L'AISE and JUE.

**The MICHELIN pages give no menu prices or opening hours.** Check each restaurant's own channels for booking and closing days.

## Halal: the official categories stop at 2021

![A thin round lahmacun on a dark board, the red minced-meat topping covered with shredded lettuce and onion](/images/hannam-food-2026-09/03.webp)

The photo shows a typical Turkish lahmacun, not a dish from any particular restaurant.

The Korea Tourism Organization's English site sorts Muslim-friendly restaurants into **four categories**.

- **Halal Certified** — certified by an accredited body such as the Korea Muslim Federation (KMF)
- **Self-certified** — all food is halal, as declared by the Muslim owner
- **Muslim Friendly** — some halal dishes; **alcohol may be sold**
- **Pork Free** — no halal menu, but no pork; alcohol may be sold

The same page warns that **hotel buffets categorised as Muslim Friendly may serve pork dishes.**

The problem is the date. The restaurant guidebook offered on that page says its **information is valid as of December 2021**, and a 2024 Kukinews youth-reporter article says the KTO categorisation programme **ran only until 2021**.

By that guidebook, Yongsan-gu had **six Halal Certified restaurants** (out of 12 nationwide) and **16 Self-certified** ones.

| Category (December 2021) | Restaurants in Yongsan-gu |
| --- | --- |
| Halal Certified | Eid (Korean, 67 Usadan-ro 10-gil), Mr. Kebab Itaewon (192 Itaewon-ro), Kervan Itaewon (190 Itaewon-ro), Kervan Turkish Bakery (204 Itaewon-ro), Sultan Kebab (126 Bogwang-ro), Jipbob Kimsunsaeng (Korean) |
| Self-certified | Makan (Korean), Salam, Kervan Café, Taj Palace, Dubai, Usmania, Cairo BBQ, Zaffran BBQ, Zaffran Restaurant, Lazzat, Itaewon Kitchen, Little India, Pak India, Petra, Murree Muslim Food, Arabesque |

**This table is nearly five years old.** Certificates expire, so current status is unknown, and the KMF website was blocking access when this was written, so we could not check its live list. **Look for the certificate on the wall and its expiry date.** That is the most reliable check.

The Kukinews article counts **29 of Seoul's 122 halal restaurants in Itaewon**, and only 18 halal restaurants in all of Seoul serving Korean food. That makes places like Eid, Jipbob Kimsunsaeng and Makan in the table unusual.

## Haebangchon Sinheung Market

![A narrow Haebangchon lane under utility poles and wires, low houses and shops on both sides and a person with a backpack walking away](/images/hannam-food-2026-09/02.webp)

Haebangchon ("liberation village") grew on the slope below Namsan after **liberation in 1945**, settled by people displaced from the North, returnees from abroad and war refugees (Korea Tourism Organization). **Sinheung Market** is its market.

- A market for daily necessities **from 1953**, declining after the 1990s (Seoul city, 2023)
- In the **early 1960s** shacks were replaced with cement buildings under a continuous slate roof
- Seoul made Haebangchon an urban regeneration area in **December 2015**. In **November 2016**, all 44 building and land owners and 46 tenants signed a **six-year pact to keep rent rises within inflation** (Seoul city, 2021)
- The new roof, **CLOUD, won the grand prize at the 42nd Seoul Architecture Awards** (Seoul city, 2024)

**Opening hours vary by shop.** The 2021 city report describes a mix of new cafés and leather workshops with older shops that keep a 1970s-80s look. Individual restaurants inside the market could not be confirmed from official sources, so none are named.

On the same Sinheung-ro are **Soul and Egg & Flour** (35 Sinheung-ro 26-gil) and the good price shop **Myeonmaru** (86 Sinheung-ro).

## Good to know

**Smoking is banned in all restaurants and cafés**, and **there is no tipping.**

In the KTO scheme, **the categories explicitly allowed to sell alcohol are Muslim Friendly and Pork Free.** Do not rely on the word "halal" on a sign alone.

**Sinheung Market and Haebangchon are on a hill.** The tourism organization notes that a slope lift has been installed on Haebangchon's 108 Steps.

**Usadan-ro and Haebangchon are residential.** Keep your voice down in the lanes late at night.

## What we could not confirm

**The KMF's current list of halal-certified restaurants** could not be accessed. The halal table above is the KTO's December 2021 data.

**Seoul OraeGage** lists five shops in Yongsan-gu, but the four in Itaewon, Hannam and Haebangchon (Lee Frameshop, Hapdeok Supermarket, Itaewon Books, Hansin Pottery) are not restaurants. No restaurant OraeGage was found in this area.

**Seoul Future Heritage and Centennial Shop** (Ministry of SMEs and Startups) restaurants were not checked.

**Menu prices and hours for the MICHELIN entries** are not on the official pages.

**Myeonmaru's** published address has no neighbourhood name. We grouped it with Haebangchon based on its Sinheung-ro street number.

**Individual restaurants on Usadan-gil and in the antique furniture street** could not be confirmed from official lists and are left out.

## Summary

- **MICHELIN Guide Seoul 2026**: Mosu (two stars), Soseoul Hannam, Escondido and Soul (one star), Youhan and ALT.a (Bib Gourmand), and 18 entries including Selected.
- **The KTO's Muslim-friendly categories stop at December 2021 data.** At that time Yongsan-gu had six Halal Certified restaurants. Check the certificate in person today.
- **Haebangchon Sinheung Market** dates from 1953, and its new roof won the grand prize at the 42nd Seoul Architecture Awards.
- **Good price shops**: ₩8,000 stews and noodles in Itaewon-dong and ₩8,000 pollock soup in Hannam-dong are the published prices.
