Hannam-dong runs east from Itaewon Station to Hangangjin Station, Hannam Station and the hillside villas of UN Village. Compared with the Itaewon side, it has **more booking-only restaurants and tasting menus**, and more of the names on official lists.

The short version: **11 restaurants with Hannam-dong addresses are in the MICHELIN Guide Seoul 2026** (One Stars Soseoul Hannam and Escondido, Bib Gourmand Youhan and eight Selected). Only one, **Kyoyang Siksa**, carries MICHELIN's lowest ₩ price band, and only one, **Buja Sikdang**, is on the government's good-price list. In between are 17 restaurants and cafés listed by the Korea Tourism Organization (KTO).

## What this guide covers

Only places with a **Hannam-dong** address. In practice that is the eastern stretch of Itaewon-ro (roughly from number 220 onwards), Hannam-daero, Daesagwan-ro, Dokseodang-ro and the UN Village lanes, served by Hangangjin Station on [[line:6]] and Hannam Station on [[line:gyeongui]].

**The western side of Itaewon, Gyeongnidan-gil and Haebangchon now live in [Where to eat in Itaewon](/en/posts/itaewon-food-2026-09/).** Mosu, Soul and ALT.a (MICHELIN), KTO's halal restaurant list, Haebangchon's Sinheung Market and the Itaewon-dong good-price restaurants that were in the earlier version of this guide are all there.

Seoul Central Mosque (39 Usadan-ro 10-gil) has a Hannam-dong address, and the Korea Muslim Federation describes it as on "Usadan-ro (Hannam-dong)". But the halal restaurants around it sit on the climb up from Itaewon Station, so **the full halal list is in the Itaewon guide**.

### Hannam-daero 20-gil: two One Stars and Orzo

Three MICHELIN entries share **Hannam-daero 20-gil**, the lane uphill from Hannam Station exit 1.

- **Soseoul Hannam** (21-18, basement, One Star) — MICHELIN describes modern Korean cooking that reworks familiar techniques such as seasoned salads, pan-fried and braised dishes. It names kelp chips, a soybean-paste stew (the Korean page says cheonggukjang, a stronger fermented paste), raw fish with gomchwi greens and aged kimchi, and chargrilled webfoot octopus. The English page adds a broad list of traditional Korean liquors.
- **Escondido** (61-7, basement, One Star, new) — according to MICHELIN's English page, the chef learned to cook in Mexico, and the menu centres on **tortillas and mole**; MICHELIN suggests tequila, mezcal or a cocktail alongside. The Korean page would not load when this was written, so the English page is the source.
- **Osteria Orzo** (47, 2F, Selected) — MICHELIN and KTO both name **white ragù** as the signature: house-made fresh pasta in a sauce of minced beef, mascarpone and truffle paste. Spicy tagliatelle and seafood tartare also get a mention. KTO data gives 12:00–22:00 with a 15:00–17:30 break.

The same lane has three KTO-listed restaurants too (see the KTO table below): **Danbi** (21-12), a Korean set-menu restaurant in a converted house; **Gnocchi Bar** (41-4); and the Chinese restaurant **Cheong Hannam** (47-24).

### Daesagwan-ro and Hannam-daero 21-gil: Youhan, Table for Four, Manao

![Pale green curry topped with basil and red chilli in a black bowl, with golden pieces of roti layered on the plate below](/images/hannam-food-2026-09/04.webp)

A generic Thai green curry with roti, not from Youhan or Manao.

- **Youhan** (10-5 Daesagwan-ro 5-gil, Bib Gourmand) — a small Thai bistro from chef Kim Yu-han, who went to culinary school in Thailand. MICHELIN warns that it is **hidden deep in Hannam-dong's alleys and hard to find the first time**. It mentions Thai sausages, seasonal dishes and curries, and natural wine pairings. No Hannam-dong restaurant was among the eight new 2026 Bib Gourmands, so Youhan is a continuing listing.
- **Manao** (2F, 18 Hannam-daero 21-gil, Selected) — Thai set menus served as many small plates, with meat and seafood grilled to order over charcoal. *Manao* is Thai for lime, MICHELIN notes.
- **Table for Four** (5F, 25-12 Daesagwan-ro 31-gil, Selected) — cooking built on produce and seafood from Taean in South Chungcheong, the chef's hometown, where MICHELIN says his family farms. The English page mentions a Burgundy-heavy wine list. KTO data gives 12:00–22:00 daily (break 15:00–18:00) and ₩3,000 valet parking.

**KTO's description of Table for Four says "Banpo-dong, Seocho-gu"**, which contradicts its own Hannam-dong address. The address and hours above come from the address and hours fields of the same record.

### Itaewon-ro 54-gil and 238 Itaewon-ro: Bawi, Borgo Hannam, Kyoyang Siksa

- **Bawi** (3F, 16-9 Itaewon-ro 54-gil) — a modern room with an open kitchen. MICHELIN says the menu lists only main ingredients and the chef plays with foams, espuma, herb oils and live fire; it singles out **sea snail** with scallion foam and seaweed sauce.
- **Borgo Hannam** (3F, 31 Itaewon-ro 54-gil) — *borgo* is Italian for neighbourhood. Chef Stefano Di Salvo makes everything from the morning bread to pasta and desserts, per MICHELIN; the signature is **Caldaro soup**, inspired by a Tuscan fishermen's soup.
- **Kyoyang Siksa** (238 Itaewon-ro) — Sapporo-style barbecue of Australian lamb under ten months old: French rack, ribs and loin, plus soup curry and garlic rice. MICHELIN says there are only about ten bar seats besides a dining room, so **booking is essential**. It is the only one of Hannam's 11 MICHELIN entries in the ₩ band.

### Dokseodang-ro: A L'AISE and Jue

**Dokseodang-ro** runs from Hannam Station towards Oksu Station.

- **A L'AISE** (89 Dokseodang-ro) — French for "at ease". MICHELIN describes courses that bring Korean touches into classic French sauces and lists **reasonable prices** among its strengths; the band is ₩₩₩.
- **Jue** (124-7 Dokseodang-ro) — Cantonese. Set menus are named after the old Chinese noble ranks (duke, marquis, count, viscount, baron). The signature is barbecued pork roasted at high heat; MICHELIN picks cheung fun, abalone shumai and spring rolls from more than 13 kinds of dim sum. KTO data says it is near Oksu Station exit 3 and that **a Chinese tea must be chosen with a set menu**.

**Opening hours from MICHELIN pages are not copied here.** Some pages list hours by day but seem to show only one of the lunch or dinner slots, so check with the restaurant.

### Cafés and brunch near Hangangjin

For a morning or daytime stop near Hangangjin Station on [[line:6]], KTO lists **Little Neck Hannam** (from 10:30) and **Cheeseflo**. KTO describes Little Neck as a brunch place known for eggs Benedict where queues are common; dogs are welcome on the terrace.

Near Hannam Station on [[line:gyeongui]] is **Crate Coffee** (97 Dokseodang-ro), a high-ceilinged two-level café that roasts its own beans and explains its hand-drip coffees, per KTO. Parking is ₩5,000 for the first two hours or ₩5,000 valet, and pets are allowed only outdoors.

**No Hannam-dong bakery could be confirmed from official sources.** KTO's restaurant data had none with a Hannam-dong address, and places that appear only in press coverage were left out.

## Good to know

**Check bookings first.** MICHELIN says Kyoyang Siksa needs a reservation because seating is limited, and KTO advises booking Maison Hannam's terrace a month ahead. Many Seoul restaurants take bookings through apps such as CatchTable.

**Parking is mostly valet.** KTO data lists ₩5,000 valet at Crate Coffee and ₩3,000 at Table for Four. Little Neck has no car park and points drivers to a nearby public lot.

**Many places are tucked down lanes.** MICHELIN says Youhan is hard to find, and KTO notes Hannam-dong Gamjatang sits a little way down an alley off the main road.
