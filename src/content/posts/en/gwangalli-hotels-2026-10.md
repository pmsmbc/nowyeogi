Book an "ocean view" room in Gwangalli (광안리) and what fills the window is not the horizon but the **Gwangan Bridge**. Signiel, Paradise and Park Hyatt are all on the Haeundae side; a hotel comparison site counts **107 places to stay in Gwangan-dong** (data updated 4 October 2026), and nearly all are mid-range hotels, serviced residences, motels and guesthouses. In exchange the whole crescent faces the bridge head-on, so even a mid-range room can have its lights in the window, and the lanes behind the beach road and the raw-fish strip in Millak-dong keep the nights long. Choosing a hotel here is less about the brand and almost entirely about **which line of buildings it stands on**. Prices and hours are as of October 2026.

![Gwangalli Beach at dusk, hotels and shops lined up along Gwanganhaebyeon-ro on the right, with Gwangan Bridge and the Marine City towers across the water on the left](/images/gwangalli-hotels-2026-10/02.webp)

## Line by line

**The beachfront line on Gwanganhaebyeon-ro.** Buildings separated from the sand by a two-lane road. The four hotels below are all at numbers 203 to 229 on this road, within one block. Sea-facing rooms see the bridge; the same hotels **sell their back rooms separately as "city view"**. On Friday and Saturday nights the noise from the beach road comes straight up.

**One block back.** The lanes of **Gwangnam-ro** and **Namcheonbada-ro** run parallel to the beach road, and more than half of the Gwangalli hotels on the comparison site's list are here. The beach is two or three minutes away and rates drop below the front line, but there is no sea view, or at best a sliver between buildings from an upper floor. Since the drone show is watched from the beach anyway, this is the value line if you do not need the bridge from your bed.

**Millak-dong and the Suyeong Station end.** Millak-dong, at the north-eastern end, has **Millak Waterfront Park** and the raw-fish strip, with small hotels and hostels on Gwanganhaebyeon-ro 278beon-gil and 284beon-gil. The park has been an **alcohol-free zone since July 2023**, so the old picnic-mat drinking scene is gone. Further inland around [[line:busan2]] Suyeong and Millak stations it is residential: quiet and cheaper, but 1.3 to 1.4 km from the sand, about a 20-minute walk.

![Gwangan Bridge seen from the sand at Gwangalli on a clear day, with yellow buoys and the white suspension towers beyond the surf](/images/gwangalli-hotels-2026-10/04.webp)

## Which way the bridge is

The Gwangan Bridge starts at **Namcheon-dong** in Suyeong-gu and crosses the bay to Centum City in Haeundae-gu (Wikipedia). The beach is a 1.4 km crescent from the Namcheon end in the south-west to the Millak end in the north-east, and the bridge cuts across its mouth.

- From a sea-facing room in the **middle of the beach**, in front of Gwangan Station, the bridge is **straight ahead**.
- At the **Namcheon end**, near Geumnyeonsan Station, the bridge begins on your left and runs out to sea; the near end is close, so it looks big.
- At the **Millak end** the bridge sits diagonally to your right, with the Marine City towers behind it.

Whether "ocean view" means a room facing the water or a diagonal glimpse past the next building is best read from the room name. Aqua Palace sells **beach**, **no view** and **side** rooms; Homers sells **ocean** and **city**.

## Saturday drone shows and weekend rates

The **Gwangalli M Drone Light Show** runs on the beach every Saturday and is free. From July 2026 it is a year-round production with 1,100 drones, and from 26 September the **first and third Saturdays add a laser show** before and after the drones, while the second and fourth are drones only (Busan city, 22 September 2026). Showtimes are **19:00 and 21:00 from October to February, 20:00 and 22:00 from March to September**, about 12 minutes each. Rain or strong wind cancels it.

Rates follow. The comparison site's average for Gwangan-dong is **₩98,942 (about $74) on a Tuesday and ₩218,541 on a Saturday**, more than double. By month, **November is highest at ₩499,658**, pulled up by Saturday 7 November, when the [Busan Fireworks Festival](/en/posts/busan-fireworks-2026-09/) is held on this beach; February is lowest at ₩74,220. Averages, not quotes, but the pattern is clear.

- **You want the drones from your room**: a sea-facing room on the beachfront line, Saturday night, and accept the price.
- **You will watch from the beach and just need a bed**: a back-lane hotel with a Friday or Sunday check-in.

## Getting there

The metro is one line, [[line:busan2]].

- **Gwangan Station** Exit 5 to the middle of the beach is a **13-minute walk**. The station is about 800 m inland on Suyeong-ro, so a hotel "at Gwangan Station" is not a hotel at the beach.
- **Geumnyeonsan Station** is officially "Geumnyeonsan (Gwangalli Beach)", and Exit 3 is **about 5 minutes** from the Namcheon end of the sand.
- **Suyeong Station** (interchange with [[line:busan3]]) and **Millak Station** are 1.3 to 1.4 km from the north-eastern end, about 20 minutes.
- Haeundae Station is 12 minutes away, Seomyeon about 20.

**From Gimhae International Airport**, take the light rail [[line:gimhaelrt]] to Sasang and change once to [[line:busan2]], which runs straight through to Gwangan; allow about an hour. **Airport Limousine 1** (Haeundae and Gijang direction, ₩9,500, about $7) does not stop in Gwangalli; its nearest stops are **BEXCO and Shinsegae Centum City**, three stops from Gwangan on [[line:busan2]] via Centum City Station.

**From Busan Station**, take [[line:busan1]] to Seomyeon and change to [[line:busan2]]: 30 to 40 minutes including the change.

## Practical notes

- **Read the room name**: ocean or beach versus city, no-view or side. Booking on another guest's ocean-view photos can land you in a back room.
- **Check-in** is mostly 15:00, 16:00 at Hotel 1. On a drone-show night with a 19:00 performance, make sure a late check-in does not clash.
- **Driving?** Ask how the parking works. Beachfront plots are narrow, so tower parking and partner car parks are common.
- **Cards** are the norm at hotels of this class. Some small hotels in the back lanes sell mainly through Korean booking apps, so a listing on an international platform is the easier route for overseas cards.

## Wrapping up

Gwangalli's hotels come in three kinds: **the single row of sea-facing rooms on the beachfront**, **the value hotels in the lanes behind**, and **the quiet streets towards Suyeong Station**. If seeing the bridge from your room is the point, pay the Saturday rate on the front line. If you will watch the drones from the sand and only need somewhere to sleep, take a back-lane room on a Friday or Sunday. Haeundae is 12 minutes away for the day you want the big beach and the five-star lobbies.
