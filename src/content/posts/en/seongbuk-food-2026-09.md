Seongbuk-dong is **a noodle neighbourhood**. And **the cheap food is not up the hill but down at the bottom of it**.

Know those two things and you are set.

![A wooded uphill path inside a temple, timber buildings standing on the slope and red flowers along the verge](/images/seongbuk-guide-2026-09/12.webp)

## The official local dish is noodles

The Seongbuk-gu archive describes **kalguksu, or guksi as it is called here, as the speciality of Seongbuk-dong**. The Seoul Future Heritage certificate even uses the phrase "kalguksu politics", from the days when politicians met in the neighbourhood's noodle houses.

**There is no official basis for naengmyeon.** Some people associate Seongbuk-dong with cold noodles, but no official document connecting the two could be found.

## Seongbuk-gu publishes the shop names itself

This is the most useful find of the research. On a page headed certified shops of Seongbuk-dong, the district office lists **the names, addresses and founding years of 21 shops**. Fourteen of them serve food.

**The criterion is not the cooking.** It is **30 years or more in business, traditional food, and contribution to the area**.

Of the 14 food and confectionery shops, these are the **11 with a founding year on record**, oldest first.

| Shop | Signature dish | Founded |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Napoleon Confectionery | Cakes and pastries | **1968** |
| Seongbuk-dong Dwaeji Galbi | Galbi baekban, bulgogi baekban | **1969** |
| Ssangdari Sikdang | Pork bulbaek | **around 1970** |
| Guksijip | Guksi, suyuk, octopus, jeon | **1973** |
| Seondong Boribap | Barley rice, doenjang jjigae | 1987 |
| Seongbuk-dongjip | Kalguksu, dumplings | 1997 |
| Andong Halmae Cheonggukjang | Cheonggukjang | 1998 |
| Songane Gom Guksu | Gom guksu, seolleongtang | 1998 |
| Suyeon Sanbang | Tea, hangwa | 1998 |
| Gukhwa Jeongwon | Ganjang gejang, set menus | 2005 |
| Myeongmun Makguksu | Bongpyeong buckwheat makguksu | 2017 |

**Four of the 14 are noodle houses**: Seongbuk-dongjip (hand-cut kalguksu), Guksijip (Andong guksi), Songane Gom Guksu and Myeongmun Makguksu (Bongpyeong buckwheat). **Seongbuk-dong as a noodle neighbourhood is borne out by the make-up of the district's own list.**

There are two Korean set-menu restaurants as well. This is how the district introduces Gukhwa Jeongwon.

> the place that first developed and presented a Korean set-menu course in Seongbuk-dong

**Napoleon Confectionery holds three certifications**: a certified shop of Seongbuk-dong, Seoul Future Heritage, and Oraegage as well.

## There is only one good price shop inside Seongbuk-dong

The Ikseon-dong post reported zero certified shops inside the legal-status neighbourhood. In Seongbuk-dong there is **exactly one**.

| Legal-status neighbourhood | Good price shops |
| --- | --- |
| **Seongbuk-dong and Seongbuk-dong 1-ga** | **1** (jjajangmyeon 7,000 won) |
| Dongsomun-dong | 4 |
| Dongseon-dong | 8 |
| Jeongneung-dong | 10 |

**Up the hill in Seongbuk-dong there is almost nothing inside the official schemes; the certifications are all down the hill and over towards Sungshin Women's University.**

### It is cheaper over the wall

Seongbuk-dong adjoins Jongno-gu across the Hanyang City Wall. **Cross at Hyehwamun Gate and it gets cheaper.**

| Shop | Published price | From the station |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cheongnyeon Bapsang Mungan, Slow branch | **Kimchi jjigae 3,000 won** | about 680m |
| Dooly Bunsik | **Doenjang jjigae 4,000 won, bibimbap 3,500 won** | about 650m |
| Hong Sundubu | Hong sundubu 5,500 won | about 1,040m |

**One wall makes the difference in price.**

### This is a floor, not an average

The usual caveat. Only shops **cheaper than the average of the surrounding commercial area** are designated under the good price scheme. So this is **a floor**, not an average.

The Korean set-menu restaurants up the hill in Seongbuk-dong do not charge these prices.

## Can you eat at Gilsangsa Temple

**There is no temple food programme.** The refectory is for monks and practising Buddhists.

**A temple stay is registered.** Seon meditation and the theory-and-meditation programme are 30,000 won each, but **there is no English-only programme.**

There is a teahouse in the grounds, **Gilsang Dawon**, which visitors may use. Its **hours and prices are not published**, though.

## Suyeon Sanbang

The house of the novelist Yi Tae-jun, and by Seongbuk-gu's classification **a traditional teahouse**. It is on the district's certified shop list too, with tea, beombeok, hangwa and injeolmi as its signature items.

That said, **no official page confirming that it is trading in 2026 could be found.** Check before you go.

Both buses leave from the stop named Hansung University Station Exit 6, Samseongyo, and a village bus is 1,200 won.

**Food at the bottom, sights at the top.** Eat before you go up, or eat on the way down.

## Things worth knowing

**Every restaurant and cafe is entirely non-smoking**, and has been since 2015.

**There is no tipping, and the price on the menu is the final amount.**

**Refusing a card payment is illegal**, but older shops may not take cards issued abroad.

**Seongbuk-dong has no official request for quiet.** Ikseon-dong has its four promises; Seongbuk-dong has nothing of the sort. Nearby Jangsu Village does. **The absence of a rule does not mean it is fine to peer in.**

## What could not be verified

**In some cases two official sources gave different prices.** Where the Ministry of the Interior and Safety's published rate conflicted with another body's figure, **only the ministry's rate** has been carried over here.

**Seongbuk-gu does not publish lists of model restaurants, safe restaurants or local speciality restaurants.** That is unlike other districts, and it is why this post rests on the certified shop list and the good price scheme alone.

**The hours and prices at Gilsang Dawon** and **whether Suyeon Sanbang is currently trading** could not be confirmed.

## Wrapping up

- **The official local dish of Seongbuk-dong is noodles.** There is no official basis for naengmyeon.
- **Seongbuk-gu publishes the names and founding years of 21 certified shops.** The criterion is not the cooking but 30 years or more in business.
- **There is only one good price shop inside Seongbuk-dong.** The rest are all at the bottom of the hill.
- **Kalguksu at 5,000 won, janchi guksu at 3,000 won and an americano at 1,500 won** are near the station. But that is a floor.
- **It is cheaper over the wall on the Jongno-gu side.** Kimchi jjigae goes for 3,000 won there.
- **There is no temple food programme at Gilsangsa Temple.** There is a temple stay, at 30,000 won.
- **Exit 5 for food, exit 6 for the hill.**
