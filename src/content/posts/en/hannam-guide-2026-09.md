On 29 September 2026, The Fact reported that **demolition in Hannam New Town's Zone 3 was 99% complete** and construction would start before the end of the year: the first shovel in the ground 23 years after the area was designated a "New Town" in 2003. Nine months earlier, on 26 December 2025, a Seoul city notice had **brought the Hangangjin Station area (Leeum Museum of Art, Blue Square) into the Itaewon Special Tourist Zone**. Hannam-dong is where two sets of records meet: **a gallery street becoming part of a tourist zone, and the slopes to its south being rebuilt from scratch**.

This guide lines those records up against city notices and dated reporting. The story of Itaewon's name, the 1997 tourist zone designation, the 10.29 Memorial Alley, Haebangchon and the Seoul Central Mosque have moved to our [Itaewon guide](/en/posts/itaewon-guide-2026-09/). Sights are in [Hannam-dong things to do](/en/posts/hannam-things-to-do-2026-09/), food in [Hannam-dong food](/en/posts/hannam-food-2026-09/) and hotels in [Hannam-dong hotels](/en/posts/hannam-hotels-2026-09/).

That hillside is **Bogwang-dong**. Despite the name "Hannam New Town", much of the redevelopment land is on slopes like this one. We could not confirm when the photo was taken, but as residents move out and buildings come down, this view is disappearing.

Korean Wikipedia describes Hannam-dong as **"the gateway from Yongsan-gu to the Gangnam side of the river"**.

### Where this guide's Hannam-dong begins and ends

**This blog's boundary**: we split the area by Yongsan-gu's administrative units. **Itaewon 1-dong and 2-dong (legal Itaewon-dong) and Yongsan 2-ga-dong (Haebangchon)** are covered in the [Itaewon guide](/en/posts/itaewon-guide-2026-09/); **the Hannam-dong administrative unit** and the Hannam redevelopment district that runs south of it (including Bogwang-dong and Dongbinggo-dong) are in the [Hannam-dong guide](/en/posts/hannam-guide-2026-09/). There are three exceptions. First, Usadan-gil and the Seoul Central Mosque have legal Hannam-dong addresses according to the Korea Tourism Organization and Wikipedia, but they sit above exit 3 of Itaewon Station, so they go with Itaewon. Second, the War Memorial of Korea has a Yongsan-dong 1-ga address (on the Samgakji side) but is treated as an Itaewon-side sight. Third, the returned Yongsan Park land such as Yongsan Children's Garden, and the former officers' housing Block 5 site (Namsan Korean Forest Garden), which has an Itaewon-dong address, are covered on the Hannam-dong side. Likewise for the Itaewon Special Tourist Zone: the 1997 designation and the Itaewon-ro stretch are in the Itaewon guide, and its December 2025 expansion to the Hangangjin Station area is in the Hannam-dong guide.

## December 2025: the tourist zone reaches Hangangjin

When and why the Itaewon Special Tourist Zone was created is covered in the [Itaewon guide](/en/posts/itaewon-guide-2026-09/). What matters for Hannam-dong is the next step.

Two reports published on 8 January 2026 (Sijung Ilbo and Munhwa News) say the zone **was expanded by a Seoul city notice dated 26 December 2025**.

| Item | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Original extent | About 1.4km from the start of Itaewon-ro to the Hannam-dong community centre, in Itaewon-dong and Hannam-dong |
| Area after expansion | About **390,000㎡** (about 380,000㎡ before, according to one report) |
| Newly included | **The Hangangjin Station area**, Leeum Museum of Art, Blue Square |
| Yongsan-gu's stated goal | From a place people "visit" to a place people "stay" |

In other words, **the Hannam-dong gallery district is now inside the tourist zone**. The two articles describe the original 1.4km stretch slightly differently, so we have not tried to draw the exact new boundary here.

## Hannam New Town: a redevelopment past its target year

### What the city notice says

The formal name is the **Hannam Redevelopment Promotion District** (한남재정비촉진지구). Seoul Notice 2023-154, published in the city gazette on 27 April 2023, opens by listing every notice before it.

- **18 November 2003** - designated a New Town district (Notice 2003-372)
- **19 October 2006** - designated a redevelopment promotion district (2006-357)
- **1 October 2009** - redevelopment plan adopted (2009-387)
- **30 March 2017** - Hannam Zone 1 **released** from redevelopment (2017-112)
- More than ten further amendments follow up to 2023

The overview from the same notice:

| Item | From the notice |
| --- | --- |
| Location | **Bogwang, Hannam, Itaewon and Dongbinggo-dong**, Yongsan-gu |
| Area | **948,390.3㎡** |
| Purpose | Improve living conditions in **the Bogwang-dong area**, dense with old, substandard housing and poor infrastructure |
| Base year / target year | 2007 / **2025** |
| Planned population and homes | 33,012 people / 12,775 households |
| Height | No building anywhere in the district above **90m above sea level** |

The plan's basic direction says it must **not damage the hillside landscape at the foot of Namsan**. That is why the cap is set in metres above sea level rather than in floors, in the same spirit as the Namsan scenic district Wikipedia mentions.

### Zone by zone (as reported in May 2026)

| Zone | Location and area (notice) | Status, May 2026 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Zone 1 | 151 Bogwang-dong area, 58,795㎡ | Released in 2017, now a "retained management" area; a fast-track planning process is under way (Seoul Shinmun) |
| **Zone 2** | **Bogwang-dong** 272-3 area, 114,580.6㎡ | Revised plan approved on 13 May 2026: **1,311 homes** (197 public). Residents moving out in 2026, construction targeted for late 2027 |
| **Zone 3** | **Hannam-dong** 686 area, 386,395.5㎡ | Residents gone, demolition nearly finished. **5,988 homes** in 127 buildings up to 22 floors; sales possibly from late 2026 |
| **Zone 4** | **Bogwang-dong** 360 area, 160,156㎡ | Project implementation approved November 2025: **2,331 homes** in 51 buildings up to 22 floors |
| **Zone 5** | **Dongbinggo-dong** 60 area, 183,707㎡ | Implementation plan recently approved: **2,592 homes** |

The four zones add up to **roughly 12,200 homes**. Seoul Shinmun described the project as **finally picking up speed after 23 years**: it stalled in the 2008 financial crisis and restarted when Zone 3 won approval in 2019.

### What changed by September 2026

Four months later, The Fact (29 September 2026) reported:

| Zone | September 2026 report |
| --- | --- |
| Zone 3 | Demolition began February 2025; **99% complete, construction due to start this year**. Management and disposal plan approved June 2023; move-in planned for 2029. About 6,000 homes |
| Zone 2 | 99% of residents moved out; **construction due in 2027**. Management and disposal plan approved July 2025. 6 floors below to 14 above, 1,311 homes |
| Zone 4 | Members' unit applications in May 2026; general meeting on the disposal plan expected in November. 7 floors below to 20 above, 2,360 homes |
| Zone 5 | Implementation plan approved April 2026; members' applications from October. 5 below to 22 above, 44 buildings, 2,401 homes plus 146 officetel units |
| Zone 1 | Selected in February 2025 as a candidate for Seoul's fast-track housing redevelopment |

The Fact puts the total for all five zones at **about 13,000 homes**. Its unit counts and floor numbers differ slightly from the May reports.

What this means for a visitor: **south of Itaewon-ro, on the slopes of Hannam-dong and Bogwang-dong, you will see hoardings and construction trucks for years to come.** These are not areas to seek out for old-alley atmosphere, and many blocks already stand empty.

## What we got wrong

These are things we believed before researching this, and corrections since.

**One: Hannam New Town is not a Hannam-dong project.** By the notice, **only Zone 3 is in Hannam-dong**. Zones 2 and 4 are in Bogwang-dong and Zone 5 in Dongbinggo-dong. Even the statement of purpose begins with "the Bogwang-dong area".

**Two: there are four zones, not five.** Zone 1 was **released on 30 March 2017** and survives in the notice only as a retained area, though it was picked as a fast-track candidate in February 2025 (The Fact).

**Three: we thought the Leeum side was outside the tourist zone.** Since December 2025, **the Hangangjin Station area is in**. The "Itaewon Special Tourist Zone" now stretches over Hannam-dong's gallery streets.

**Four: the first version of this guide (29 September 2026) covered Itaewon and Hannam-dong together.** It overlapped with our earlier [Itaewon guide](/en/posts/itaewon-guide-2026-09/), so we split the area by administrative unit and moved the Itaewon-side records (the name, the 1997 designation, events since 2022, Haebangchon, the mosque) there. The mosque's dress guidance we had here also differed from the Korea Muslim Federation's wording, and was corrected when it moved.

## The returned land to the west: Yongsan Park

Heading west from Hannam-dong along Itaewon-ro, you used to reach **the wall of the US Army's Yongsan Garrison**. Part of the returned land has been opened on a temporary basis as "Yongsan Children's Garden", and two things have changed.

- **30 December 2025** - the Ministry of Land, Infrastructure and Transport dropped advance booking and ID checks, **opening it to anyone who walks in**. Hours: Tuesday to Sunday 9am-6pm, Saturday until 9pm, **closed Mondays**. More than 1.8 million people had visited since the temporary opening. The ministry also said it would rename the park in the first half of 2026 after public consultation.
- **14 August 2026** - the vice minister said in a radio interview that the government **would discuss using the garden site for housing with the Seoul city government**, which Seoul Shinmun reported the city opposes. The same article puts Yongsan Park as a whole at about 3.3 million㎡ and the garden at about 297,000㎡.

Check the ministry's Yongsan Park office notices before you go. Opening hours for other spaces on the returned land, such as the Namsan Korean Forest Garden, are in [Hannam-dong things to do](/en/posts/hannam-things-to-do-2026-09/).

## How to do it

1. **Morning** - the gallery street from [[line:6]] Hangangjin. Check opening hours and closing days in [Hannam-dong things to do](/en/posts/hannam-things-to-do-2026-09/).
2. **Lunch** - the side streets either side of Hannam-daero. How to choose is in [Hannam-dong food](/en/posts/hannam-food-2026-09/).
3. **Afternoon** - south along Hannam-daero to [[line:gyeongui]] Hannam Station and the river. The redevelopment zones are visible only over hoardings; do not enter building sites.
4. **If you have time** - west towards Itaewon Station; see the [Itaewon guide](/en/posts/itaewon-guide-2026-09/). Yongsan Children's Garden is closed on Mondays.

## Where to stay

The expanded tourist zone now ties Itaewon and Hangangjin stations together, but **the character of the hotels still changes station by station**. Around Itaewon Station it is busy late into the night; the Hangangjin side is quieter and generally pricier. The Hangangjin and Hannam side is compared in [Hannam-dong hotels](/en/posts/hannam-hotels-2026-09/), and the Itaewon Station and Haebangchon side in [Itaewon and Haebangchon hotels](/en/posts/itaewon-hotels-2026-09/). If a property is close to a redevelopment zone, check the map for possible construction noise.

## Who this suits

**Good fit:**

- You want museums and a concert hall at the centre of your day - the Hangangjin side is now inside the zone
- You are curious about **the stories behind official records**: a city rebuilding a whole neighbourhood through notices and approvals
- You want a walk that ends at the Han River and Hannam Bridge

**Not such a good fit:**

- If old lanes and traditional housing are the goal, the Hannam and Bogwang slopes are a building site. Try the [Bukchon guide](/en/posts/bukchon-guide-2026-09/) or the [Yeonnam-dong and Mangwon-dong guide](/en/posts/yeonnam-guide-2026-09/)
- If world food and evenings are the point, the [Itaewon guide](/en/posts/itaewon-guide-2026-09/) is the better fit

## Summary

- **Boundary**: the Hannam-dong administrative unit, the Hannam redevelopment district to its south (including Bogwang-dong and Dongbinggo-dong) and the Yongsan Park returned land. Itaewon 1-dong, 2-dong and Haebangchon are in the [Itaewon guide](/en/posts/itaewon-guide-2026-09/).
- **Hannam-dong**: the gateway to Gangnam, split by Hannam-daero, much of it in the Namsan scenic district; 2.99 km² (2018).
- On **26 December 2025** the Itaewon Special Tourist Zone grew to include **the Hangangjin area (Leeum, Blue Square)**, about 390,000㎡.
- **Hannam New Town (Hannam Redevelopment Promotion District): designated 2003, 948,390.3㎡, target year 2025.** Only Zone 3 is in Hannam-dong; Zones 2 and 4 are in Bogwang-dong, Zone 5 in Dongbinggo-dong.
- **September 2026**: Zone 3 demolition 99% done, construction due this year; Zone 2 due in 2027. About 13,000 homes across five zones (The Fact).
- **Yongsan Children's Garden** has been open without reservations since 30 December 2025; closed Mondays.

In one line: **while Hannam-dong's gallery street was being brought into the tourist zone, the slopes to its south were being rebuilt wholesale, past their own deadline.**
