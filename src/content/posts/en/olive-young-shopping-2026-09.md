If you came to Korea with a shopping list of skincare, the plan is short: go to Olive Young. It is the drugstore-style chain where most Korean beauty brands sit on the same shelves, testers open, ranked by what sold best last week — so you can skip the tour of individual brand boutiques. The bigger branches also take the 10% VAT off at the register if you have your passport. Details below reflect September 2026.

## What Olive Young is

![Lit Olive Young storefront and sign at night](/images/olive-young-shopping-2026-09/01.webp)

Olive Young is a health-and-beauty chain run by CJ, and it is close to unavoidable in a Korean city — you will pass one near almost any subway station. Stock is a mix of Korean brands, small indie labels and a handful of imports.

The scale is easier to grasp from the company's own figures. In a February 2025 release, Olive Young reported **9.42 million purchases by foreign shoppers from 189 nationalities** over a year, spread across **1,264 stores** — roughly 92% of its branches. Foreign customers are not just in the tourist districts; they are in neighbourhood shops too.

## The flagship "Town" stores

Any branch sells much the same products, but the large **Town** stores are the ones set up for foreign shoppers: multiple floors, multilingual staff, and a reliable tax refund process.

**Myeongdong Global Town** is at 53 Myeongdong-gil and opens **daily 10:00–22:30**. It is close to Myeongdong Station ([[line:4]]) Exit 8 and Euljiro 1-ga Station ([[line:2]]) Exit 5. Two floors of about 1,157㎡ hold skincare, makeup, haircare, body care, supplements and fragrance, with the tax refund desk on the second floor. WeChat Pay and Alipay are accepted, and gift wrapping is available for a fee. Note that the official site says there is **no luggage storage and no restroom** inside the store.

**Hongdae Town** opened in July 2024, on the main road running from Hongik Univ. Station ([[line:2]]) Exit 8 toward the pedestrian shopping street. Half of the first floor is a collaboration pop-up zone that changes monthly — brand tie-ins with films, games and characters — while colour cosmetics and premium brands sit on the second floor and skincare on the third. Staff carry handheld translators covering 16 languages.

**Gangnam Town** is at 429 Gangnam-daero, about four minutes on foot from Gangnam Station Exit 10, served by [[line:2]] and [[line:sinbundang]]. Its draw is the K-Beauty Now zone and pop-up area, both refreshed monthly.

## The tax refund, explained

![Checkout queue at Olive Young Myeongdong with multilingual signs asking shoppers to prepare a physical passport](/images/olive-young-shopping-2026-09/02.webp)

The signs above the checkout counters in the photo say the important part: prepare your physical passport.

- Spend **₩15,000 (about $11) or more on a single receipt** and the roughly 10% VAT becomes refundable.
- Show your **physical passport** at the register and the tax comes off on the spot — you pay the reduced price immediately. A photo or photocopy is not accepted, and there is no way to claim it back after the transaction is complete.
- Instant refunds are capped: **under ₩1,000,000 (about $750) per transaction** and **₩5,000,000 (about $3,750) total** during your stay. Those limits doubled from ₩500,000 and ₩2,500,000 in January 2024.
- You must **leave Korea within three months** of the purchase date.

If you go over the limits, or shop at a smaller branch that does not offer instant refunds, keep the receipts and tax-free forms and process them at the airport before check-in. Incheon has both kiosks and staffed counters. Pack those items where you can pull them out, in case you are asked to show them.

## What foreign shoppers actually buy

Baskets differ by country, according to the company's own breakdown. For American customers, **sun care accounts for half of the top ten sellers** — Korean sunscreens have a reputation abroad for lighter textures at low prices. Shoppers from China, Japan and Taiwan buy sheet masks and toners alongside serums, ampoules and essences. The company credits the "glass skin" trend, the Korean ideal of a clear, luminous complexion built through layered hydration rather than coverage.

Inside the store, the ranking displays are the shortcut. Shelves near the entrance are ordered by current sales and updated frequently, so if you have no specific brand in mind, **buying off the ranking shelf is the fastest way to choose**.

## When things go on sale

- **Olive Young Sale** — the big one, four times a year as the seasons turn (generally March, June, September and December), running about a week.
- **Olive Young Day** — a smaller monthly sale, usually the **25th to the 27th**.
- **1+1 and 2+1 deals** run constantly. Watch the shelf tags: often only one specific size of a product is included.

Exact dates are usually announced shortly before each event, so if your trip overlaps with one, it is worth saving the big purchases for those days.

## Practical notes

- **Carry your passport whenever you shop.** Nothing else on this list matters as much.
- Hours vary by branch. Town stores generally run past 22:00; neighbourhood branches close earlier.
- Not every small store handles instant refunds. If the refund matters, go to a Town or another large branch.
- Subway station lockers are the answer for bags — Myeongdong Town has no storage of its own.
- Buying a lot of liquids means carry-on limits apply, so leave room in checked baggage.

## Wrapping up

One large store, one visit, passport in hand — that is the whole strategy, and it beats walking between brand boutiques. Prices are fixed, so there is no haggling to worry about either.
