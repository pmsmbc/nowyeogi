Start with the fact. **There is nowhere to sleep in Seongbuk-dong.**

The legal-status neighbourhood of Seongbuk-dong has **zero registered lodging businesses and zero rooms**. There are only three records of closed businesses. **Tourist accommodation businesses: zero.** **Hanok stays: zero.**

It is the most extreme of the five neighbourhoods this blog has covered.

![The main hall of Simujang, a name board hung beneath the tiled roof and a red brick annexe standing alongside](/images/seongbuk-things-to-do-2026-09/01.webp)

## The five neighbourhoods compared

| Neighbourhood | Registered lodging | Hanok stays |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Euljiro (Jung-gu) | 485 businesses, 28,340 rooms | 0 |
| Ikseon-dong | 13 businesses, 675 rooms | 6 (within 500m of Jongno 3-ga) |
| Seochon | 2 businesses, 39 rooms | **33** |
| Seongsu-dong | 21 businesses, 299 rooms | 0 |
| **Seongbuk-dong** | **0** | **0** |

Seochon had no hotels but more hanok than anywhere in Seoul. **Seongbuk-dong has neither.**

## Seongbuk-gu as a whole does have some

Do not be misled by the name of the neighbourhood. **Seongbuk-gu as a whole has 54 lodging businesses with 1,283 rooms.** There are four tourist hotels with 327 rooms as well.

But **almost all of it is over by Sungshin Women's University and Dongseon-dong**. That is a different neighbourhood from the Seongbuk-dong hill.

**Seoul Stay lists seven hanok stays in Seongbuk-gu** too, and **by street address every one of them is around Hansung University or Sungshin Women's University**, with **not one up the Seongbuk-dong hill**. Four of the seven are by Hansung University Station and three by Sungshin Women's University Station.

The closest to the hill is a hanok at **76-11 Seongbuk-ro**, and even that is advertised as **a ten-minute walk from Hansung University Station**.

Seven out of Seoul's 133 hanok stays makes Seongbuk-gu **a fifth of Seochon's 33**.

## So where do you stay

There are three options.

**One: around Hansung University Station or Sungshin Women's University.** It is the entrance to Seongbuk-dong, so the village bus takes you straight up. This is where the accommodation actually is.

**Two: Seochon or Bukchon, if you want to sleep in a hanok.** The seven hanok in Seongbuk-gu are all at the bottom of the hill, and the real choice is in Jongno-gu. It is set out in [the Seochon accommodation post](/en/posts/seochon-hotels-2026-09/).

**Three: treat Seongbuk-dong as a daytime trip.** Honestly, this is the most practical answer. **Half a day covers it.**

## If you do pick a hanok stay

You may well take one of the seven in Seongbuk-gu, so here are the essentials again. The detail is in [the Ikseon-dong accommodation post](/en/posts/ikseon-hotels-2026-09/).

**What the law does not require**

- **A private bathroom is not compulsory.** A shared toilet is legal
- **Nor is there any rule that you have to sleep on the floor.** Some hanok have beds

**What the law does require**

- **At least one fire extinguisher**, and **a standalone smoke alarm** in every room
- **A carbon monoxide alarm** as well, if heating is individual
- Total floor area of the sleeping space **under 230 square metres**
- **A manager on duty during business hours**, and **the tariff posted and kept to**

**There is no 24-hour front desk.** If you are arriving late, contact them in advance.

### Do not mix the two figures

**The seven hanok stays are not included in the 54 lodging businesses above.**

The hanok-stay business is **registered under the Tourism Promotion Act**, while the lodging statistics cover only businesses **notified under the Public Health Control Act**. The governing laws are different.

## There is a direct bus from the airport

This is the unexpected advantage.

**The airport limousine [[bus:6011]] stops at Hansung University.** It is the same route that stops at Gyeongbokgung Palace, Anguk and Changdeokgung Palace.

**It runs from Incheon Airport to the foot of Seongbuk-dong with no change.**

Coming **from the airport**:

| Departing | First bus | Last bus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Incheon Airport **Terminal 1** | 05:44 | 23:14 |
| Incheon Airport **Terminal 2** | 05:24 | 22:54 |

You get off at **the median bus lane stop in front of the Elim Pharmacy** (08010). The fare is 17,000 won for an adult and 12,000 won for a child.

Going **to the airport**, you board at **the median bus lane stop in front of exit 7** (08009). **First bus 04:18, last bus 20:33.**

That last bus time is worth pinning down. **The 20:48 given in the Seochon post is the time at Gyeongbokgung Station.** The same bus **passes Hansung University at 20:33 and reaches Gyeongbokgung Station at 20:48**. **From Seongbuk-dong you need to work to 20:33.**

**Of the 19 routes in the 6000 series, 6011 is the only one that goes through Seongbuk-gu.** **There is no limousine from Gimpo Airport.**

By rail, take [[line:arex]] to Seoul Station and change to [[line:4]]. **Seven stops from Seoul Station.** The station number for Hansung University is **419**. 420 is Hyehwa, so do not confuse the two.

## Is Seongbuk-dong a good place to stay

**In its favour:**

- **The direct limousine from Incheon Airport** stops here
- Hansung University Station is on [[line:4]], so the city centre is close
- **It is quiet.** It is residential, and there is little noise at night

**Against it:**

- **There are zero places to stay inside Seongbuk-dong.** You have to book at the bottom of the hill
- **It is steep.** This is not a neighbourhood to drag luggage up
- **Little is open late**
- A good many of the sights **close on Mondays**, and the Kansong Art Museum opens only during exhibitions

## What could not be verified

**There is no official source for room rates in Seongbuk-gu.** That is why no figures appear here.

**Complete data on hanok-stay businesses could not be obtained.** The Seoul Stay listing stands in for it.

**The legal-status neighbourhood of the seven hanok stays could not be established.** Seoul Stay does not record legal-status neighbourhoods, so their locations were judged from street addresses and the nearest station each one advertises.

**The arrival time of the city-bound 6011 at Hansung University** is not published by the airport limousine site. Only the location of the drop-off point is given.

## Wrapping up

- **Registered lodging inside the legal-status neighbourhood of Seongbuk-dong is zero businesses and zero rooms.** Hanok stays are zero as well.
- **Seongbuk-gu as a whole has 54 businesses with 1,283 rooms**, all over by Sungshin Women's University and Dongseon-dong.
- **The seven hanok stays in Seongbuk-gu are at the bottom of the hill** too.
- **If you want to sleep in a hanok, go to Seochon or Bukchon.**
- **Airport limousine 6011 stops at Hansung University.** It is direct from Incheon Airport.
- **The last bus to the airport is 20:33 at Hansung University.** The 20:48 in the Seochon post is the Gyeongbokgung Station time.
- **Half a day in Seongbuk-dong, in daylight**, is the practical way to do it.
