Search for a hotel in Euljiro (을지로) and most of what comes up has "Myeongdong" or "Dongdaemun" in its name. Counting the 20 legal neighbourhoods (dong) around Euljiro in Seoul's own licensing data gives a clearer picture:

- **62 registered tourist lodgings with 4,668 rooms**: 20 hotels and 42 hostels
- The rooms sit **at the two ends**. The Euljiro 1-ga Station end (Euljiro 1–2-ga, Jeo-dong, Jangkyo-dong) has 1,644 rooms and the Dongdaemun end (Euljiro 5-ga, Ojang-dong, Ssangnim-dong) has 1,876, while **the middle, Euljiro 3–4-ga around Sewoon Sangga, has just 431**
- **40 of the 42 hostels were registered in 2025–2026**, and **Jugyo-dong (11 hostels) is joint sixth among all Seoul neighbourhoods**
- Zoom out to the whole district and **Jung-gu holds 33% of Seoul's tourist-lodging rooms and 25% of its general lodging rooms**

In short: the big hotels are **near Euljiro 1-ga Station and the Cheonggyecheon stream (Lotte City, Four Points, President)**, **north of Chungmuro Station (Ninetree Myeongdong II, Hotel PJ)** and **at the Dongdaemun end in Euljiro 5-ga and Ojang-dong (Mercure, Travelodge, Skypark)**. In between, among the printing and lighting workshops, are small hostels opened since 2025 and one large serviced-residence block next to Sewoon. To compare Seoul neighbourhoods side by side, see [where to stay in Seoul](/en/posts/seoul-where-to-stay-2026-09/) first.

## How these were counted

From Seoul Open Data Plaza we downloaded four licensing datasets (tourist lodging, general lodging, foreign-tourist urban homestays, and hanok stays) and kept only places marked "open" **whose legal address is in one of the 20 dong below**. Tourist and general lodging data are as of 24 September 2026, homestays 27 September, hanok stays 23 September. This is the same data and method as our [Myeongdong](/en/posts/myeongdong-hotels-2026-09/) and [Dongdaemun](/en/posts/dongdaemun-hotels-2026-10/) hotel guides.

### Jung-gu as a whole: a third of Seoul's rooms, or a quarter

Jung-gu, the district Euljiro belongs to, has more lodging than any other district in Seoul. **How big its share is depends on which dataset you count.**

| Dataset (law) | All Seoul | Jung-gu | Jung-gu share | Next largest |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Tourist lodging (Tourism Promotion Act) | 814 places · 56,119 rooms | 216 · **18,467 rooms** | **32.9%** | Jongno-gu 5,043 (9.0%) |
| General lodging (Public Health Control Act) | 2,792 places · 113,704 rooms | 486 · **28,342 rooms** | **24.9%** | Gangnam-gu 11,758 (10.3%) |

- **Tourist lodging** covers tourist hotels and hostels registered under the Tourism Promotion Act. **General lodging** covers inns, business hotels and serviced residences, plus tourist hotels, reported under the Public Health Control Act. Many hotels appear in both, so the two totals must not be added together
- In the general lodging data, Jung-gu has **125 tourist hotels (17,966 rooms), 128 serviced residences (4,240), 119 inns (2,290) and 83 business hotels (3,412)**. By number of businesses, serviced residences come first
- Jung-gu has **137 hostels** in the tourist lodging data, **105 of them registered in 2025–2026**
- Euljiro's 4,668 rooms are **about 25% of Jung-gu's tourist-lodging rooms and about 8% of Seoul's**. **Euljiro 5-ga ranks ninth among all Seoul neighbourhoods by rooms (1,048)**

### Jung-gu's 384 homestays

A foreign-tourist urban homestay (외국인관광 도시민박업) is defined in the Tourism Promotion Act enforcement decree as **a resident hosting foreign tourists in the home they live in**, so **Korean nationals generally cannot book one.** Our [Yeonnam-dong and Mangwon-dong guide](/en/posts/yeonnam-hotels-2026-09/) explains the rules.

Seoul has 7,365 open homestays in this data, and **Jung-gu has 384, seventh of 25 districts** (Mapo-gu leads with 2,003). Inside Jung-gu they cluster in **Namsan-dong 2-ga (72), Sindang-dong (67), Gwanghui-dong 2-ga (38), Hoehyeon-dong 1-ga (28), Jangchung-dong 2-ga (27) and Hoehyeon-dong 2-ga (24)**, mostly the residential streets below Namsan and around Sindang. The Myeongdong and Dongdaemun guides cover those in detail.

**The 20 Euljiro dong have only 8.** Four are in Sallim-dong, and oddly **three are flats inside Daerim Sangga and one inside Sewoon Cheonggye Sangga**, the old arcade blocks. The rest are two in Jugyo-dong and two in Ssangnim-dong. In Euljiro, hostels are far more common than homestays.

Jung-gu has just **one registered hanok stay, Jungnimjae in Jungnim-dong (2022)**. Of Seoul's 438, 358 are in Jongno-gu, so for a night in a hanok look at our [Jongno guide](/en/posts/jongno-hotels-2026-09/).

### Not tourist hotels

Line Hotel Myeongdong (92 rooms), Calista (two entries, 24 and 17 rooms), Hotel Drip & Drop (15) and Ewha Hotel in Jeo-dong 1-ga (38) appear **only in the general lodging data**. They are "hotels" by name but are registered as **business hotels under the Public Health Control Act**, not as tourist hotels. The category says nothing certain about the facilities, so check booking-site photos and recent reviews.

## Euljiro 3–4-ga and Sewoon: tiny hostels and a serviced-residence block

Between Euljiro 3-ga and Euljiro 4-ga stations, around the Sewoon and Daerim arcades and the printing and lighting alleys, seven dong hold 431 tourist-lodging rooms, **295 of them in one place: Hotel Kukdo**.

- **Hotel Kukdo** (164 Eulji-ro, registered 2007) gives its name to the 6015 airport bus stop "Kukdo Hotel (Euljiro 4-ga)", right outside the hotel at Euljiro 4-ga Station Exit 10
- There are **24 hostels**: 11 in Jugyo-dong, 7 in Euljiro 3-ga, 3 in Euljiro 4-ga, 2 in Sallim-dong and 1 in Bangsan-dong. Apart from 2U Hostel (2015), **all were registered in 2025–2026**
- Most of those with a room count have **1 to 10 rooms**. The larger ones are 2U Hostel (Jugyo-dong, 20), E4 Elly Stay (Sallim-dong, 14) and Elim Hostel (Jugyo-dong, 12); Roaster Block in Euljiro 3-ga has a single room
- Most of these hostels also have **a general-lodging filing at the same address**: 38 of the 42 hostels in the 20 dong

The biggest place to sleep between Euljiro 3-ga and 4-ga is not a tourist hotel but a **serviced residence (생활숙박업) in the general lodging data**.

The area also still lists **inns licensed in the 1960s** as open, such as Hwaseong Yeogwan and Gwangsinjang Yeogwan in Sallim-dong (1960) and Okcheon Yeogwan in Ipjeong-dong (1965).

### Euljiro 3-ga at night

According to the Korea Tourism Organization, the **Nogari Alley near Euljiro 3-ga Station Exit 4** fills with plastic tables and drinkers every evening. It is said to have started with a first beer hall in 1980 that served print workers coming off night shifts. Many of the Euljiro 3-ga hostels are close to alleys like this, so if you want an early night, search recent reviews for mentions of noise before booking. No block-by-block noise data could be found. The alleys themselves are covered in [things to do in Euljiro](/en/posts/euljiro-things-to-do-2026-09/) and [Euljiro food](/en/posts/euljiro-food-2026-09/).

Around Mareunnae-ro, between Euljiro 3-ga and Chungmuro stations, are Cho-dong and Inhyeon-dong.

- **Hotel PJ** names the airport bus stop where both 6015 ("PJ Hotel (Chungmuro Station)") and 6001 ("Hotel PJ (Chungmuro Station)") stop, **across from Daehan Cinema at Chungmuro Station Exit 8** (02679). The general lodging data lists it with 247 rooms, not 272
- Hostels: four in Cho-dong (High Stay Euljiro Hostel, 15 rooms, and others on Mareunnae-ro 2-gil) and two in Inhyeon-dong 1-ga, all registered since November 2025

### Five serviced-residence licences in one building

At **24 Chungmu-ro (Namsan Central View Suites)** in Inhyeon-dong 1-ga, **five separate serviced-residence licences totalling 304 rooms** were filed in 2021–2022: Central View Suites (113), Swim-In Seoul (66), Urban Stay Myeongdong (54), Comocasa (39) and The Share Seoul (32). It looks like several operators each running units in one building, so the operator name on your booking may differ. Nearby, Hostel K-Pop at 53 Mareunnae-ro is, despite its name, a **business hotel** (29 rooms).

## Getting to the airport

Euljiro is **where two airport bus routes split**. The western part, Euljiro 1–4-ga, is served by [[bus:6015]]; the eastern part, Euljiro 5-ga and Ojang-dong, by [[bus:6001]]; and at Chungmuro Station both use the same stop. Both cost **₩17,000 for adults (about $13) and ₩12,000 for children**, and the operator's notice says **first come, first seated, no online booking, pay by transit card or cash**.

The **City Airport Terminal** is on basement level 2 of the AREX side of Seoul Station. According to AREX's official page:

- **It is only for passengers holding an AREX Express train ticket.** You scan the ticket's QR code at the terminal gate
- You can **check in, drop bags and clear immigration** in advance, **for international flights leaving Incheon the same day**
- **Check-in runs 05:20–19:00 (last entry 18:50); immigration 05:30–19:00.** Check-in closes 3 hours before departure for T1 flights and 3 hours 20 minutes before for T2 flights
- The page shows logos for **Korean Air, Asiana, Jin Air, Air Busan and Air Seoul (T2) and Jeju Air, T'way and Eastar Jet (T1)**. But **eligible routes differ by airline**: some exclude **Americas routes (including Guam and Saipan)**, and codeshares only qualify when operated by that airline. The page itself says to **check with your airline** first
- You cannot buy tickets there or check sports equipment, instruments or oversized bags. If you clear immigration there, you use a **dedicated departure lane** at Incheon

## Which to choose

- **Big hotel next to an airport bus stop** — Lotte City, Four Points or Travelodge Myeongdong Euljiro (6015 "Euljiro 2-ga" stop), Hotel Kukdo (6015 "Kukdo Hotel"), Baiton Hotel (where 6001 starts)
- **Long-established hotels** — President Hotel (1969) in Euljiro 1-ga, Hotel PJ (1970) in Inhyeon-dong 2-ga. The data only shows how old the registration is; check the official site and recent reviews for renovation
- **Close to Dongdaemun shopping** — Mercure, Travelodge Dongdaemun, Ninetree Dongdaemun, Ramada and Skypark Dongdaemun 1 in Euljiro 5-ga and Ojang-dong
- **Budget, solo** — hostels in Jugyo-dong and Euljiro 3-ga. 40 of 42 opened in 2025 or later, so reviews may be thin, and many have 1–10 rooms
- **Homestay or hanok** — Euljiro has 8 homestays and no hanok. For homestays look at Namsan-dong 2-ga or Sindang-dong; for hanok, Jongno-gu
- **Late-evening flight** — the last airport buses leave around 20:00, so plan on [[line:2]] or [[line:4]] to Seoul Station and AREX. If you take the Express, ask your airline whether you can check in early at the City Airport Terminal
- **Hotels with "Myeongdong" or "Dongdaemun" in the name** — check whether the address is Jangkyo-dong, Jeo-dong, Cho-dong, Ipjeong-dong or Euljiro 5-ga

## Summary

- The 20 Euljiro dong have **62 tourist lodgings with 4,668 rooms**: 20 hotels and 42 hostels, about 25% of Jung-gu's tourist-lodging rooms.
- The biggest hotels are **Lotte City (435), Ninetree Myeongdong II (408), Four Points (375), Mercure (336), President (303), Hotel Kukdo (295) and Hotel PJ (272)**.
- Rooms cluster at **the Euljiro 1-ga end (1,644) and the Dongdaemun end (1,876)**; Euljiro 3–4-ga has 431, plus a 731-room serviced residence next to Sewoon.
- Jung-gu holds **32.9% of Seoul's tourist-lodging rooms and 24.9% of its general lodging rooms**. It has 384 homestays, 8 of them in Euljiro.
- **40 of the 42 hostels were registered from 2025**, and Jugyo-dong is joint sixth in Seoul.
- Airport buses: **6015 for Euljiro 1–4-ga (last bus 19:42–19:55)**, **6001 for Euljiro 5-ga and Ojang-dong (last 19:50–19:52)**; Myeongdong Station has one more 6015 at 20:03.
- The Seoul Station City Airport Terminal is **for AREX Express passengers only**, and route rules vary by airline.
