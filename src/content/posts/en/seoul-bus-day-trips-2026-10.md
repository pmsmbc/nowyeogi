If you want to see Seoraksan or Naejangsan in autumn color without renting a car or booking a hotel, the easiest tool in Korea is the package bus day trip. The pattern is simple: **gather near Gwanghwamun or City Hall Station around 7am**, come back to the same spot between 7pm and 9pm, and pay roughly ₩20,000-40,000 (about $15-30). This guide is based on the product pages of four Korean agencies (Hanatour, Yellow Balloon, Modetour and Verygoodtour) and on English-language tour listings, all read on October 27, 2026. Prices and times are as of that date, so check the listing before you pay.

One honest caveat up front: the Korean agencies sell through Korean-language websites and apps, and the tours themselves are run in Korean. They are great value if you travel with a Korean-speaking friend or host. If you don't, skip to the English-language tours section, which uses different pickup points and costs more but needs no Korean at all.

## Where "departs from Seoul City Hall" actually is

Korean listings often say "Seoul City Hall departure", but the actual meeting spots are two different places near the station.

- **Donghwa Duty Free at the Gwanghwamun intersection**: the shared spot for Hanatour, Yellow Balloon and Modetour. It is right outside [[line:5]] Gwanghwamun Station Exit 6, or a 300m walk from [[line:1]] City Hall Station Exit 3. All three product pages ask you to be there 10 minutes before departure.
- **City Hall Station Exit 11, in front of the Yeonho Building**: Verygoodtour's meeting point on [[line:2]]. The group meets at 07:00, staff count heads, then everyone boards.

Departure time depends on how far the bus has to go. Modetour's Daejeon Jangtaesan trip leaves Gwanghwamun at 07:30, the Gangneung trips (Yellow Balloon and Hanatour) at 07:00, and Modetour's trip to Purple Island in Sinan, at the far southwest tip of the country, at 06:30. Yellow Balloon's Gangneung page also warns that on the first day of a holiday weekend the bus leaves 30 minutes earlier (06:30 at Gwanghwamun, 07:00 at Jamsil) to beat the traffic.

Most buses make one or two more stops after Gwanghwamun. The Hanatour and Yellow Balloon Gangneung trips call at **Jamsil Station, Lotte Mart main entrance** at 07:30, 100m from [[line:2]] Jamsil Station Exit 4. Modetour stops at **the village-bus stop in front of Seocho-gu Office at Yangjae Station**, 100m from [[line:3]] Yangjae Station Exit 12, and then at **the Jukjeon stop on the Gyeongbu Expressway**. The catch is the return: both Gangneung pages state that Jamsil passengers are **dropped at Gangbyeon Station, not Jamsil**, on the way back. Arrival back in Seoul is 19:00 for Daejeon, 20:30 for Gangneung and 21:30 for Hampyeong.

Meals are usually excluded, but a few products build one in, such as Yellow Balloon's Gurye trips (mountain-vegetable or tofu set meals) and its Yanggu trip with lunch.

## What the fare covers, and the subsidized "support" fares

A standard day-trip fare covers **the round-trip bus, a tour leader or guide, and vehicle insurance**. Admission varies by product. Modetour's Jangtaesan trip includes transport, guide and vehicle insurance only, with lunch and travel insurance excluded. Verygoodtour's Hampyeong trip included the bus, admission, a tour leader and domestic travel insurance up to ₩50 million, and left out only meals. Budget an extra ₩10,000-20,000 for lunch plus any admission the listing excludes.

What pulls prices down is the **local-government subsidy** (지자체 지원). An August 2026 article in Hi US Korea explained the mechanism: a city or county pays part of the travel cost and the agency sells the package at the reduced price. It cited Mungyeong trips falling from ₩34,900 to ₩17,450 and Yeongju trips from ₩21,900 to ₩10,950. On today's lists, Hanatour shows Hwacheon at ₩14,900 (about $11), Sangju ₩19,900, Gimcheon ₩21,000, Mungyeong ₩22,900, Asan and Yesan ₩23,900, and Jecheon ₩25,900, and its promotional page lists Inje Birch Forest at ₩32,200 instead of ₩53,900 and Yanggu at ₩24,600 instead of ₩45,900. Modetour's ₩20,900 Daejeon trip is the same kind of deal. The article's two warnings are worth repeating: a "from" price does not apply to every date, and once the subsidy budget or the seats run out, the price changes or the product disappears.

For context, the same article quoted preliminary first-quarter 2026 national travel survey figures: about 64% of 85 million domestic trips were day trips, and the average spend on a day trip last year was ₩69,000 (about $52). A ₩30,000-something bus fare plus lunch and admission lands right there.

## Autumn foliage routes: Seoraksan, Naejangsan, Danyang, Jangtaesan

From late October to mid-November, foliage trips sit at the top of every list. These are the Seoul-departure routes on sale now.

- **Seoraksan**: Modetour's "Seoraksan foliage trekking day trip" from ₩37,900. The English tour operator runs its Seoraksan departure only from October 12 to 31.
- **Naejangsan + Jeonju Hanok Village**: Hanatour's Jeongeup trip from ₩39,900, Modetour's Jeongeup-Jeonju trip from ₩39,900. The English Naejangsan tour runs October 28 to November 27, which neatly shows that Naejangsan's small "baby maples" turn two to three weeks after Seoraksan.
- **Baegyangsa Temple's baby maples**: Hanatour's Jangseong trip from ₩39,900. Modetour's train version to Baegyangsa costs from ₩124,900.
- **Danyang**: Hanatour's Danyang trip from ₩39,900, covering Guinsa Temple, the Mancheonha Skywalk, the Danyang River cliff walkway and Dodamsambong rocks in one day.
- **Daejeon Jangtaesan Recreational Forest**: Modetour's subsidized trip at ₩20,900. A walk through the metasequoia forest, lunch on your own at Jungang Market, then Uam Historic Park and the Busodamak cliffs on Daecheong Lake.
- **Inje Birch Forest**: Hanatour's Yangyang-Sokcho-Inje trip from ₩37,000 (birch forest, Naksansa Temple, Sokcho Jungang Market), or the subsidized version from ₩32,200.

## Before you book

**Minimum group size** is the biggest variable with Korean agencies. Hanatour's Gangneung trip needs 25 adults, Modetour's Jangtaesan trip 25, Verygoodtour's Hampyeong trip 25. If the group falls short, the agency calls or texts two or three days before departure and refunds everything. Yellow Balloon's terms promise notice at least 24 hours before a day trip. If you are taking a day off work for it, pick a Saturday departure or a product flagged "departure confirmed".

Cancellation fees differ by company and even by product.

| Company and product | Full refund | After that |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hanatour Gangneung day trip | up to 4 days before | 3 days 80% · 2 days 50% · 1 day 20% · same day 0% |
| Hanatour Jeongdongjin overnight | up to 3 days before | 2 days 70% · 1 day 50% |
| Yellow Balloon Gangneung 1-day | up to 3 days before | 2 days 80% · 1 day 70% · same day or no-show 0% |
| Trazy English tours | up to 2 days before | non-refundable from 1 day before |

The Korean agencies accept **cancellations and changes only by phone on weekdays, 09:00-18:00**. Yellow Balloon's terms even state that cancelling a Saturday, Sunday or Monday departure after Friday's close of business counts as a same-day cancellation, and day counts skip weekends and holidays. Hanatour follows payment with a confirmation call and text, then sends the bus details, meeting point and guide's phone number to the lead traveler the day before. The page adds that if the text doesn't arrive, you can simply be at the meeting point 10 minutes early and staff will sort you out.

## Practical notes

- **First trains**: a 07:00 meeting means arriving by 06:50, so check the first subway from where you're staying. The English Seoraksan and Naejangsan tours leave Hongik Univ. Station at 06:00, which is earlier still.
- **State your pickup**: Hanatour asks you to write your boarding point (Gwanghwamun or Jamsil) in the booking request field. Leave it blank and you are counted at Gwanghwamun.
- **Lunch**: most itineraries leave you free at a market, such as Gangneung Jungang Market, Danyang Market, Daejeon Jungang Market or Hampyeong's traditional market. Carry some cash for stalls that don't take cards. A Korean market "free lunch" stop usually means 60-90 minutes to eat, so don't plan a sit-down feast.
- **Getting back**: Jamsil pickups return to Gangbyeon Station. Gwanghwamun drop-off is 19:00-20:30, and 21:30 for far trips like Hampyeong, so if you have a KTX to catch afterwards, check the last train.
- **Overnight products**: Hanatour sells a Jeongdongjin sunrise plus Gangneung coffee street "overnight" trip departing December 31. You leave at night, watch the first sunrise of the year on the east coast and return in the morning. Final details arrive by text the day before, and the cancellation rules differ from day trips.
- **Train day trips**: the same lists include KTX day trips. Hanatour's Gyeongju KTX day trip starts at ₩159,000, its Gunsan-Jeonju trip on the West Gold Train at ₩99,000, and Modetour's Jeongeup-Iksan KTX trip at ₩114,900, two to four times the bus fare.
- **Seats and gear**: seats are often unassigned, so arrive early if a window matters. For the hiking routes (five and a half hours of free time at Seoraksan), bring trainers, water and snacks. The English tour page recommends packing a lunch for Daedunsan.

## Wrapping up
