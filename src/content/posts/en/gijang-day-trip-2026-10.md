If you are staying in Haeundae and have one free day, Gijang (기장) is the easiest "Busan outside Busan" you can reach: a temple built on sea rocks, a theme park and outlet mall, a crab alley, and a church that is really a film set, all within three stops on the [[line:donghae]] Donghae Line. This guide is written from Haeundae, station by station, with a half-day and a full-day route and the last train home. Prices and hours are as of October 2026.

## Getting onto the Donghae Line from Haeundae

The Donghae Line does not stop at Haeundae Station on metro Line 2. The standard move is to ride [[line:busan2]] two stops back to **BEXCO Station** and change there to [[line:donghae]]. From BEXCO it is about 10 minutes to Osiria, 15 to Gijang and under 20 to Ilgwang.

The catch is frequency. Trains run every 15 minutes at rush hour but every **30 minutes** in the middle of the day, stretching to 40 between Bujeon and Ilgwang. Check the timetable on the platform before you plan the next hop. The base fare with a transit card is ₩1,600 (about $1.20) for up to 10 km, plus ₩200 per additional 10 km. Transferring from the Busan metro discounts only the base fare; the distance charge is extra.

Station by station:

- **Osiria**: Lotte World Adventure Busan, Lotte Premium Outlets Dongbusan, Ananti Cove, Skyline Luge, and Haedong Yonggungsa (bus connection)
- **Gijang**: Gijang Market's crab alley (walk), Daebyeon Port and Jukseong Church (bus connection)
- **Ilgwang**: Ilgwang Beach (walk), Imnang Beach (bus connection)

If you would rather stay on buses, [[bus:181]] runs Haeundae Station → Songjeong → Osiria → Yonggungsa → Daebyeon Port entrance → Gijang County Office, which is almost exactly this article's route, every 13 to 19 minutes.

## Haedong Yonggungsa

![A white stone bridge crossing the surf to the halls of Haedong Yonggungsa temple on the hillside](/images/gijang-day-trip-2026-10/18.webp)

A Buddhist temple built onto the rocks of the Gijang shore, said to date from 1376 and renamed in the 1970s. Admission is **free** and the gates open at 04:30. Closing time is listed as 19:20 by some official sources and 20:30 by others, so plan to be there in daylight.

Transport is the main thing to get right. Walking from [[line:donghae]] Osiria Station takes about 35 minutes, so take the bus: [[bus:139]] or [[bus:1001]] from Osiria Station exit 2, or [[bus:181]], [[bus:100]] or [[bus:1001]] from [[line:busan2]] Haeundae Station exit 7. Get off at the **Yonggungsa / National Institute of Fisheries Science** stop and walk about 10 minutes to the gate.

![Four stone pagodas stacked on the seaside rocks at Haedong Yonggungsa, with the blue East Sea behind](/images/gijang-day-trip-2026-10/17.webp)

From the gate you descend **108 steps** to the sea and climb them again on the way out. It is not accessible by wheelchair or stroller, and on weekend afternoons the queue starts at the narrow entrance. Parking uses the fisheries institute lot at ₩2,000 for the first 30 minutes and ₩500 per 10 minutes after, card only. Public transport is the less stressful option.

## Osiria Tourism Complex

### Lotte World Adventure Busan

![The red loops of the Lotte World Adventure Busan roller coaster against a sunset sky](/images/gijang-day-trip-2026-10/09.webp)

A walkway connects Osiria Station to the park. The Pirates Swing Ship opened in April 2026, and Giant Revolution and Forbidden Castle are scheduled to open later this year.

List prices for the 1-Day pass are **₩49,000 (about $37)** for adults, ₩41,000 for teens and ₩35,000 for children and seniors. The After3 pass, valid from 15:00, is ₩38,000, ₩34,000 and ₩32,000. Discounted tickets are common on Korean booking apps, so check before paying full price at the gate. Standard hours are 10:00–20:00 on weekdays and 10:00–21:00 on weekends and holidays, but they vary by date and the official calendar is the only reliable source. Ticket sales close an hour before closing.

The autumn season runs **29 August to 22 November 2026**. By day it is a MapleStory collaboration with a 16:00 parade and Pink Bean photo sessions at 11:00 and 17:00. After dark the Underland zone becomes **Zombie World**: zombie photo time at 18:00, the prequel show "Zombie World: The Beginning" at 19:00, and the rock musical "Big Daddy Zombie Night" in Royal Garden Plaza at 20:00. **There are no zombie shows on Tuesdays.** A makeup pop-up near the Underland entrance runs until 8 November. There is no separately named Halloween event; the whole autumn season is the zombie theme.

### Lotte Premium Outlets Dongbusan

Right next to the park. Weekday closing is between 20:00 and 20:30, weekends and holidays run to 21:00, and the Maison living hall closes 30 minutes earlier. Open daily except the Lunar New Year and Chuseok holidays. Many stores offer tax refunds for foreign visitors, which makes it a reasonable shopping stop for travellers from Singapore, Hong Kong or Malaysia on the way back.

### Ananti Cove and Eternal Journey

About 1 km north of the outlet along Gijanghaean-ro is Ananti Cove, a resort whose shopping street, Ananti Town, is open to the public. Its bookshop, **Eternal Journey**, is the draw: open 07:00–24:00 daily, with book sales 09:00–21:00 and a cafe 07:00–22:00. After 22:00 it runs unstaffed with a kiosk for purchases. It stocks Busan authors, foreign-language titles and limited editions, and it is a good rainy-day fallback.

### Skyline Luge Busan

Directly across the road from the Yonggungsa bus stop. A chairlift takes you up and a gravity cart brings you down. There is no single-ride ticket: **2 rides ₩27,000 (about $20)**, 3 rides ₩30,000, 4 rides ₩33,000, 5 rides ₩36,000. Children 110 cm and taller ride alone; smaller children ride with a guardian on a ₩12,000 ticket. Hours are 10:00–19:00 on weekdays and 10:00–20:00 on weekends and holidays, with sales ending 30 minutes before closing. It shuts for rain, lightning and typhoons. Since it shares a bus stop with the temple, pairing the two is the natural plan.

## Daebyeon Port and Gijang Market's crab alley

Daebyeon Port (대변항) is famous for anchovies, but the anchovy festival is a **spring** event; in 2026 it ran 24 to 26 April. Autumn is not raw-anchovy season. According to the Korea Tourism Organization, Gijang Market sets up anchovy and seaweed stalls in spring and **hairtail stalls in autumn**, and Gijang hairtail (cutlassfish) is at its best in the two months around Chuseok. In October, hairtail is the seasonal catch.

[[bus:181]] stops at the Daebyeon Port entrance, so it links directly from the temple. Gijang Market is a walk from [[line:donghae]] Gijang Station and runs 07:00–22:00. Its crab alley is a row of restaurants that steam snow crab and king crab picked from the tank. Prices are **market rate** and change daily; one restaurant's menu lists snow crab at ₩70,000–80,000 per kg (about $54–60), and some charge a ₩6,000-per-person table fee. Confirm weight, price and table charge at the tank before you sit.

## Jukseong Church

![Jukseong Church film set on the seaside rocks under a clear sky, with a red spire and grey brick walls](/images/gijang-day-trip-2026-10/10.webp)

Not a real church: it was built on the shore rocks in 2009 as a set for the SBS drama "Dream". The official name is **Jukseong Dream Set**. The drama is forgotten, but the red spire with waves breaking below keeps it on every Gijang itinerary. It is always open, with free entry and free parking, and the interior is used as a small gallery when exhibitions are on.

From [[line:donghae]] Gijang Station exit 1, Gijang village bus [[bus:village:6]] stops right at the set. In strong wind, stay inside the railings.

## Ilgwang, Imnang and Waveon

![The wide sand of Imnang Beach and a calm sea, with a promenade and village buildings on the right](/images/gijang-day-trip-2026-10/15.webp)

**Ilgwang Beach** is walking distance from [[line:donghae]] Ilgwang Station exit 1. Swimming season is summer only, but the sand is always open, and the beach curves in an arc from Icheonpo to Hangni, which makes it a pleasant walk. Because it is so close to the station, it is the easiest filler if you have an hour before sunset.

**Imnang Beach** is further north. It is about 1 km from Wollae Station on the Donghae Line, or [[bus:180]] from Ilgwang Station, though that bus runs only every 45 minutes or more, so time it carefully. The beach is over 1 km of sand backed by a pine grove, with a cluster of wild-catch raw fish restaurants nearby.

Just north of Imnang, on the Wollae-ri seafront, is **Waveon Coffee**, open 10:00–24:00 daily. The signatures are the Full Moon Coffee and the Wolnae Latte. It is a large building opened up towards the sea and draws a photo crowd; about 15 minutes by taxi from Gijang Market.

## Ahopsan Forest (a separate half day)

![Bamboo grove at Ahopsan Forest, with sunlight falling in patches between thick bamboo stems](/images/gijang-day-trip-2026-10/03.webp)

This one is inland in Cheolma-myeon, in the opposite direction from the Donghae Line route. It is a private forest kept by one family for close to 400 years, with bamboo groves and Korean red pines that appear regularly in dramas and films. **Entry is by advance reservation only**: adults ₩8,000 (about $6), youth and children ₩5,000. Open 09:00–18:00 with last entry at 17:00. **Cash only**, and strollers, bicycles and hiking poles are not allowed.

By public transport, take Geumjeong village bus [[bus:village:2-3]] from [[line:busan1]] Nopo Station to the Midong Village stop. Rather than squeezing it into the coastal day, give it a morning on another day.

## Two routes

**Half day** (morning or afternoon, 4–5 hours)
Haeundae Station exit 7, [[bus:181]] → Yonggungsa / fisheries institute stop → Haedong Yonggungsa, one hour → Skyline Luge across the road, two rides → [[bus:181]] from the same stop to Daebyeon Port entrance → look around the port, then [[bus:181]] to Gijang County Office → crab alley lunch or dinner at Gijang Market → [[line:donghae]] Gijang Station → change at BEXCO → Haeundae

**Full day** (out at 09:00, back by 21:00)
BEXCO → [[line:donghae]] Osiria → [[bus:139]] to Haedong Yonggungsa → luge → [[bus:181]] to Daebyeon Port entrance → lunch at Gijang Market → Gijang Station exit 1, village bus [[bus:village:6]] to Jukseong Church → Gijang Station → Ilgwang Station → walk Ilgwang Beach → back to Osiria for Lotte World on the After3 pass (entry from 15:00) → 18:00 Zombie World photo time and 19:00 show → outlet or Eternal Journey → Osiria Station → BEXCO → Haeundae

If you include Lotte World, something else has to give. A theme park takes half a day on its own.

## The last train back

Last [[line:donghae]] trains towards Bujeon, as of the timetable at time of writing, leave Ilgwang at 23:34, Gijang at 23:37 and Osiria at 23:41 on weekdays, one minute later at weekends. Change to [[line:busan2]] at BEXCO and you are in Haeundae with time to spare. These times come from an unofficial compilation, so confirm on the day in the KorailTalk app or on the station board.

Buses stop earlier. The last [[bus:181]] from the Gijang end towards Haeundae leaves before 22:00. If you stay late at Lotte World, plan on the train.

## Practical notes

- **The Donghae Line runs every 30 minutes by day.** Check the timetable before leaving the platform area.
- **Yonggungsa means 108 steps** and Ahopsan is a dirt trail. Wear shoes you can walk in.
- **Ahopsan Forest is cash only.** Everywhere else takes cards. At the crab restaurants, confirm the price before paying.
- **From Gimhae Airport**, take [[line:gimhaelrt]] to Sasang, [[line:busan2]] to BEXCO, then the Donghae Line. Allow an hour and a half.
- **Lotte World's zombie shows pause on Tuesdays**, and hours change by date.
- **From Busan Station**, [[bus:1001]] goes Busan Station → Haeundae Station → Osiria Station → Yonggungsa in one ride, about 1 hour 20 minutes.

## Wrapping up

Gijang's sights hang off three stations, which makes it the right distance for leaving a Haeundae hotel after breakfast and coming back for dinner. Temple and sea alone fill a half day; add Lotte World and it is a full one. Come back in spring for the anchovies, and in October eat hairtail and crab.
