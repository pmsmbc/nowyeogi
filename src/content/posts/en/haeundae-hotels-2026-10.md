Once you have decided on Haeundae, the next question is which part of Haeundae. A beachfront five-star, a Marine City resort tower, a mid-range hotel in the streets behind Haeundae Station, a small place up Dalmaji Hill and a business hotel next to BEXCO in Centum City are all "Haeundae", and they differ in price, noise and how far you walk to the sand. Counting from the lodging register that Haeundae-gu publishes on Korea's open data portal (as of 15 June 2026), the district has **358 registered properties with 15,855 rooms**, and **84% of those rooms sit in the two beachside neighbourhoods, U-dong and Jung-dong**.

Our [Busan neighbourhood comparison](/en/posts/busan-hotels-2026-09/) weighs Haeundae against Seomyeon and Nampo-dong. This guide stays inside Haeundae and splits it block by block. Fares and timetables are as of October 2026.

## Haeundae by the numbers

The district register covers everything licensed as lodging under Korea's public hygiene law: tourist hotels, motels, old-style inns, guesthouses and pensions. Serviced apartments where each unit is licensed separately count as separate properties.

| Neighbourhood | Properties | Rooms | Where |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| U-dong | 112 | 8,134 | Western beach, Dongbaek Island, Marine City, Centum City, north of Haeundae Station |
| Jung-dong | 102 | 5,176 | Eastern beach, east side of Gunam-ro, LCT, Dalmaji-gil, Mipo |
| Songjeong-dong | 110 | 1,854 | Songjeong Beach, mostly pensions and hostels |
| Jaesong-dong | 23 | 542 | Motel strip north of Centum City |
| Bansong, Banyeo, Jwa-dong | 11 | 149 | Residential, far from anything a visitor wants |
| **Total** | **358** | **15,855** | |

- **Twenty-one properties with more than 200 rooms hold 6,674 rooms**, 42% of the total. At the other end, **122 properties have ten rooms or fewer**, and 64 of those are Songjeong pensions
- 123 names include "hotel", 22 "motel", 8 "yeogwan" (an older type of inn) and 11 "residence"
- On the city's register of tourist accommodation (December 2025 list), Haeundae-gu has **91 entries**: 25 tourist hotels (five five-star, three four-star, five three-star, six two-star, six unrated), one small hotel, two resort condominiums, one family hotel and 62 hostels, most of them in Songjeong

## The beachfront: five five-star hotels

Busan has ten five-star tourist hotels on the city register, and **five of them are in Haeundae-gu**, all within two kilometres along the beach, Dongbaek Island and Marine City.

| Hotel | Address | Registered rooms | Worth knowing |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Paradise Hotel Busan | 296 Haeundaehaebyeon-ro (Jung-dong) | 537 | Largest in the district, centre of the beachfront |
| Grand Josun Busan | 292 Haeundaehaebyeon-ro (Jung-dong) | 330 | Opened October 2020, next door to the Paradise |
| The Westin Josun Busan | 67 Dongbaek-ro (U-dong) | 285 | Busan's oldest luxury hotel, opened June 1978; full renovation completed 2024 |
| Signiel Busan | 30 Dalmaji-gil, LCT (Jung-dong) | 260 | Opened June 2020, floors 3–19 of the LCT tower at the eastern end |
| Park Hyatt Busan | 51 Marine City 1-ro (U-dong) | 269 | Beside the yacht marina in Marine City, facing Gwangan Bridge |

Room counts come from the district register and may differ slightly from the hotels' own figures (a 2024 article put the Westin at 290).

Location is the whole story here. **Paradise and Grand Josun** sit directly on the sand in the middle of the beach, so everything is walkable. **The Westin Josun** is at the quiet western end by the entrance to Dongbaek Island, with the coastal walk outside the door. **Signiel** is at the opposite eastern end inside LCT, close to Dalmaji-gil and Mipo but about ten minutes' walk from the centre of the beach. **Park Hyatt** is not on the beach at all; it looks across the marina to Gwangan Bridge from Marine City.

There are three four-star hotels. **L7 Haeundae by Lotte** (55 Haeun-daero 570beon-gil, 383 rooms) opened in July 2024, about five minutes on foot from Haeundae Station and three from the beach, with a year-round heated rooftop pool on the 19th floor according to Lotte. **Shilla Stay Haeundae** (407 rooms) is across the same lane, and **Kwangsu Hotel** (171 rooms) at the western end of the beach is the third.

One warning for anyone searching by name: **Ananti at Busan, Hilton Busan and Banyan Tree Haeundae Busan** are often bundled with Haeundae, but their addresses are in **Gijang-gun**, the next county east. You need a car or the limousine bus to reach Haeundae Beach from them; the Gijang branch of airport limousine 1 stops at all three.

## Marine City: resort towers and residences

Marine City is the cluster of high-rise towers on reclaimed land west of Dongbaek Island. There is no beach, but Gwangan Bridge fills the view and the yacht marina is next door. Only three properties carry a Marine City address in the register, and all are large.

- **Park Hyatt Busan** (51 Marine City 1-ro) — 269 rooms
- **Hanwha Resort Haeundae** (52 Marine City 3-ro) — 417 rooms, registered as a resort condominium
- **Daewoo Worldmark Haeundae** (137 Marine City 1-ro) — a 393-room residence

Together that is 1,079 rooms. The beach is a 20–30 minute walk around Dongbaek Island or one stop from Dongbaek Station on [[line:busan2]]. Airport limousine 1 stops at the **yacht marina**, **Park Hyatt Busan** and **Hanwha Resort Haeundae** in turn, which makes Marine City the easiest part of Haeundae to reach with heavy luggage.

![The high-rise towers of Marine City beyond a bridge at the mouth of the Suyeong River on a clear day](/images/haeundae-hotels-2026-10/05.webp)

## Behind Haeundae Station: Gunam-ro and the mid-range belt

**Gunam-ro** runs straight from Haeundae Station on [[line:busan2]] down to the beach. Properties with a Gunam-ro address (including its side lanes, Gunam-ro 8beon-gil to 41beon-gil) number **56 with 2,733 rooms**; add the beachfront road, Haeundaehaebyeon-ro, and you get **130 properties with 7,979 rooms**, half the district's total. This is Haeundae's thick middle layer.

- **Chains and business hotels**: Ramada Encore Haeundae (9 Gunam-ro, 402 rooms), Toyoko Inn Busan Haeundae No. 2 (510 rooms), Sono Moon Haeundae (237), Kensington Resort Haeundae (243 Haeundaehaebyeon-ro, 244), Best Western Haeundae Hotel (42 Gunam-ro, 134) and Fairfield by Marriott Busan (314 Haeundaehaebyeon-ro, 225)
- **North of the station**: the lanes off Haeun-daero (570beon-gil, 594beon-gil, 608beon-gil and 620 Haeun-daero) hold 24 properties with 1,708 rooms. L7 and Shilla Stay are here; the rest are hotels of 30–70 rooms
- **The inner lanes**: Gunam-ro 12beon-gil and 18beon-gil are packed with 30–80 room hotels. Some are registered as motels or yeogwan (22 and 8 respectively) even where the sign says hotel. In Korea a "motel" is simply a small, cheap hotel with no restaurant, not a roadside lodge
- **Many licences in one building**: 6 Gunam-ro 21beon-gil (Elbon The Stay) holds ten licensed operators with 306 rooms, and 29 Haeundaehaebyeon-ro 298beon-gil (Haeundae Prugio City) holds thirteen with 396. Much of what booking sites list as "residence" or "stay" is this kind of officetel-style unit, often with no front desk and self check-in

A room within ten minutes' walk of both the station and the beach puts the sand, Haeundae Market, the restaurant streets and the subway all on foot. The trade-off is that the Gunam-ro side stays loud late on weekend nights.

## Dalmaji-gil and Mipo: LCT and the small places on the hill

From the eastern end of the beach, Dalmaji-gil climbs Dalmaji Hill. The road has **23 properties with 1,010 rooms**, and **632 of those rooms share one address, 30 Dalmaji-gil, the LCT complex**: Signiel Busan's 260 plus four LCT Residence operators with 372 rooms licensed unit by unit. If you book "LCT", check whether you are getting Signiel or a residence; they are different products.

Take LCT away and Dalmaji-gil is a neighbourhood of small lodgings. Hotel Illua (97 Dalmaji-gil, 61 rooms), Paul A Resort (60) and Suntree Hotel (209 Dalmaji-gil, 32) are the larger ones, and the lanes off Dalmaji-gil 62beon-gil down in Mipo have places of 3 to 35 rooms. The Blueline Park Mipo station and the coastal path are close and it is quiet, but it is a **hill**: you walk down to the beach and back up. Treat it as a taxi distance with luggage. The subway stop is Jung-dong on [[line:busan2]].

![Aerial view of the wooded Dalmaji Hill and Mipo village, with the LCT towers, Gwangan Bridge and Marine City behind](/images/haeundae-hotels-2026-10/09.webp)

## Centum City: BEXCO and the Busan Cinema Center

Centum City is around Centum City and BEXCO stations on [[line:busan2]]: the Shinsegae Centum City department store, the BEXCO exhibition halls and the Busan Cinema Center, three stops from the beach. 17 Centum 1-ro (the Centum Premier Hotel building) holds three operators with 583 rooms, 20 Centum 3-ro (the BEXCO business hotel building) holds seven with 321, and with Travelodge Suites Busan Centum (21 APEC-ro, 99 rooms) the area comes to **12 properties with 1,040 rooms**. No sea, but efficient if you are here for a trade show, the film festival or shopping. Jaesong-dong just north of Centum (23 properties, 542 rooms) is a strip of 30–40 room motels.

## When prices jump, and what "ocean view" means

Haeundae is Busan's most expensive district and its most volatile. Two periods to avoid or book early:

- **Beach season**: Haeundae Beach was officially open from 26 June to 15 September in 2026, with late July and early August the peak
- **Busan International Film Festival**: 6–15 October 2026. The main venue is the Busan Cinema Center in Centum City, so Centum and the beachfront hotels fill first

"Ocean view" means different things at different hotels. Even a hotel on the sand has rear rooms facing the city, and at hotels one block back an "ocean view" can be a sliver between neighbouring buildings. Where a hotel grades its rooms **ocean front, ocean view, ocean side and city view**, that is the order from most to least sea, and it explains why rates within one hotel vary so much. If the water matters, check the grade name; if it does not, a city-view room in the same hotel is often far cheaper.

## Gimhae Airport to Haeundae

### Airport limousine 1 (Haeundae–Gijang route)

According to Busan city's guide (as reorganised on 5 January 2026), airport limousine 1 leaves Gimhae Airport and stops at **Sangsudo Nambu office (The Beach Prugio Summit) → Shinsegae Centum City → BEXCO → yacht marina → Park Hyatt Busan → Hanwha Resort Haeundae → Dongbaek Island entrance → Haeundae Beach → Haeundae Oncheon intersection → Jangsan Station**. Jangsan is the terminus of the short service; the long service continues to **Osiria Theme Park → Dongam back gate (Ananti Cove) → Banyan Tree Haeundae Busan** in Gijang.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Fare | ₩9,500 (about $7) adult · ₩7,600 teen · ₩4,800 child |
| From the airport | To Jangsan 07:10–16:50 (16 a day) · to Gijang 10:10–21:40 (26 a day) |
| To the airport | From Jangsan Station 05:30–19:00 · from Gijang 04:50–16:30 |
| Journey | 90 minutes end to end on the Jangsan service, 110 on the Gijang service |
| Tickets | Bus ticket counter by Gate 4, first floor of the airport, or the Bustago app (QR boarding pass) |
| Payment | No cash on board. If seats remain you can tap a transit card when boarding |
| Luggage | Two pieces per passenger, 30 kg combined |

Headways are uneven: 20–60 minutes on the Jangsan service and 20–280 minutes on the Gijang one, so check the timetable rather than turning up. The operator is Gyeongnam Express New Busan Tourism. Get off at **Haeundae Beach** for the beachfront hotels, **Park Hyatt Busan** or **Hanwha Resort Haeundae** for Marine City, and **BEXCO** for Centum City.

### Subway

From Airport Station take the [[line:gimhaelrt]] to **Sasang**, then change to [[line:busan2]] for Haeundae: one transfer. Allow 25–35 minutes to Seomyeon and another 30–35 to Haeundae, so **a little over an hour** in all. With a transit card the fare is the higher of the light rail (₩1,600) and the Line 2 zone-2 fare (₩1,800), so **₩1,800** (about $1.35). The surcharge for transferring between Busan, Gimhae and Yangsan transport was abolished on 19 September 2025, so the transfer itself is free; cash fares get no transfer discount.

At Sasang the light rail is above ground and Line 2 is underground, and the transfer corridor is long. With big cases, the limousine or a taxi (about 40 minutes to Haeundae) is easier.

## Busan Station to Haeundae

If you arrive by KTX, take [[line:busan1]] from Busan Station six stops to **Seomyeon**, change to [[line:busan2]] and ride sixteen stops to Haeundae. Count on **about 50 minutes** including the change, ₩1,800 by transit card (zone 2). Check which station your hotel is nearest first.

- **Dongbaek** — The Westin Josun, Marine City (Park Hyatt, Hanwha Resort), western beach
- **Haeundae** — Gunam-ro, central beach (Paradise, Grand Josun), L7 and Shilla Stay
- **Jung-dong** — LCT (Signiel), the foot of Dalmaji-gil, Mipo
- **Centum City and BEXCO** — Centum hotels, Busan Cinema Center
- **Jangsan** — terminus of limousine 1's Jangsan service, handy for the earliest airport bus

## Which part to choose

- **The sea is the point and the budget allows** — a beachfront five-star. Paradise or Grand Josun for the centre of the sand, The Westin Josun for the quiet western end, Signiel for the eastern end and Dalmaji-gil
- **Gwangan Bridge at night, yachts, newer buildings** — Marine City, one subway stop from the beach
- **Beach, subway and restaurants all on foot** — the Gunam-ro belt between Haeundae Station and the sand, with the widest choice from chain hotels down to 30-room places
- **Quiet, with a coastal walk** — Dalmaji-gil and Mipo. It is a hill and the places are small, so travel light
- **BEXCO, the film festival, shopping** — Centum City. No sea, but three stops to the beach
- **Surfing, pensions** — Songjeong: 64 of its 110 properties have ten rooms or fewer, and the station is Songjeong on [[line:donghae]]
- **Heavy airport luggage** — a hotel on the limousine 1 route (Park Hyatt, Hanwha Resort, the beachfront) or near Jangsan Station

## Summary

- Haeundae-gu has **358 registered lodgings with 15,855 rooms** (as of 15 June 2026); **84% of the rooms are in U-dong and Jung-dong**.
- The five five-stars are **Paradise, Grand Josun, The Westin Josun, Signiel and Park Hyatt** (537, 330, 285, 260 and 269 rooms), all within two kilometres of beach, Dongbaek Island and Marine City.
- Gunam-ro and Haeundaehaebyeon-ro addresses hold **130 properties and 7,979 rooms**, so mid-range choices are between Haeundae Station and the beach.
- One LCT address holds 632 rooms and one officetel building holds ten-plus operators, so check whether a "residence" has a front desk.
- Airport **limousine 1 is ₩9,500**; the subway via Sasang is **₩1,800 and a little over an hour**; from Busan Station via Seomyeon it is **about 50 minutes**.
- Late July to August and the **film festival** (6–15 October) fill the beachfront and Centum first.
