If you are searching for "Lotte World Halloween 2026", here is the first thing to know: **Lotte World Adventure does not call its 2026 season "Halloween".** The event running through 31 October is an autumn horror festival called **"Welcome to Another World"** (기묘한 세계의 초대, roughly "An Invitation to a Strange World"). It is the Halloween season, just under a different name.

The short version: the festival runs **from 12 September to 15 November 2026**, the park is open as normal on Saturday 31 October, and nearly all of the horror shows happen after 5 pm, so an **After4 ticket** (entry from 4 pm) covers most of it.

Times and prices below are from **Lotte World Adventure's official website as checked on 6 October 2026**. The park says show times and ride closures can change on the day, so check the official app or website the morning you go.

## What this year's festival is

The official notice sets up the story like this: somewhere in Lotte World Adventure there is a door to a strange world, and only people holding an invitation can go through. Asia Today, covering the opening event on 15 September, described the concept as a door opening onto a horror world where reality and fantasy mix.

The **Junji Ito Collection tie-in** started on 19 September. According to Newsis (19 September), it is the first time the world of the 2018 anime adapting Junji Ito's short stories has been recreated in a theme park. It uses characters such as **Tomie** and **Souichi** and well-known episodes for in-park content, collaboration merchandise and food and drink. A report from Oneul Gyeongje (24 September) notes Junji Ito sculptures placed along the path to Magic Island.

### AR stamp tour

The official notice describes a free interactive trail: find **QR codes hidden around the park, complete AR missions and collect map pieces**. The five stops listed are:

1. "A Suspicious Invitation" at The Light of the Heart, Adventure 1F
2. "Suspicious Traces" at the Meeting Plaza, Adventure 1F
3. "The Map Comes Together" at the lamp-post banners, Adventure 1F
4. "Clues Left Behind" at Winnie Binnie, Adventure 1F
5. "The Last Piece", whose location is hinted at in mission 4

The notice does not say whether there is a prize for finishing.

### Dark Fantasy Festival

The official description is "a mysterious and strange festival taking place in another Lotte World", and Newsis called it a new sub-parade. The official schedule lists it from 12 September to 15 November.

It has become the talking point of the season. According to Oneul Gyeongje's on-site report (24 September), it features characters Lotte World created itself, including a lamp-post figure named **Vesper** (nicknamed "the lamp-post gentleman" online), a candle character called **Candra**, a queen and a showman. The same report says a character video Lotte World posted on X on 16 September passed 10 million views within a day. Visitors in the report were saving seats on the **benches behind the Garden Stage** an hour early, because the parade starts and finishes there. A Lotte World staff member said the parade was planned by the same director as 2024's "Street Horror Show: The Marionette".

### Dracula's Love, Tomie and Trick or Tarot

- **Dracula's Love**: officially "a special show that tells Dracula's love story as a rock musical". 19 September to 15 November, Garden Stage.
- **Tomie: A Strange Encounter**: a 15-minute programme on the third floor of Magic Castle. The official page states that **there is no interaction with guests** and that **it is cancelled in bad weather**.
- **Trick or Tarot**: a photo time with twin witches and a ghost (Newsis). The official schedule says 16:30 on weekends and holidays; one news report says 16:00, so go by the official time.

## Horror attractions: age limits and fees

There are three horror attractions, each with different rules.

| Name | Location | Hours | Price | Age |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Fantasy Dream: Strange Nightmare | Magic Island | 18:00 to 20:50 daily, to 15 Nov | Included in park ticket | **13+**, height 110 cm+ |
| Gwidam | Behind Magic Castle | 11:30 to 20:30, to 15 Nov | **₩10,000** extra | 13 to 64 |
| Madosindang | Behind Magic Castle | 11:30 to 20:30, to 15 Nov | **₩8,000** extra | 13 to 64 |

### Fantasy Dream: Strange Nightmare

Fantasy Dream is a dark ride that opened in 1993 (Oneul Gyeongje). A dark ride is an indoor ride where a vehicle carries you past scenes in the dark. In the evenings it switches to a horror version; the official line is that the sweet, colourful Fantasy Dream "turns into a grotesquely twisted world of nightmares".

- **No Magic Pass.** Standard queue only.
- Oneul Gyeongje reported that the night version was **fully booked four days in a row, 24 to 27 September**, after it launched.
- **As of 6 October, the official closure calendar lists Fantasy Dream as closed for scheduled maintenance from 6 to 11 October.** That includes the night version. Dates after that can still change.

### Gwidam and Madosindang (extra fee)

Both walk-through experiences run **from 26 June to 15 November 2026** according to the official pages, so they started in summer and continue through the autumn festival. Gwidam is set in "the mansion consumed first after the seal was broken"; Madosindang in "a sealed-off shrine and a failed sealing ritual".

Key points from the official rules:

- **Tickets are sold online only; no on-site sales.** They can sell out early.
- **Not included in the park ticket.** You need park admission as well.
- Break times 13:00 to 14:00 and 17:00 to 18:00.
- **Staff may ask for ID to check age.** The Gwidam page lists a passport and an alien registration card among accepted IDs, so bring your passport.
- Children under the age limit **cannot enter even with a parent**.
- People who have been drinking, pregnant guests, older guests and those with claustrophobia or heart conditions may be refused.
- High heels, slippers and open-toed shoes may be refused.
- Refunds only before your booked time slot.

The Magic Pass notice also names "Tomb of Horror" as a paid attraction, but it was not in the official attraction list on 6 October.

The official calendar, checked date by date on 6 October, shows the same pattern throughout the festival: **Sunday to Thursday 10:00 to 21:00, Friday and Saturday 10:00 to 22:00**, including Hangeul Day on Friday 9 October. Korea Tourism Organization data puts **last entry one hour before closing**.

| Date | Official note |
| --- | --- |

## Tickets (from 1 April 2026)

The "comprehensive pass" (종합이용권) covers park entry, rides and the Folk Museum. Paid attractions such as Gwidam and Madosindang are extra.

| Ticket | 1Day | After4 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Adult (19+) | ₩67,000 (about $50) | ₩55,000 |
| Youth (13 to 18) | ₩58,000 | ₩47,000 |
| Child (36 months to 12) | ₩50,000 | ₩39,000 |
| Baby | ₩17,000 | ₩17,000 |

The official FAQ says **children under 36 months enter free**; a baby ticket is only needed for toddler rides. Ages in Korea are counted in full years ("만" age), the same as in most countries. Since the horror programme is concentrated after 17:00, After4 is the better value if that is what you are coming for.

### Seasonal package and discounts

- **Strange Horror Make-up Package**: a 1Day pass plus a wound-sticker set (choose one of three). Regular ₩77,000, **online price ₩47,000**. 19 September to 15 November, at the Magic School make-up room on Adventure 1F. Cannot be combined with other discounts.
- Most October discounts on the official site are for Korean credit cards and payment apps.
- **We did not find a foreigner-only discount** on the official English or Japanese benefits pages on 6 October.

### Magic Pass (from 3 April 2026)

Magic Pass is a paid skip-the-line ticket. It now comes **only as a 5-ride pass**, in four tiers by which rides it covers.

| Tier | Price | Covers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Light | ₩54,000 | Stone Express, Eos Tower, Arcana Ride + basic rides |
| Standard A | ₩65,000 | Atlantis, Flume Ride, Gyro Swing and others |
| Standard B | ₩65,000 | Comet Express, French Revolution, Spanish Pirate Ship and others |
| Premium | ₩80,000 | All of the above + Pharaoh's Fury |

- **Only Premium can be bought in advance**, from midnight two days before your visit until 23:59 the day before. Other tiers go on sale **from 08:30 on the day in the official app**.
- Maximum 4 per person, valid on the day of purchase only, and you must hold a park ticket.
- **Not valid on Kong x Godzilla The Ride, Fantasy Dream: Strange Nightmare, Lotty Train, or paid attractions (Tomb of Horror, Moon Boat).**

### Free ride reservation system

There is also a free official reservation system. **Spanish Pirate Ship and French Revolution** open reservations at 10:00, 13:00 and 17:00; **Kidstoria** at 11:00 and 16:00. Go to the kiosk at the ride entrance, tell the staff how many people, and receive boarding tickets. **Everyone in your group must be there** (one ticket per person). Slots can run out early.

## Magic Island and the weather

![Magic Island's white castle and rides on Seokchon Lake on a clear day, with Lotte World Tower rising on the right](/images/lotte-world-halloween-2026-10/04.webp)

Lotte World Adventure has two parts: the indoor park under a glass dome, and **Magic Island**, an outdoor section on Seokchon Lake. **All of this season's horror content** (Strange Nightmare, Gwidam, Madosindang, Tomie) is on the Magic Island side.

The official FAQ says Magic Island rides may stop for:

- heavy rain or snow (including light but continuous rain)
- temperatures of minus 5°C or below
- winds of 7 m/s or stronger

Rides housed inside buildings, such as Comet Express and Fantasy Dream, run regardless of weather. The Tomie programme, however, is listed as cancelled in bad weather.

Magic Island sits on open water, so October evenings there can feel colder than the city forecast. For what to pack, see [Korea weather in October 2026 and what to wear](/en/posts/korea-weather-what-to-wear-2026-10/).

## Getting there

According to the official FAQ, take **[[line:2]] or [[line:8]] to Jamsil Station**, which connects underground directly to the park. Follow signs towards **exits 3 and 4**. The address is 240 Olympic-ro, Songpa-gu.

There is a car park (about 5,400 spaces according to the Korea Tourism Organization) with automatic number-plate recognition. On weekend evenings Jamsil also fills with visitors to Lotte World Mall and Lotte World Tower, so the subway is easier.

![Magic Island and the green 'LOTTE WORLD' roof of the indoor park seen from Lotte World Tower, linked by a bridge](/images/lotte-world-halloween-2026-10/10.webp)

## Park rules worth knowing

- **No re-entry**, with exceptions only for the ice rink, the Folk Museum (via the 3F gate), and a medical referral from the park's first-aid room.
- **Outside food is allowed**, except alcohol and strong-smelling food. The picnic area is on 1F, by the stairs next to the Spinning Basket exit.
- **Screenshots of QR tickets will not scan.** Use the app's "gift" function to send tickets to companions.
- The FAQ mentions lockers, but we could not confirm their location or price.
- Show enquiries: performance office 02-411-4339 (12:00 to 20:00, Korean).

## Halloween night in Seoul: a safety note

31 October 2026 falls on a Saturday. According to News1 (22 September), Interior and Safety Minister Yoon Ho-jung told a cabinet meeting that larger crowds than usual are expected because Halloween falls on a weekend, and that the government would **pre-emptively issue a "caution" level crowd-disaster alert** for the special safety period.

Inside the park, follow the queue lines and staff directions, and consider leaving a little before or after the post-parade rush. The same week also marks the fourth anniversary of the Itaewon crowd crush on 29 October. Memorial dates and the 2025 safety measures are covered in our [Itaewon travel guide](/en/posts/itaewon-guide-2026-09/).

## Wrapping up

The festival runs **12 September to 15 November**, and the action is **after 18:00**. Three things to remember: **Dark Fantasy Festival at 20:20**, **Strange Nightmare is 13+ and not on Magic Pass**, and **16 October is a private-hire day**.
