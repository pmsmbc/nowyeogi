There are two ways to eat in Gangnam. You either book one of the **MICHELIN restaurants in Cheongdam, Apgujeong and Sinsa**, or you do what the office workers do and eat lunch in the **back streets of Gangnam Station, Yeoksam and Sinnonhyeon**. In between sit hanwoo beef houses, Pyongyang cold-noodle shops and a galbi restaurant that has been going since 1939.

The short version: **of the 178 Seoul restaurants in the MICHELIN Guide Seoul 2026, 70 have a Gangnam-gu address.** That includes Mingles, the only three-star restaurant in Korea, seven of Seoul's ten two-stars and twenty of its thirty-one one-stars. At the other end, government "good-price" listings near Gangnam Station publish **₩7,000–8,000 (about $5–6) rice sets and noodles**. Every place below was checked against MICHELIN, the Seoul city government, the Ministry of the Interior and Safety or the Korea Tourism Organization (KTO), and anything about flavour is taken from those write-ups.

## What this guide covers

This guide covers **Gangnam-gu**, the district. For visitors that means Cheongdam-dong, Apgujeong and Dosan Park, Sinsa Station and Garosu-gil, Nonhyeon and Hakdong, Gangnam Station and Yeoksam, and Samseong-dong around COEX.

**Gangnam Station sits on Gangnam-daero, and the west side of that road is Seocho-gu, a different district.** This guide focuses on the Gangnam-gu side; where a shop just across the road is in Seocho-gu, like the Bib Gourmand Ggupdang outside Sinsa Station, it says so.

**Jamsil (Songpa-gu) and Seongsu (Seongdong-gu) are not covered.** Seongsu has its own [Seongsu food guide](/en/posts/seongsu-food-2026-09/).

## MICHELIN Guide Seoul 2026: 70 places in Gangnam-gu

MICHELIN announced the Seoul & Busan 2026 selection on **5 March 2026**, the tenth year of the Korean guide. Seoul has **178 restaurants**: one three-star, ten two-stars, thirty-one one-stars, fifty-one Bib Gourmands and 85 Selected restaurants.

Matching every address on MICHELIN's Seoul list gives this for Gangnam-gu:

| Distinction | All Seoul | Gangnam-gu |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Three stars | 1 | 1 (Mingles) |
| Two stars | 10 | 7 |
| One star | 31 | 20 |
| Bib Gourmand | 51 | 4 |
| Selected | 85 | 38 |
| Total | 178 | 70 |

**Gangnam has the stars but few Bib Gourmands.** Only four of Seoul's 51 Bibs are here, and none of the eight new 2026 Bibs. A Bib Gourmand in the Korean guide means a meal for **₩45,000 (about $34) or less per person**, by MICHELIN's definition.

**The ₩ signs are a band, not a price.** One ₩ is the lowest of four, ₩₩₩₩ the highest.

## Old shops: Hanilkwan, Motungijip, Mannadang

### Hanilkwan (Seoul Future Heritage 2013-281)

**Hanilkwan is the only restaurant in Gangnam-gu designated Seoul Future Heritage** (the city's register of everyday places worth preserving; Gangnam-gu list checked 6 October 2026). It is at **14 Apgujeong-ro 38-gil**.

According to the Future Heritage entry:

- **1939** — founder Shin Woo-kyung opens "Hwaseonok" in Pimatgol, the old alley behind Jongno
- **1945** — renamed Hanilkwan
- **1979** — her daughter Gil Sun-jeong takes over; it is now run by Gil's daughters Kim Yi-sook and Kim Eun-sook
- Redevelopment of Pimatgol forced a move to Sinsa-dong. The same page gives the year as **2008** in the timeline and **2007** in the description

The signature **goldongban** is bibimbap with about ten toppings in a stone pot heated above 200°C, with kelp crisps and gochujang fried in beef bone stock. Traditional galbi and galbitang are listed too. DiningCode lists goldongban at ₩18,000, galbitang at ₩20,000 (about $15) and Seoul naengmyeon at ₩14,000.

The entry gives **11–15 minutes on foot** from Apgujeong Station [[line:3]] and free parking for diners.

### Motungijip (Seoul Oraegage 2021)

![Two sliced rolls of gimbap on a white plate, with a small bowl of soup and chopsticks beside it](/images/gangnam-food-2026-09/05.webp)

A generic plate of gimbap, not from Motungijip.

Seoul's **Oraegage** programme recognises long-running small shops. Motungijip, at **20 Teheran-ro 1-gil** north of Gangnam Station, is a snack shop that has sat on a building corner since this busy district was still houses, according to the Oraegage site. It began as "Somunnan Guksujip", selling anchovy noodles to students going to the study hall. Today the best sellers are **ramyeon with rice cakes and fish-cake gimbap**, and the site says foreign visitors find it with map apps and translators.

Open **daily 10:00–23:00** per the Oraegage site. DiningCode lists ramyeon with rice cakes at ₩6,000 (about $4.50), fish-cake gimbap at ₩5,000 and kimchi fried rice at ₩8,000.

### Mannadang (Seoul Oraegage 2021)

![A yellow plate piled with flower-patterned brown yakgwa honey cookies on a mint-green background](/images/gangnam-food-2026-09/06.webp)

Generic yakgwa, not Mannadang's.

At **61 Nonhyeon-ro 175-gil**, east of Garosu-gil, this royal-court confectionery opened in **1991**, per the Oraegage site; the master and a successor run it together. The best-known product is **yakgwa made with grain syrup**, and the site says it is the only place in Seoul that both makes and sells the court rice cakes and sweets used on traditional ceremonial tables. Mon–Sat 10:00–18:00.

Gangnam-gu has four Oraegage shops; the other two are not food businesses.

On **6 October 2026** the good-price site lists **103 businesses in Gangnam-gu**, of which **97 are restaurants or cafés** (the rest are hair salons and the like). Many of those not in the tables above are in residential areas such as Gaepo-dong, Daechi-dong, Irwon-dong and Suseo-dong.

The scheme designates places **cheaper than the local average**, so these prices are the low end, not the norm. Each listing shows only a few headline dishes.

At the very bottom of the list: **tteokbokki for ₩3,000** (Gongwon Bunsik, Irwon-dong), **americano for ₩1,500** (a 24-hour fruit-and-coffee shop at 161 Yeoksam-ro), **noodles for ₩5,000** (Guksu Maeul, Samseong-dong) and **anchovy noodles for ₩5,500** (Sigol Bapsang, 313 Teheran-ro).

## Good to know

**Book the starred restaurants first.** MICHELIN describes Yun Seoul's guests as those "lucky enough to get a reservation", and Gaggen starts dinner for every guest at 19:00.

**Many places close at weekends.** Around Gangnam Station and Yeoksam a lot of good-price restaurants cater to office workers and list Saturday and Sunday closures. On the other hand, Jeongdeun Gimbap and Byeolha Gopchang are listed as 24-hour.

**KTO's rating text can be out of date.** Its Mingles entry still mentions two stars from 2022, so rely on MICHELIN's own announcement for ratings.

## Wrapping up

- **MICHELIN Guide Seoul 2026**: 70 entries in Gangnam-gu — three-star Mingles; seven two-stars (Kwonsooksoo, Mitou, Jungsik, Evett, Soigné, Alla Prima, Restaurant Allen); twenty one-stars, six of them new; four Bib Gourmands (Mandujip, Myeon Seoul, Gebang Sikdang, Gomtang Lab); and 38 Selected.
- **Old shops**: Hanilkwan, founded 1939 and on the Seoul Future Heritage register; Oraegage shops Motungijip and Mannadang (opened 1991).
