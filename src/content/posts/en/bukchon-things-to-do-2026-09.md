Bukchon is a neighbourhood you **walk by the clock and the calendar**. Its most famous lanes in Gahoe-dong are a red zone that tourists may enter **only between 10:00 and 17:00**, and everything around it keeps different days. The Baek In-je House and the Ko Hui-dong Art Museum close on Mondays, Jeongdok Library closes on **the first and third Wednesday**, while **MMCA Seoul and the National Folk Museum stay open on Mondays.** A few things apply only to autumn 2026: **Seoul Hanok Week runs until 11 October**, and **Songhyeon Green Plaza closes in December** for good.

The neighbourhood's background, the full red-zone rules and transport are in our [Bukchon and Samcheong-dong guide](/en/posts/bukchon-guide-2026-09/). This page is about **what to see, when, and in what order**. All hours and prices are **as of 6 October 2026, from each venue's official information**.

## What this page covers

"Bukchon" (north village) is a nickname for the old upper town between Gyeongbokgung and Changdeokgung palaces, not an administrative name. Here it means **Gahoe, Gye, Jae, Wonseo, Samcheong, Anguk, Sogyeok, Hwa, Sagan and Songhyeon-dong**, plus the Bugaksan slope behind them.

| --- | --- | --- |
| Gahoe-dong | The red zone (Gahoe-dong 31), Eight Views 3–7, Baek In-je House, craft centre | [[line:3]] Anguk Exit 2 |
| Gye-dong | Bukchon Culture Center, Bae Ryeom House, Seokjeong Boreum Well, Joongang High School | Anguk Exit 3 |
| Wonseo-dong | Changdeokgung's west wall (Views 1–2), the laundry stream, Ko Hui-dong Art Museum, Arario Museum | Anguk Station |
| Hwa, Sogyeok, Sagan-dong | Jeongdok Library and Museum of Education, MMCA Seoul, National Folk Museum | Anguk Station |
| Samcheong-dong | Samcheong-dong-gil, the stone steps (View 8), Samcheong Park, Malbawi, the Baegak city-wall trail | Jongno [[bus:village:11]] |
| Songhyeon, Anguk-dong | Songhyeon Green Plaza, Gamgodang-gil | Anguk Station |

## The red zone: what it means for sightseeing

The full rules are in the [Bukchon guide](/en/posts/bukchon-guide-2026-09/). Here is what matters when planning sights, from Jongno-gu's official page.

- **The red zone covers about 34,000 m² around Bukchon-ro 11-gil**, and tourist visits are allowed **only 10:00–17:00**.
- **Entering for tourism between 17:00 and 10:00 the next morning carries a ₩100,000 (about $75) fine**, enforced since 1 March 2025.
- Residents, their guests, shop customers and people just passing through are exempt, but Jongno-gu counts **taking photos or video, lingering to look around, and wandering unrelated to a shop** as "tourist activity" and says **even exempt people can be fined for it.**
- The **yellow zone** around Bukchon-ro 12-gil has no fine, but visitors are asked to keep to the same 10:00–17:00.
- The exact boundary follows land parcels, so we do not draw it here. **Follow the signs on the street.**

In practice: **before 10:00 and after 17:00, see what is outside the red zone** (Wonseo-dong, Gyedong-gil, Jeongdok Library, Samcheong Park, MMCA), and **save the Gahoe-dong lanes for 10:00–17:00.**

## The Eight Views of Bukchon: half are in Gahoe-dong 31

![Between the eaves of hanok in a Bukchon lane: layered tile roofs and an autumn tree, with Namsan and N Seoul Tower in the distance under a clear sky](/images/bukchon-things-to-do-2026-09/90.webp)

The city's Mediahub (August 2017) and the Korea Tourism Organization (KTO) list the same Eight Views.

| | Name | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | View of Changdeokgung | Wonseo-dong side |
| 2 | Wonseo-dong workshop street | Along Changdeokgung's west wall |
| 3 | Gahoe-dong 11 | Lane beside Jaedong Elementary School |
| 4 | Gahoe-dong 31 hill | KTO adds "Bukchon Observatory" |
| 5 | Gahoe-dong lane (uphill) | Gahoe-dong 31 |
| 6 | Gahoe-dong lane (downhill) | Gahoe-dong 31 |
| 7 | Gahoe-dong 31 | Gahoe-dong 31 |
| 8 | Samcheong-dong stone steps | Samcheong-dong |

Mediahub notes that **four of the eight are in Gahoe-dong 31**, which is the red-zone lane (Bukchon-ro 11-gil). So **Views 4–7 are safest between 10:00 and 17:00.** We found no official map checking each point against the boundary. **Views 1–2 (Wonseo-dong) and 8 (Samcheong-dong) are outside the red zone** and fine early or late.

The same article says that from **the junction between Views 6 and 7** you see Namsan and N Seoul Tower over the hanok roofs. It gets crowded; don't block the lane.

### Taking photos

- After 17:00, even hotel guests and shop customers can be fined for taking photos in the lanes (Jongno-gu).
- Jongno-gu states the policy exists to protect **residents' right to a quiet home**. Don't pose on doorsteps or talk loudly.
- **Bukchon Observatory** (22-3 Bukchon-ro 11da-gil) is, per KTO, **a private viewpoint in a converted rooftop**: you pay an entry fee that includes a drink, and it is open 11:00–18:00. We could not confirm the fee. It sits inside the Gahoe-dong 31 lanes, so if you leave after 17:00, don't stop for photos on the way out.

You'll often read that "Seoul selected the Eight Views", but **we have still not found an official document saying who chose them, or when.**

## Baek In-je House: free, but the inside is by tour only

![The men's quarters of the Baek In-je House, a tiled building with red pillars and rows of glass-paned doors, a yard and lawn in front and white clouds above](/images/bukchon-things-to-do-2026-09/15.webp)

Run by the Seoul Museum of History, this is a **Seoul Folklore Heritage** house (designated 17 March 1977). The museum's timeline:

| Date | Event |
| --- | --- |
| 3 July 1913 | Completed for banker Han Sang-ryong |
| 1928 | Sold to Hansung Bank |
| 1935 | Sold to newspaper owner Choi Seon-ik |
| 1 September 1944 | Sold to Baek In-je, founder of Paik Hospital |
| 30 November 2009 | Bought by the Seoul city government |
| 18 November 2015 | Opened as a historic-house museum |

According to the museum, it was built with **black pine from the Yalu River, first shown in Seoul at the 1907 Gyeongseong Exposition**. Unusually for a hanok, the men's and women's quarters are joined by a corridor and part of the house has a second floor. The Korea Heritage Service text says the house was "built by Han Sang-ryong in 1874", which conflicts with the operator's own timeline (1913); we follow the museum.

### Hours and tours

- **09:00–18:00, last entry 17:30.** It opens an hour before the red zone does.
- **Free.** Closed Mondays (open if a public holiday) and 1 January.
- **The grounds are open without booking.** **The interiors of the women's and men's quarters are for booked tour visitors only**, and the museum says the women's quarters open "flexibly depending on conditions".
- Tours are booked **online only, on the Seoul Public Service Reservation site** (Korean). **Arrive more than 10 minutes late and you lose your slot.** Children below school age need an adult.

| | Korean tours |
| --- | --- |
| Weekdays | 10:00 · 11:00 · 14:00 · 15:00 |
| Weekends & holidays | 10:00 · 11:00 · 14:00 · 15:00 · 16:00 (branch info page) |
| Other languages (16:00) | **Wed Japanese · Thu Chinese · Fri English**, booked or on the spot |

The weekend schedule **differs between two pages of the same museum website**: the branch page lists five tours, the booking page six (adding 13:30). If you want 13:30, check the booking screen.

## Gyedong-gil: a well, a school and a church

![The main building of Joongang High School, a grey stone building with a tall central tower and a bronze statue in the flower bed in front](/images/bukchon-things-to-do-2026-09/30.webp)

From the Culture Center, Gyedong-gil climbs to the gate of Joongang High School. It's **outside the red zone, so you can walk it before 10:00.**

- **Seokjeong Boreum Well**: per KTO, a well on Gyedong-gil about 90 m from the school gate. The name ("half-month well") comes from the story that **its water was clear for half the month and cloudy for the other half**. Mediahub says the Chinese priest **Zhou Wenmo, who entered Joseon in 1794, is said to have baptised converts with its water.** It's no longer used and the opening is sealed. These are traditions, not documented facts.
- **Joongang High School**: **the main, west and east buildings are each a Historic Site** (Korea Heritage Service). Per KTO the school moved here in 1917; after a 1934 fire the current main building, designed by architect Park Dong-jin, was **completed in September 1937**. **It's a working school, and we found no published visiting hours for outsiders.** Look from the gate.
- **Gahoe-dong Catholic Church** (57 Bukchon-ro): per KTO the parish dates from 1949, and the current church, built on the site of the old one, was **completed in November 2013**. Keep quiet during Mass.

## Wonseo-dong: outside Changdeokgung's wall

**View 1 (Changdeokgung)** and **View 2 (Wonseo-dong workshop street)** are here, along the palace's west wall heading north, outside the red zone.

### The Wonseo-dong laundry stream

According to the on-site sign quoted by Dong-A Ilbo (18 July 2026), this spot **to the right of the outer gate of Seonwonjeon shrine in Changdeokgung** is where **palace staff and ordinary people did their washing in the Joseon period**. It's about 4.8 m long and 2.4 m wide, beside a small stream that runs outside the palace's north-west wall; plentiful springs inside the palace meant it never dried up. Steps at the end of the wall lead down to a ledge where four or five adults could sit. The local village-bus stop is still called "Ppallaeteo" (laundry place).

### Ko Hui-dong Art Museum

At 40 Changdeokgung 5-gil (16 Wonseo-dong). According to the Jongno Cultural Foundation, **Ko Hui-dong, regarded as Korea's first Western-style painter, designed this hanok himself in 1918** after studying in Japan, and lived here for 41 years. It mixes hanok and Japanese house elements: a studio next to the men's room and corridors linking the wings. It became Registered Heritage in 2004; Jongno-gu bought and restored it and first opened it in 2012.

- **Exhibition 만고상청 ("ever green through the ages", our translation), 5 September 2025 – 27 December 2026**: Ko's ink paintings with the plum, orchid, chrysanthemum and bamboo paintings, landscapes and joint works of his contemporaries.
- **10:00–18:00 (last entry 17:30), free.** Closed Mondays, 1 January and the day of Seollal and Chuseok. **On a Monday holiday it opens and closes on Tuesday instead.**
- **Shoes off, and up to 15 visitors at a time.** No parking. Phone 02-741-8424.

### Arario Museum in Space

At 83 Yulgok-ro, a private contemporary art museum in the former Space Group building (Registered Heritage) opposite Changdeokgung. Newsis (12 February 2026) reported **about seven months of exterior brick repairs from 19 February to September**, with the galleries staying open **Tuesday–Sunday 10:00–19:00**. KTO lists admission at **₩15,000 for adults**, ₩9,000 for youths and ₩6,000 for children, closed Mondays. We could not confirm on the official site whether the works are finished or what is showing in October. The restaurant on the fifth floor is in [where to eat in Jongno](/en/posts/jongno-food-2026-09/).

### Changdeokgung, from the outside

If you plan to go in, read [things to do in Jongno](/en/posts/jongno-things-to-do-2026-09/) first. From the Bukchon side, remember:

## Samcheong Park and Bugaksan: Seoul's first city park

**Ninth, the cover photo was a rainy indoor hall.** We switched to Samcheong Park in autumn colour and matched the other photos to their sections.

## Quick summary

- **Red zone (Bukchon-ro 11-gil area): tourist visits 10:00–17:00 only**, ₩100,000 fine outside those hours. **Even exempt people can be fined for taking photos.**
- **Four of the Eight Views are in Gahoe-dong 31**; Views 1–2 (Wonseo-dong) and 8 (Samcheong-dong) are outside the red zone.
- **Baek In-je House is free**; grounds open, interiors by online-booked tour. 16:00 tours: **Japanese Wed, Chinese Thu, English Fri.**
- **Open on Mondays**: MMCA, National Folk Museum, Jeongdok Library and Museum of Education, Samcheong Park, the Baegak trail.
- **MMCA is free Wed & Sat 18:00–21:00**, closed 1 December.
- **National Folk Museum is free, open to 20:00 on Wednesdays through October**; Gallery 1 closed until 21 December; moving to Sejong by 2031.
- **Jeongdok Library and the Museum of Education close on the 1st and 3rd Wednesdays and public holidays**: 3, 5, 7, 9 and 21 October.
- **Ko Hui-dong Art Museum is free, shoes off, 15 at a time**; closes Tuesday after a Monday holiday.
- **The Baegak city-wall trail is open 24 hours**, 20 minutes from Samcheong Park.
- **Songhyeon Green Plaza closes in December.** **Seoul Hanok Week runs until 11 October.**
