In Bukchon you **have to watch the clock.**

The most famous lanes, **around 31 Gahoe-dong, are a red zone**, and tourists may enter only **between 10:00 and 17:00**. Yet Changdeokgung Palace opens at 9:00, and the MMCA Seoul stays open until 21:00 on Wednesdays and Saturdays. **Where you should be depends on the hour.**

So alongside the places themselves, this post adds **a table of closing days and a route by time of day**. The red zone rules themselves are covered in detail in [the Bukchon overview](/en/posts/bukchon-guide-2026-09/).

## The Eight Views of Bukchon: half of them are in the red zone

This is how the Seoul Metropolitan Government's media hub and the Korea Tourism Organization list the Eight Views of Bukchon.

| | Name | Relation to the red zone |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | View over Changdeokgung Palace | Outside |
| 2 | Wonseo-dong craft lane | Outside |
| 3 | Around 11 Gahoe-dong | Outside (craft workshop street) |
| 4 | 31 Gahoe-dong hill (Bukchon Observatory) | **Around 31 Gahoe-dong** |
| 5 | Gahoe-dong alley (uphill) | **Around 31 Gahoe-dong** |
| 6 | Gahoe-dong alley (downhill) | **Around 31 Gahoe-dong** |
| 7 | 31 Gahoe-dong | **Around 31 Gahoe-dong** |
| 8 | Samcheong-dong stone steps | Outside |

The **red zone set by Jongno-gu covers about 34,000 square metres around Bukchon-ro 11-gil**. That is the 31 Gahoe-dong lane. **Views 4 to 7, the ones you see in most photographs, are clustered inside it.**

- **Open to visitors: 10:00-17:00**
- **Entering for tourism between 17:00 and 10:00 the next day: a 100,000 won fine**
- Fully enforced since 1 March 2025

Residents, shop customers and people simply passing through are exempt, but according to Jongno-gu **even exempt people can be fined if they behave as tourists, for instance by taking photographs or lingering.** "Just one shot on the way through" does not count as passing through.

The exact boundary runs plot by plot, so this post does not try to draw it. **Follow the signs on the ground.**

One more thing. You will often read "the Eight Views of Bukchon selected by the Seoul Metropolitan Government", but **I could not find any official document saying who chose them or when.** Neither the city's media hub nor the tourism organisation names a selector.

## The Baek In-je house: free, but the rooms are by booking

The **Baek In-je house in Gahoe-dong** is **Seoul Folklore Heritage** (designated 17 March 1977) and is run as a branch of the Seoul Museum of History.

Some write-ups call it "paid, booking only". **That is only half right.**

- **Free admission**
- **09:00-18:00**, last entry **17:30**
- **Closed Mondays** (open if Monday is a public holiday) and on 1 January
- **The grounds are open to wander without booking**

**Only entry into the inner quarters and the men's quarters is by guided tour**, booked through Seoul's public service reservation site.

| | Tour times |
| --- | --- |
| Weekdays | 10:00 · 11:00 · 14:00 · 15:00 |
| Weekends and holidays | 10:00 · 11:00 · 13:30 · 14:00 · 15:00 · 16:00 |
| Foreign language (16:00) | **Wed Japanese · Thu Chinese · Fri English** |

Arrive **more than ten minutes late and the booking is cancelled automatically.**

The city's media hub says the house's **annexe looks out over the whole of Bukchon** (the interiors are by guided tour only). And it **opens at 9:00, an hour before the red zone.** It is 500 metres from exit 2 of Anguk Station [[line:3]], towards the Constitutional Court.

## The Bukchon Culture Center: Min Hyeong-gi's house, or Yu Jin-gyeong's?

![The wooden hall of a hanok on a rainy day, a courtyard and another wing visible through the open doors, shoes left on the stone step](/images/bukchon-things-to-do-2026-09/06.webp)

This is the hanok at the foot of Gyedong-gil. Its registered name with the Korea Heritage Service is **"Modern Hanok in Gye-dong, Seoul"**, and it is **National Registered Cultural Heritage** (registered 2 March 2006).

It was **built in 1921** and has long been called **Min Hyeong-gi's house**. Locals also knew it as "the Gye-dong lady's house".

But the **Seoul Hanok Portal, run by the city that operates the centre,** says that **recent research is identifying it as the house of Yu Jin-gyeong**, Min Hyeong-gi's wife. The Korea Heritage Service description still says "Min Hyeong-gi's house", so **the two official sources disagree.**

- **Tue-Fri 09:00-18:00**, **Wednesdays until 20:00**
- **Sat-Sun 09:00-17:00**
- **Closed Mondays**
- Opened in October 2002; a five-minute walk from exit 3 of Anguk Station

Note that **another page on the same Seoul Hanok Portal ("main facilities in Bukchon") gives different figures: no closing day and weekends 10:00-17:00.** This post follows the centre's own introduction page.

There is no notice of any admission charge.

## MMCA Seoul: open on Mondays

![The beige front of the MMCA Seoul on a clear winter day, a large bare tree and a vertical sign standing before it](/images/bukchon-things-to-do-2026-09/01.webp)

Of everything in Bukchon, this is the place that **runs the opposite way to the red zone.**

- **Mon, Tue, Thu, Fri, Sun 10:00-18:00**
- **Wed and Sat 10:00-21:00**
- **The only regular closures are 1 January, Seollal and Chuseok.** It does not close on Mondays.
- **Temporarily closed on Tuesday 1 December 2026**

Prices **vary by exhibition.** When three or more paid exhibitions are on, there is a **10,000 won combined ticket.**

**There are two ways in for free.**

- **Evening opening on Wednesdays and Saturdays, 18:00-21:00, is free**
- **The last Wednesday of every month is free**

People **aged 24 and under or 65 and over also go free.**

When the red zone closes at 17:00 **on a Wednesday or Saturday, come here.** It is free until 21:00.

## Jeongdok Library: the old Kyunggi High School

![A white three-storey modern building behind pine trees, the Jeongdok Library name board above its arched entrance and a fountain in front](/images/bukchon-things-to-do-2026-09/03.webp)

Its registered name is **"former Kyunggi High School, Seoul"**, **National Registered Cultural Heritage** (registered 28 February 2002). The school moved south of the river in 1976 and the building became a library.

**It does not close on Mondays.**

- **The first and third Wednesdays of each month**
- **Public holidays, except Sundays**

Each reading room keeps its own hours; the humanities, social and natural sciences room is open 09:00-20:00 on weekdays and 09:00-17:00 at weekends.

The **Seoul Museum of Education** shares the grounds. According to the city's media hub it opens **09:00-18:00 on weekdays and 09:00-17:00 at weekends**, closing on the same pattern as the library. The museum's own site listed its **September 2026 closures as the 2nd and 16th (first and third Wednesdays) and the 24th to 26th (Chuseok).**

## Changdeokgung and Wonseo-dong: the palace rests on Mondays

**View 1, over Changdeokgung**, and **View 2, the Wonseo-dong craft lane**, lie along the palace wall. They are outside the red zone, so **you can walk them before 10:00.**

Changdeokgung **opens at 9:00.**

| Period | Hours | Last entry |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Feb-May, Sep-Oct | 09:00-18:00 | 17:00 |
| Jun-Aug | 09:00-18:30 | 17:30 |
| Nov-Jan | 09:00-17:30 | 16:30 |

- **Closed Mondays** (open on public holidays)
- Palace **3,000 won**; the Secret Garden is **another 5,000 won** (palace ticket required)
- **Free if you wear hanbok**; free for Korean residents aged 24 and under or 65 and over

Gyeongbokgung closes on Tuesdays, but **Changdeokgung closes on Mondays.** It is easy to mix them up.

**Secret Garden tickets in the October foliage season** go fast. When bookings open is set out in [autumn leaves in Seoul](/en/posts/seoul-autumn-foliage-2026-09/).

## Gyedong-gil: a well and a school

Walk up Gyedong-gil from the Bukchon Culture Center and **Joongang High School** stands at the top.

The **Seokjeong Boreum Well** (110 Gyedong-gil) is said to take its name from being **clear for half the month and cloudy for the other half.** The city's media hub adds that **Father Zhou Wenmo, who entered Joseon in 1794, is said to have baptised people with its water.** All of this is tradition, not record.

At **Joongang High School**, **the main, east and west buildings are each a Historic Site** (Korea Tourism Organization). It is famous as a filming location for the drama "Winter Sonata".

But **it is a school.** I **could not find any official notice from the school of hours when outsiders may visit.** Do not go into the grounds on school days; the view from the main gate is enough.

## Samcheong Park: Seoul's park No. 1

At the top of Samcheong-dong-gil, **Samcheong Park** was, according to the city's media hub, **No. 1 of the 140 city planning parks gazetted on 12 March 1940.**

The **Forest Library** sits at the park entrance.

- **Tue-Sun 10:00-19:00**
- Closed **Mondays**, 1 January and the Seollal and Chuseok holidays

**View 8, the Samcheong-dong stone steps,** is on this side too. It is outside the red zone, so **you can walk it after 17:00.**

## Days of the week: watch Mondays and Wednesdays

| | Mon | Wed | Note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Baek In-je house | **Closed** | Open | Free |
| Bukchon Culture Center | **Closed** | **Until 20:00** | |
| Changdeokgung | **Closed** | Open | |
| Forest Library | **Closed** | Open | |
| MMCA Seoul | **Open** | **Until 21:00, free after 18:00** | Free all day on the last Wednesday |
| Jeongdok Library and Museum of Education | **Open** | **Closed first and third Wednesdays** | |
| Red zone (31 Gahoe-dong) | 10:00-17:00 | 10:00-17:00 | Every day |

**On a Monday, go to the MMCA and Jeongdok Library.** **On a Wednesday evening, the MMCA (free) and the Bukchon Culture Center.**

## A route by time of day

**09:00-10:00 - outside the red zone**
Changdeokgung (opens at 9:00), Views 1 and 2 along the Wonseo-dong wall, Gyedong-gil, Jeongdok Library.

**10:00-17:00 - inside the red zone**
Views 4 to 7 in the 31 Gahoe-dong lanes, the Baek In-je house. People live in these houses, so **do not photograph their front gates or talk loudly.**

**After 17:00 - outside the red zone again**
Samcheong-dong-gil and the View 8 steps; the MMCA on Wednesdays and Saturdays (until 21:00, free from 18:00); the Bukchon Culture Center on Wednesdays (until 20:00).

## What I could not confirm

**Who selected the Eight Views of Bukchon, and when**, is not in any official document I found.

**The plot-by-plot red zone boundary** is not drawn here. Follow the signs on site.

**The Bukchon Culture Center's hours** differ between two pages of the Seoul Hanok Portal.

**Joongang High School's hours for outside visitors** are not published by the school as far as I could find.

**The Seoul Museum of Education's hours** could not be confirmed on the museum's own site; the figures here are from the city's media hub.

## In short

- **Around 31 Gahoe-dong (Bukchon-ro 11-gil) is a red zone**, open to tourists **10:00-17:00** only. **Views 4 to 7** are inside it.
- **Even exempt people face a 100,000 won fine for tourist behaviour such as taking photographs.**
- **The Baek In-je house is free**; the grounds are open, and **only the rooms need a guided tour booking.** English at 16:00 on Fridays, Japanese on Wednesdays.
- **The Bukchon Culture Center is a 1921 hanok** that the city now presents as **Yu Jin-gyeong's house.**
- **The MMCA Seoul opens on Mondays** and is **free after 18:00 on Wednesdays and Saturdays.**
- **Jeongdok Library closes on the first and third Wednesdays**, not Mondays.
- **Changdeokgung closes on Mondays**, and hanbok gets you in free.
- **Samcheong Park was city planning park No. 1 in 1940.**
