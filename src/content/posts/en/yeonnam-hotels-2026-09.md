Search for a place to stay in Yeonnam-dong and you get hundreds of results. Count them in Seoul's official licensing data, though, and the picture is lopsided.

- **Operating hotels and inns in Yeonnam-dong - none.** Widen it to tourist accommodation and you get **one six-room hostel**
- **Operating urban homestays for foreign tourists in Yeonnam-dong - 413.** That is **more than any other neighbourhood in Seoul**

In short, nearly every bed in Yeonnam-dong and Mangwon-dong is **a room in a house where the host also lives**. For most international visitors that is fine, and it is what the law intends. If you want a front desk, you walk five minutes south to Donggyo-dong, just below Hongik University Station.

![A grey stone building in Yeonnam-dong's workshop street, its large upstairs windows half hidden behind green leaves, with a café entrance below](/images/yeonnam-hotels-2026-09/17.webp)

## How I counted

I downloaded four licensing datasets from the Seoul Open Data Plaza and kept only businesses whose address is **Yeonnam-dong or Mangwon-dong in Mapo-gu**. The homestay data is the 27 September 2026 update; the lodging and tourist accommodation data are from 24 September.

| Business type (governing law) | Yeonnam-dong, operating | Mangwon-dong, operating |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lodging - hotels, inns, serviced residences (Public Health Control Act) | **0** | 1 (an inn licensed in 1970) |
| Tourist accommodation - tourist hotels, hostels (Tourism Promotion Act) | **1** (hostel, 6 rooms) | 0 |
| Hanok stay (Tourism Promotion Act) | 0 | 0 |
| **Urban homestay for foreign tourists** (Tourism Promotion Act) | **413, with 688 rooms** | **149, with 214 rooms** |

Yeonnam-dong **does not even have a closed inn on record.** It has been a residential area throughout.

The one tourist-accommodation business is **Chris' House** (15 World Cup buk-ro 6-gil, hostel, 6 rooms), **registered on 21 July 2026**. I could confirm the registration but not how it operates or what it charges.

The Mangwon-dong inn was licensed in 1970 (8 World Cup-ro 11-gil, 10 floor-heated rooms). The data still lists it as operating, but **I could not confirm that it actually takes guests.** Mangwon-dong did have a 69-room tourist hotel at 90 Huiujeong-ro, which closed in January 2022.

## Yeonnam-dong is number one in Seoul

Seoul has **7,365** operating urban homestays for foreign tourists. Ranked by neighbourhood:

| Rank | Neighbourhood | Operating |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1** | **Yeonnam-dong, Mapo-gu** | **413** |
| 2 | Seogyo-dong, Mapo-gu | 356 |
| 3 | Sillim-dong, Gwanak-gu | 306 |
| 4 | Donggyo-dong, Mapo-gu | 256 |
| 5 | Changcheon-dong, Seodaemun-gu | 212 |
| ... | | |
| 10 | **Mangwon-dong, Mapo-gu** | **149** |

**Mapo-gu alone has 2,003**, or 27% of the city's total, more than three times second-placed Yongsan-gu (604). Seongsan-dong, which sits between Yeonnam and Mangwon, has another 154.

### Yeonnam is established, Mangwon is brand new

Split by year of registration, the two neighbourhoods look very different.

| Registered | Yeonnam-dong | Mangwon-dong |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2012-2020 | 91 | 1 |
| 2021-2023 | 80 | 9 |
| **2024** | **85** | **48** |
| **2025** | **85** | **37** |
| **2026 (to September)** | **72** | **54** |

**139 of Mangwon-dong's 149 homestays were registered in 2024 or later.** In Yeonnam-dong it is 242 of 413, but the neighbourhood has had homestays steadily since 2012. Turnover is real too: Yeonnam-dong has 201 closed homestay records, 78 of them from 2020-2021.

### Mostly one or two rooms

- **233 of Yeonnam-dong's 413 homestays (56%) have a single guest room**, and 125 have two
- The **median declared floor area is about 57㎡**
- By building type: **235 multi-household houses, 108 detached houses, 61 small multi-unit buildings**, and one apartment

Think **one floor of a house, or one room**, not a building full of rooms.

## What "urban homestay" means for you

The Enforcement Decree of the Tourism Promotion Act (article 2, version in force from 4 August 2026) defines the business as one where **a resident uses the home they live in** to offer **foreign tourists** lodging and a taste of Korean home life. Two things follow:

1. **It is for foreign visitors.** Korean nationals generally cannot book these legally (there is a narrow exception for urban-regeneration village enterprises). If you are travelling with Korean friends or family, check before you book
2. **The host must live there.** An empty flat let out whole does not qualify under this licence

Enforcement does happen. In 2019, a joint inspection by Mapo-gu, the city and the tourist police **checked 252 places and caught 19**: six registered homestays that had taken Korean guests, and 13 unregistered operations (Green Economy News, 31 December 2019).

### If you are booking on Airbnb

Airbnb made **business registration mandatory for new Korean listings from 2 October 2024 and for existing ones from 16 October 2025**. It said unregistered listings **could not take bookings for stays from 1 January 2026**. So a Yeonnam-dong listing on Airbnb today should be registered in some form. If you are Korean, or travelling with someone who is, ask the host which licence they hold.

## Living on a residential street

Yeonnam-dong's homestays cluster on **Seongmisan-ro (31), Donggyo-ro 38-gil (30), Yeonnam-ro 1-gil (26) and Yeonnam-ro 3-gil (25)**, which run along the Gyeongui Line Forest Park (the linear park locals call "Yeontral Park") and the lanes behind it. In Mangwon-dong the densest street is **Bang-ullae-ro (15)**, in the houses between Mangwon Market and the Han River park.

What that means in practice:

- **Your neighbours live next door**, and your host is in the same building. Late-night voices and suitcase wheels carry
- **Many houses have no lift.** If you have large bags, ask which floor
- **There is no 24-hour reception.** Get self check-in instructions before a late arrival
- **For quiet nights**, a block or two back from the park beats a room right on it; the park stays busy into the evening

For what to do in the area, see [things to do in Yeonnam-dong and Mangwon](/en/posts/yeonnam-things-to-do-2026-09/).

## If you want a hotel: Donggyo-dong

There are none in Yeonnam-dong, so head **just south of Hongik University Station to Donggyo-dong**, which has 14 operating tourist-accommodation businesses. The three large ones line Yanghwa-ro.

| Hotel | Address | Registered rooms |
| --- | --- | --- |
| L7 Hongdae by Lotte | 141 Yanghwa-ro | 340 |
| Holiday Inn Express Seoul Hongdae | 188 Yanghwa-ro (Aekyung Tower) | 294 |
| Mercure Ambassador Seoul Hongdae | 144 Yanghwa-ro | 270 |

**Holiday Inn Express** is registered in Aekyung Tower, the building attached to Hongik University Station. For the other two, RYSE in Seogyo-dong and counts by neighborhood, see [where to stay in Hongdae](/en/posts/hongdae-hotels-2026-09/).

Donggyo-dong also has **several small hostels registered in 2025 and 2026**; three of them share one building at 19 World Cup buk-ro 2-gil, each with six or seven rooms.

## Getting to the airport

Yeonnam-dong's biggest practical advantage is that **Hongik University Station is on the airport railway [[line:arex]], with no change needed**. Mangwon-dong is on [[line:6]], so it takes a little longer.

### AREX

**Towards the airport**, from Hongik University Station (all-stop train, the cheaper commuter service rather than the non-stop Express):

| Route | Fare (transit card) | First | Last |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hongik Univ. → Incheon Airport T1 | 4,650 won (about $3.50) | 05:28 | 23:48 |
| Hongik Univ. → Incheon Airport T2 | 5,250 won | 05:28 | **23:48** |

Times are the same on weekdays and holidays. A single-journey card costs 100 won more. AREX quotes 59 minutes from Seoul Station to T1 and 66 minutes to T2 on the all-stop train, and Hongik University is two stops closer to the airport than Seoul Station.

**Coming from the airport**, watch the late trains. The last all-stop train to Seoul Station leaves **T1 at 23:38 and T2 at 23:32**. The trains after that (T1 23:56, T2 23:50) **terminate at Digital Media City and do not reach Hongik University.**

**For Mangwon-dong**, change at Digital Media City to [[line:6]] and ride three stops (World Cup Stadium, Mapo-gu Office, Mangwon). Or ride AREX to Hongik University, take [[line:2]] one stop to Hapjeong, and change to Line 6 for one stop.

![The station sign at Mangwon on Line 6, number 621, with Mapo-gu Office to the left and Hapjeong to the right](/images/yeonnam-hotels-2026-09/06.webp)

### Airport buses

Stops **towards the airport**. All fares are 17,000 won for adults. Buses take transit cards or cash; there is no online booking for 6002.

| Route | Stop (number) | First | Last |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [[bus:6002]] | Hongik Univ., median bus stop on Yanghwa-ro (14016) | 04:26 | **21:11** |
| [[bus:airport:N6002]] | Hongik Univ., same stop | 23:00 | 04:00 |
| [[bus:6011]] | Yeonhui 104 Goji, median stop in front of Geo-Young (13009) | 04:41 | 20:58 |
| [[bus:6011]] | Mapo-gu Office Station, next to exit 7 (14816) | 04:46 | **21:03** |

The night bus N6002 calls at Hongik University at **23:00, 23:50, 02:20, 02:50, 03:20 and 04:00**, which is useful for early flights since it leaves well before the first AREX train (05:28).

After Hongik University, route 6002 also stops at Seogyo-dong (RYSE Hotel) and Hapjeong Station exit 1, where the last bus is 21:17. **For the southern part of Mangwon-dong use Hapjeong; for the northern part, bus 6011 at Mapo-gu Office Station.**

## Which to choose

- **You are an international visitor and want neighbourhood life** - a Yeonnam-dong homestay. The most choice in Seoul, and the airport train next door
- **You want quieter and newer** - Mangwon-dong. Most homestays opened in 2024 or later, near Mangwon Market and the Han River
- **You are Korean or travelling with Korean nationals** - the Yeonnam-dong hostel, or the hotels and hostels in Donggyo-dong
- **You want a front desk and breakfast** - the three hotels on Yanghwa-ro
- **You have a very early flight** - near Hongik University Station, where both the N6002 night bus and the first AREX train are available

For food, see [Yeonnam-dong and Mangwon restaurants, cafés and Mangwon Market](/en/posts/yeonnam-food-2026-09/).

## What I could not verify

**Prices.** There is no official source, so I have left them out. Homestay and hostel rates vary by site and by date.

**Whether Chris' House and the 1970 Mangwon-dong inn are actually operating.** I only confirmed that the licensing data lists them as open.

**Recent noise-complaint figures or enforcement results for Yeonnam-dong.** I could not find published data; the enforcement example here is from 2019.

**Airport bus drop-off times in the city-bound direction.** Airport Limousine does not publish them stop by stop.

## Summary

- **Yeonnam-dong has no hotels or inns**, and one **six-room hostel** registered in July 2026.
- It has **413 urban homestays with 688 rooms**, the most of any neighbourhood in Seoul. Mangwon-dong has 149.
- **139 of Mangwon-dong's 149 homestays** were registered in 2024 or later.
- Urban homestays are **for foreign tourists only**, in a home **where the host lives**.
- Airbnb has **blocked bookings at unregistered Korean listings from 1 January 2026**.
- **Last AREX train to the airport from Hongik University: 23:48.** From the airport, the last trains that reach Hongik University leave **T1 at 23:38 and T2 at 23:32**.
- Airport buses: **6002 from Hongik University (last 21:11)**, **6011 from Mapo-gu Office (last 21:03)**, and the **N6002** night bus.
