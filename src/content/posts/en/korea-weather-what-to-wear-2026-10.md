If you land in Seoul this week straight from Singapore, Hong Kong or Kuala Lumpur, the first morning will feel like a different planet. **Seoul's low this morning, Tuesday 6 October, was 10.0°C.** Two days earlier the afternoon had reached 26.3°C, and the Korea Meteorological Administration (KMA) put part of Gangwon Province under a cold wave advisory yesterday before lifting it at 9am today.

The short answer for packing: this cold is **sharp in the morning and short-lived.** Seoul still reaches about 22°C this afternoon, and the KMA expects it to get warmer towards the weekend. What you need is not a winter coat but **layers that work at 11°C at breakfast and 26°C at lunch.** Every number below was checked against KMA data at 1:40pm KST on 6 October 2026. Forecasts are updated several times a day, so check again before you go out.

**How cold is 10°C if you're from the tropics?** The Meteorological Service Singapore says Singapore's night-time minimum usually doesn't fall below 23–25°C. In other words, **Seoul's afternoon high today is roughly a normal Singapore night.** Hong Kong readers can compare it with the Hong Kong Observatory's 1991–2020 normals: even January, Hong Kong's coolest month, averages a daily minimum of 14.6°C. This morning in Seoul was colder than an average January morning in Hong Kong.

## Why it suddenly got cold

The KMA's weather information bulletin at 06:20 today put it simply: **cold air moving south from the north-west.** By 6am, morning temperatures were 5°C or below across inland Gyeonggi, inland and mountainous Gangwon, North Chungcheong and the north-eastern hills of North Gyeongsang, and 10°C or below across most other inland areas.

In order:

- **Sun 4 Oct** — Seoul peaked at 26.3°C with 2.4 mm of rain.
- **Mon 5 Oct, 10:00** — the KMA issued a **cold wave advisory for parts of Gangwon Province.** Its 22:00 commentary forecast Tuesday morning at 3–14°C, down from 9.7–18.6°C.
- **Tue 6 Oct, 04:00** — strong wind advisories lifted. The KMA said gusts of around 55 km/h (15 m/s), and around 70 km/h in the mountains, were possible until late morning, making it **feel colder than the thermometer said.**
- **Tue 6 Oct, 09:00** — cold wave advisory lifted.

The KMA expects morning temperatures **through Thursday 8 October to stay about 5°C below Monday's** (about 10°C below in parts of the Gangwon mountains). It also forecast **ice in parts of the Gangwon mountains** today and tomorrow, and frost in some inland Gangwon and north-eastern Gyeongsang hills.

## What to wear at each temperature

The KMA doesn't publish an official clothing chart, so this table is based on the temperature-by-temperature guide in the Korean daily Maeil Shinmun (1 October 2026), cross-checked against a similar guide in Dailian (13 September 2026). Both note that it depends on how easily you feel the cold, and on wind and sun.

| Temperature | What Koreans typically wear |
|---|---|
| 20–22°C | Long-sleeve T-shirt or shirt, light sweatshirt; thin cardigan for morning and evening |
| 17–19°C | Knit, sweatshirt or hoodie plus a cardigan or light jacket |
| 12–16°C | Jacket, trench coat, light transitional jacket, thicker cardigan |
| 9–11°C | Knit or fleece-lined top under a jacket or coat; thin padded jacket |
| 5–8°C | Thick knit, coat, lightweight down jacket, thin thermal layer |
| 0–4°C | Heavy coat or down jacket, scarf and gloves |
| Below 0°C | Long padded coat, thermals, scarf, gloves, hat |

![A man in a black fleece jacket with a backpack walking uphill beneath bright yellow ginkgo trees](/images/korea-weather-what-to-wear-2026-10/03.webp)

Applied to Seoul this week:

- **7–8am (11–14°C)** — between the 9–11°C and 12–16°C rows. A long-sleeve top plus **a jacket or trench coat.**
- **1–4pm (23–27°C)** — warmer than the top row. Jacket off; a single long-sleeve or even a T-shirt is fine for most people.
- **After 9pm (15–18°C)** — back around the 17–19°C row. The jacket goes back on.

For travellers from hot climates, two practical points. First, if you don't own a jacket, **one light jacket plus a long-sleeve top covers this week**; you don't need to buy a winter coat for an early-October trip. Second, the Maeil Shinmun guide's own advice is to **layer thin pieces rather than wear one thick one**, and to dress for the temperature at the time you'll actually be outside.

## Tips for specific situations

**Wind** — According to Korea's former Ministry of Public Safety and Security, as reported by Yonhap (January 2017), **perceived temperature drops about 2°C for every 1 m/s of wind.** Open spots such as the Han River parks or the viewpoints on Namsan and Bugaksan can feel noticeably colder than the street.

**Hiking** — The same ministry guidance, reported by Yonhap and Maeil Business, says **air temperature drops about 0.6°C for every 100 m you climb.** This morning Gangneung on the coast was 11.8°C while Daegwallyeong in the mountains above it was 0.7°C. Maeil Business recommends several layers of quick-drying clothing.

**Night festivals and night views** — Standing still outdoors after dark feels colder than walking around. Most of the events below run late into the evening.

**Going in and out all day** — On days with lots of malls and subway rides, a jacket that zips or buttons open is easier than a pullover.

**Buying something on arrival** — Myeongdong and Hongdae have plenty of clothing shops; we haven't checked specific stores, stock or prices. See [Things to do in Myeongdong](/en/posts/myeongdong-things-to-do-2026-09/) and [Things to do in Hongdae](/en/posts/hongdae-things-to-do-2026-09/) for the areas.

**Umbrella** — Seoul's chance of rain stays at 0–30% until Tuesday 13th. The only real rain in the forecast is **Wednesday 14th (60%).**

## Wrapping up

This is a **cold morning, warm afternoon** snap, and it won't last long.

- **Today to Thursday 8th** — Seoul mornings 10–12°C, afternoons 22–24°C. Long sleeves and a jacket you can take off.
- **Hangeul Day weekend (9–11 Oct)** — clear, 25–27°C in the afternoon. Jacket for evenings only.
- **After rain on the 14th** — cooler again, 9°C on the morning of the 16th. If you're coming in late October or November, pack a proper coat.
