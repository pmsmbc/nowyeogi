Search for a hotel in Seongsu (성수) and plenty of places with "Seongsu" in the name come up. But count only the two legal neighbourhoods that actually make up Seongsu, **Seongsu-dong 1-ga and 2-ga** (성수동1가·2가), in Seoul's licensing data and you get this:

- **8 registered tourist accommodations.** Just **one tourist hotel, Hotel POCO Seongsu (80 rooms)**; the other seven are hostels, all registered **since December 2025**
- **14 other lodging businesses** with 186 rooms between them: 13 traditional inns (*yeogwan*) and one even smaller inn (*yeoinsuk*)
- **140 foreign-tourist homestays**: 81 in Seongsu-dong 1-ga and 59 in 2-ga
- **No hanok guesthouses**

The short version: **inside Seongsu there is one place you would call a hotel, and most of the bigger hotels with "Seongsu" in their names are in neighbouring Gwangjin-gu, around Konkuk University.** This post goes through what is inside Seongsu one by one, then the hotels just outside it near Konkuk University and Wangsimni, and the airport bus 6013 timetable stop by stop.

## How these were counted

The four licensing datasets on the Seoul Open Data Plaza were filtered to **addresses in Seongsu-dong 1-ga and 2-ga**. Tourist accommodation and lodging data are as of 24 September 2026, homestays as of 27 September; only places licensed by that date and trading at that point are counted. This is the same data and method as the [Gangnam](/en/posts/gangnam-hotels-2026-09/) and [Itaewon](/en/posts/itaewon-hotels-2026-09/) accommodation posts.

| Dataset | Law | What it covers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tourist accommodation | Tourism Promotion Act (registration) | Tourist hotels, hostels, small hotels |
| Lodging business | Public Health Control Act (notification) | Inns, motels, general hotels, serviced residences — and tourist hotels and hostels again |
| Foreign-tourist urban homestay | Tourism Promotion Act (designation) | A resident's own home taking in foreign visitors |
| Hanok experience | Tourism Promotion Act (registration) | Stays in traditional Korean houses |

| Legal neighbourhood | Tourist accommodation | Lodging only | Homestays |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Seongsu-dong 1-ga (Seoul Forest, Ttukseom, Seoul Forest Station) | 2 | 5 (68 rooms) | **81** |
| Seongsu-dong 2-ga (Seongsu Station, Yeonmujang-gil) | **6** | **9 (118 rooms)** | 59 |
| **Total** | **8** | **14 (186 rooms)** | **140** |

Three things to know:

- **Places registered as tourist accommodation also appear in the lodging data.** Nine of the 23 lodging records in Seongsu share an address with the eight tourist accommodations, so the "lodging only" column counts the remaining 14
- **Room counts come from the lodging data.** The tourist accommodation dataset leaves the room field blank for most hostels
- **"Seongsu" is also used loosely as a neighbourhood brand.** Hotels and homestays around Konkuk University Station (Hwayang-dong and Jayang-dong in Gwangjin-gu) and in Songjeong-dong often put Seongsu in their names. They are listed separately under "Just outside the boundary"

### Hotel POCO Seongsu: the only tourist hotel in Seongsu

- **The official site calls it "a lifestyle hotel with 80 rooms"**, two minutes' walk from [[line:2]] Seongsu Station. The licensing data also lists 80 rooms
- The footer names **Kolon Global** as the operator, and the site presents it as part of the Kolon hotel family alongside Kolon Hotel in Gyeongju and Hotel Cappuccino in Gangnam
- Room types are Terrace Suite, Terrace Double, Deluxe Family Twin (one double and one single bed, about 23㎡), Superior King, Standard Double/Twin and a themed Art Room Double
- **Parking is in a ground-floor parking tower at ₩10,000 per night for guests** (15:00 on the day of stay to 11:00 the next morning). Free luggage storage ended on 11 July 2025; now there are only **paid self-service lockers** on the first and second floors (₩4,000 small, ₩8,000 large for the first five hours)
- **Its registration history is a little tangled.** The tourist accommodation data shows it registered as a tourist hotel in September 2014, closed that registration on 30 December 2022, and **registered again as a tourist hotel on 7 April 2026**. In the lodging data it has traded continuously since 2014 as an 80-room "general hotel"

### Seven hostels, all registered since December 2025

- **All seven were registered between December 2025 and September 2026.** The largest, Onjaestay, has 21 rooms; the rest have one to six. They are hostels on paper, but in practice **guesthouses of a few rooms**
- Cheonghwa Stay and the two Koda Hostel units sit side by side at 75 and 75-33 Dongil-ro, the road running east from Seongsu Station
- The Staybut Seongsu address also appears in the lodging data under a second name, **"Sweet Spot Seongsu"**, licensed on the same date. Booking sites may show either name
- Obhouse Seongsu was registered on 22 September, two days before this post's cut-off

### Places that have gone

The lodging data records **nine closures in Seongsu since 2020**. The largest were **Victoria** at 90 Achasan-ro (32 rooms, closed August 2024) and the general hotel **High Noon Hotel** at 9 Seoulsup 9-gil (28 rooms, closed December 2023). The other seven were inns and motels of 10 to 21 rooms.

**So since 2020, nine inns and motels have closed and, from late 2025, seven small hostels have opened.** In rooms, what closed (about 150) far outweighs what opened (39).

### Where, and in what kind of home

![A residential lane in Seongsu-dong, an ivy-covered two-storey house and red-brick low-rise flats, with a cyclist and a man with a backpack passing by](/images/seongsu-hotels-2026-09/03.webp)

- Grouped by road name: **Wangsimni-ro and its side lanes 25, Seoulsup-gil 20, Seongdeokjeong-gil 18, Dulle-gil 17, Yeonmujang-gil 13.** Seoulsup-gil and Wangsimni-ro cover the area north of Seoul Forest between Seoul Forest and Ttukseom stations; Yeonmujang-gil is the grid of lanes south of Line 2 between Ttukseom and Seongsu stations
- Some buildings hold several: four each at 11 Seoulsup 7-gil and 19-5 Wangsimni-ro 8-gil, three each at 18 Wangsimni-ro 6-gil, 26 Ttukseom-ro 3na-gil and 84 Yeonmujang-gil
- 81 of the 140 have a unit number in the address, meaning **one flat in a low-rise apartment or multi-household building**. Of those with a building name, "Forestside" appears most (four)
- The median floor area, where given (58 places), is **45㎡**, a little smaller than Gangnam (about 52㎡)

## Homestays are for foreign visitors only

Article 2 of the Enforcement Decree of the Tourism Promotion Act defines a foreign-tourist urban homestay as **a resident offering lodging in the home they live in** to **foreign tourists**, so that they can experience Korean home life. Only five kinds of housing qualify: **detached houses, multi-household houses, apartments, row houses and multi-family houses.**

1. **Korean nationals generally cannot stay in them.** If you are travelling with a Korean friend or partner, plan around the eight hotels and hostels, the 14 inns or the hotels just outside Seongsu
2. **The host has to live there.** Renting out an empty flat as a whole does not become legal under this licence
3. **Officetels (studio-office buildings) are not on the list of qualifying housing.** A short-term officetel rental cannot be designated as a homestay

Two ways to check whether a listing is registered. The Seoul Tourism Organization's **Seoul Stay** site lists homestays and hanok stays that "have completed legal business registration" with their district and that the city helps promote. Seongdong-gu's tourism website also has a homestay list, but it shows only nine places against 251 in the licensing data. Searching the name or address in the Seoul Open Data Plaza homestay dataset is the most reliable check.

A homestay in a low-rise block is **somebody's neighbour's home**. Ask in advance about rubbish rules and noise late at night. There is no 24-hour front desk, so get check-in instructions before a late arrival.

## Getting to the airport

Only one airport bus stops in Seongsu: Airport Limousine [[bus:6013]], whose route name is "Myeonmok / Gunja Station / Konkuk Univ. / Seongsu-dong". Its three Seongsu stops are **the last ones before Incheon Airport**. The fare is ₩17,000 (about $12.75) for adults and ₩12,000 for children at the time of writing.

### Staying in Wangsimni: bus 6010

[[bus:6010]] leaves Wangsimni Station (stop no. 04781, **across from exit 4, opposite Seongdong Police Station**) **19 times a day between 04:10 and 20:30**, running via Eungbong-dong, Geumho-dong, Oksu-dong, Hannam-dong and Dongbu Ichon-dong to the airport. The fare is the same as the 6013.

## Which to choose

- **A hotel inside Seongsu** — Hotel POCO Seongsu (80 rooms) is the only tourist hotel. Its official directions say two minutes from Seongsu Station; for the airport, use the 6013 at Seongsu 119 Center or Seongsu E-mart
- **Small and solo inside Seongsu** — the seven hostels opened since late 2025. Most have one to six rooms, so check photos and facilities carefully
- **A larger hotel** — head to the Konkuk University side in Gwangjin-gu: Pullman Ambassador Seoul Eastpole (150 rooms) or Hotel The Designers Premier Seongsu & Konkuk (100 rooms). One stop from Seongsu on [[line:2]], and the 6013 stops there too
- **Airport connections first** — Wangsimni, where [[line:2]] [[line:5]] [[line:gyeongui]] [[line:suinbundang]] meet and the 6010 departs. Three stops to Seongsu
- **A foreign visitor who wants to live near Seoul Forest** — homestays on the Wangsimni-ro and Seoulsup-gil side of Seongsu-dong 1-ga, the 20th busiest homestay neighbourhood in Seoul
- **Travelling with a Korean national on a budget** — homestays are foreigner-only, so inside Seongsu the 14 inns are effectively the whole choice. Widening the search to Konkuk University and Wangsimni adds many more

## Summary

- Seongsu-dong 1-ga and 2-ga have **8 tourist accommodations**: one tourist hotel, **Hotel POCO Seongsu (80 rooms)**, and seven hostels all registered since December 2025.
- **14 places trade only as lodging businesses, with 186 rooms**; 13 are inns. Nine inns and motels have closed since 2020.
- There are **140 homestays** (81 in 1-ga, 59 in 2-ga), all designated since September 2023 and open **to foreign visitors only**, in the host's own home.
- Most bigger hotels with "Seongsu" in their names are in **Gwangjin-gu around Konkuk University**. The five places on Seongdong-gu's own list are in **Wangsimni and Yongdap-dong**.
- The airport bus is **6013 (last from Seongsu 119 Center 20:43, Gyeongil Elementary School 20:50)**; from Wangsimni it is **6010 (04:10 to 20:30)**.
