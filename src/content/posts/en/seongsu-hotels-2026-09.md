Start with the fact. **There is not a single registered tourist hotel in Seongsu-dong.**

Seongdong-gu officially lists five registered tourist accommodation businesses, and every one of them has an address in **Wangsimni or Yongdap-dong**. In Seongsu-dong 1-ga and 2-ga the count is **zero**. Counting through the Ministry of the Interior and Safety licensing data gives the same picture: of 299 rooms in Seongsu-dong, **186 are in yeogwan and yeoinsuk**, the old-style inns, and only one place is hotel grade.

So sleeping in Seongsu-dong means, in practice, **sleeping in somebody's home** - and that is exactly why **there are things to check before you book.**

## How many rooms there actually are

These are counts from the Ministry of the Interior and Safety licensing data. **In Seongsu-dong 1-ga and 2-ga there are 21 lodging businesses trading, with 299 rooms between them.**

Those 299 rooms break down like this.

- **186 rooms (62%) are yeogwan and yeoinsuk**, the old-style inns
- **Only one place counts as a hotel**: a general hotel with 80 rooms

Widen it to the whole of Seongdong-gu and the pattern holds. Of 74 businesses and 1,397 rooms, **half - 689 rooms - are in Doseon-dong**, over by Wangsimni Station, not in Seongsu.

The official lists make it plainer still.

| Measure | Seongdong-gu | Seongsu-dong |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Registered tourist accommodation (district office list) | 5 | **0** |
| Seoul Tourism Organization official lodging list | 4 | 1 |
| Seoul Stay licensed urban homestays | 24 | 9 within 1km of Seongsu Station |

Put together: **it is not that there is nowhere to sleep in Seongsu-dong, it is that most of it is old inns**, and the places officially listed for tourists can be counted on one hand.

**There are no hanok in Seongdong-gu at all.** To sleep in a hanok, see the [Jongno accommodation post](/en/posts/jongno-hotels-2026-09/).

## What you get at each budget level

No prices here. **Not one official source - the Seoul Metropolitan Government, the Seoul Tourism Organization or the Korea Tourism Organization - publishes nightly rates for accommodation in Seongsu-dong.** What can be stated precisely is **what you get legally** at each level.

### Budget - an urban homestay, meaning somebody's home

At this level the real option in Seongsu-dong is **an urban homestay on the Seoul Stay list**. There are nine within 1km of Seongsu Station.

Be clear about what that is. **One room, or one floor, of somebody else's house.** There is no front desk and no 24-hour check-in.

In exchange, the safety requirements are written into law.

- **At least one fire extinguisher**
- **A standalone smoke alarm** in each room (plus a carbon monoxide alarm where heating is individual)
- **A system capable of providing foreign-language assistance**
- A total floor area of **under 230 square metres**

If you want a hostel, you need to go outside Seongsu-dong, towards **Yongdap-dong or Ha-Wangsimni**.

### Mid-range - about one place inside Seongsu-dong

Inside Seongsu-dong there is effectively one place on the official lists trading under a hotel sign. The rest are in Wangsimni, Majang and Yongdap.

At this level you may come across somewhere described as a "residence", and it is worth knowing what that means. It is a lodging business of the residence category - **a "residence"-type serviced lodging**. It has a kitchen and is required to have a front desk and a lobby, but **it has not been through tourist hotel grading.** For a few nights that is no problem at all.

### Luxury - not in Seongsu-dong

The whole of Seongdong-gu has five registered tourist accommodation businesses and **no large brand hotels.**

If you want something upmarket, the realistic answer is **to stay in Wangsimni and come to Seongsu-dong during the day.** It is three stops on [[line:2]] to Seongsu Station.

## Check this before you book

This is the most important part of the post.

**Running a lodging business without registering it carries up to 2 years in prison or a fine of up to 20 million won** (about $15,000). A 2021 amendment singled out lodging and doubled the penalty. A lot of material online still says one year and 10 million won; that applies to the other trades, not to lodging.

The traveller is not the one who gets prosecuted, but **if the place is raided you have to leave.** Better to look first.

### Four warning signs

**One: "You get the whole flat to yourself."** The foreign-tourist urban homestay scheme only works **in a home the owner actually lives in**. Renting out an empty flat entire is not made legal by this scheme. If there is no sign at check-in that the owner or their family lives there, be suspicious.

**Two: "It's an officetel."** The law allows five kinds of home - **detached houses, multi-family houses, apartments, row houses and multiplex houses** - and **officetels are not among them.** Seongsu-dong has a lot of officetel-style short-term rentals, and none of them can be made legal under this scheme.

**Three: the rate on the booking screen differs from the rate on site.** The registration conditions include a requirement **to honour the rate posted online**. If more is demanded in person, you can point to that as a breach of the registration conditions.

**Four: it has a kitchen but calls itself a hotel.** Cooking facilities make it a "residence"-type serviced lodging. That is not a bad thing; it simply means **it has nothing to do with hotel grading.**

On officetels the government has been explicit. This is from a Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism press release.

> Lodging businesses using officetels are not permitted under current law, and all such cases are therefore subject to enforcement.

### This matters more than the fine

The real reason to avoid illegal lodging is not the fine. Busan city puts it most precisely.

> In the case of unlicensed lodging businesses, the installation of fire-safety equipment and the taking out of liability insurance under the relevant laws are not mandatory, so it may be difficult to obtain proper compensation if a fire or other accident occurs during your stay.

**Neither fire-safety equipment nor liability insurance is compulsory.** Which means that if something happens, you may not be covered. In an unfamiliar country that is not something to trade away for a difference in price.

### How to tell whether a place is legal

There is a simple method.

- **If it is on the Seoul Stay list, it is legal.** The Seoul Tourism Organization states that it only lists places that have completed legal registration with their district office
- **If the name is on Seongdong-gu's Seongdong lodging page**, the district office knows about the business
- If something goes wrong, there is **the 1330 tourist interpretation line**, the Seoul tourist complaint centre, and for unlicensed operation itself, **the district office's public health department**

For what it is worth, the homestay list Seongdong-gu publishes gives **the block and unit number of apartments**, in the form "Haengdang Hanjin Town, block 121, unit 1802". **Being in an apartment does not make it illegal.** It only has to be registered.

## Is Seongsu-dong worth staying in

![The plaza in front of Wangsimni Station and the glass-clad station building](/images/seongsu-hotels-2026-09/01.webp)

On transport alone, the location is rather good.

**The advantages.**

- **Gangnam (Samseong Station) in 16 minutes and Hongik University Station in 27, both with no transfer.** That is an unusual position to be in anywhere in Seoul
- **Three stops on [[line:2]] to Wangsimni Station, about 5 minutes.** From there you can change to [[line:5]] [[line:gyeongui]] [[line:suinbundang]]
- **The Incheon Airport limousine stops inside Seongsu-dong** (see below)

**The disadvantages.**

- **The Seoul Tourism Organization's official lodging list has only four places in the whole of Seongdong-gu**, and inside Seongsu-dong the practical choice is an urban homestay
- No hanok, no hostels, no large hotels
- **The last train on Saturday is early** - 23:21 towards Gangnam
- Even Seoul Forest takes 13 minutes by subway, looping round via Wangsimni. Walking is better

## The airport bus is route 6013

This is probably the single most useful line in the post.

**The Incheon Airport limousine [[bus:6013]] stops inside Seongsu-dong.** The official route is written as "Myeonmok / Gunja Station / Konkuk University Station / Seongsu-dong".

- The Seongsu-dong stops - **Seongsu 119 Fire Station, Seongsu E-Mart and Gyeongil Elementary School**
- Fares: **17,000 won for an adult (about $12.75), 12,000 won for a child**
- Those three stops are **the last boarding section before the airport.** Once you are on, the bus runs straight through

If you are staying in Wangsimni, the bus is [[bus:6010]]. At Wangsimni Station the first departure is 04:10 and the last is 20:30, and the stop is **across from exit 4, opposite Seongdong Police Station**.

**[[bus:6002]] does not stop in Seongdong-gu.** Routes 6001 and 6002, which come up constantly in other Seoul travel posts, have nothing to do with Seongsu-dong. Get that mixed up and you will get off in the wrong part of the city.

## What could not be verified

**There is no official source for actual room rates in Seongsu-dong.** That is why no figures appear in this post. You will have to look on the booking sites yourself.

**The number of lodging businesses in Seongdong-gu differs from source to source.** Seongdong-gu's own statistics say 64 (as of 31 December 2025); the Ministry of the Interior and Safety's licensing data says 74 (updated 8 September 2026). The reference dates and the counting basis are different, so neither can be called wrong - which is why this post does not mix the two and cites each figure to its own source.

**Seongdong-gu's lodging pages do not say when they were last updated.** Absence from the list is not proof that a place is unregistered.

## Wrapping up

- **Zero registered tourist hotels in Seongsu-dong.** All five in Seongdong-gu are in Wangsimni and Yongdap.
- **186 of Seongsu-dong's 299 rooms are yeogwan and yeoinsuk.** Only one place is hotel grade, with 80 rooms.
- **For tourists it comes down to urban homestays** - nine within 1km of Seongsu Station on the Seoul Stay list.
- **Unlicensed lodging carries up to 2 years in prison or a fine of up to 20 million won.**
- **An officetel short-term rental can never be legalised as an urban homestay.**
- **Renting an empty flat entire is not allowed either.** The owner has to live there.
- **If it is on Seoul Stay or the Seongdong-gu list, you are safe.**
- **The airport bus is route 6013**, or 6010 for Wangsimni. Routes 6001 and 6002 do not stop here.

In short: **Seongsu-dong is a good neighbourhood to come to in the daytime and an awkward one to sleep in.** If you still want to stay, pick from the Seoul Stay list; otherwise book in Wangsimni and ride three stops in.
