If you are in Seoul over the Hangul Day long weekend and want a field of flowers without a long trip, this is the closest big one. The **2026 Guri Cosmos Festival** runs **Friday 9 to Sunday 11 October** on the Han River banks in Guri, a small city on Seoul's eastern edge.

The short version: **take the subway and the festival bus, not a car.** Guri City says it will extend village buses from three Line 8 stations to the festival site until 11 pm on all three days. Last year about 360,000 people came over three days.

Everything here is as of 2 October 2026. The performer line-up and timetable had not been published by Guri City when this was written; what could not be confirmed is listed at the end.

![A wide field of pink and white cosmos with people walking along a path behind it and silver grass on the slope beyond](/images/guri-cosmos-festival-2026-10/15.webp)

To be clear, the photo above is a cosmos field at Haneul Park in Seoul (October 2015), used to show what a Korean cosmos field looks like. It is not the Guri festival site.

## At a glance

- **Dates** — Friday 9 to Sunday 11 October 2026 (9 October is Hangul Day, a public holiday)
- **Venue** — Guri Hangang Park (Guri Hangang Simin Gongwon), Topyeong-dong, Guri
- **Slogan** — roughly "Come to Guri to flower-gaze" (Kkotmeong-hareo Guri-ro on)
- **Edition** — the **18th**, according to Yonhap
- **Flower field** — **79,620㎡**, 11,940㎡ larger than last year
- **Cost** — the park is open year-round; no 2026 entry fee notice was found
- **Park enquiries** — Guri City parks team, +82-31-550-2472 (Korean)

"Kkotmeong" is a playful Korean word for zoning out while looking at flowers, on the model of "bulmeong" (staring into a campfire). Because 9 October is a Friday holiday, **the whole three-day weekend is festival time**. On 8 October the city also holds its 41st Citizens' Day event in the same park area.

## What's new: a bigger field

At a planning meeting on 7 September, Guri City said it wanted the festival to centre on the flowers themselves, and announced a cosmos field of 79,620㎡. Yonhap rounded this to "80,000㎡ on the riverbank".

One number does not add up. In 2025 Yonhap described the field as "90,000㎡, the largest in the capital region", but this year's figure implies last year's was about 67,680㎡. The reason for the gap was not found, so this guide uses the city's 2026 figure.

![A single white cosmos with deep pink edges, a blurred flower and a bud behind it](/images/guri-cosmos-festival-2026-10/13.webp)

The mayor, Shin Dong-hwa, inspected the site on 1 October. No official bloom report was found. Last year the festival was in late September and there were worries the flowers would not be ready, but reports said the field was in full bloom on the first two days. This year's dates are almost two weeks later.

## Programme: what's confirmed and what isn't

**Confirmed or reported**

- **A concert by well-known singers and fireworks at the closing ceremony.** The city's safety committee met on 16 September specifically to plan for crowds leaving all at once afterwards.
- Food stalls, with hygiene checks planned.
- More stages for local artists, according to the mayor.

**Not yet announced**

- Performers by day, stage times, opening and closing ceremony times
- The list of hands-on activities

For reference, **the 2025 festival (26–28 September)** had an eve-of-festival concert with a drone show, an opening ceremony, and a final-day concert with fireworks. Daytime activities included a "cosmos tour train" through the fields, a 1970s–80s classroom, old school uniform dress-up, photo booths, and a re-enactment of Beolmal Dari-balki, a folk bridge-walking custom from the Beolmal area of Guri. Whether these return this year is not yet known.

## Getting there from Seoul

Guri Hangang Park sits on the river flats, too far to walk from any station. During the festival the city **extends local village buses (maeul buses, small neighbourhood routes) to the park**:

- **Linked stations** — [[line:8]] Guri, Donggureung and Jangja Lake Park (Jangjahosu Gongwon) stations
- **Hours** — **09:00–23:00**, 9 to 11 October, 4 routes, 36 buses
- **Temporary stop** — **under Godeok-Topyeong Bridge**, on a newly built, wide and lit road
- On 8 October (Citizens' Day): 09:00–16:00, 4 routes, 28 buses

[[line:8]] reached Guri on 10 August 2024, when the Byeollae extension opened, so you can ride straight from Jamsil or Cheonho in Seoul. Guri Station is also on the [[line:gyeongui]], handy if you are coming from Yongsan or Cheongnyangni.

**Route numbers and stop locations were not in the city's announcement.** Follow festival signs or staff at the station. The city ran similar buses last year and admitted some passengers found transfers confusing; it promised more routes and clearer signs for 2026.

## Driving

![A hexagonal pavilion and white flowering shrubs in the Guri Hangang Park flower garden, an elevated road behind](/images/guri-cosmos-festival-2026-10/01.webp)

According to the Korea Tourism Organization, the park has **about 800 parking spaces**. With 360,000 visitors over three days last year, roughly 120,000 a day, that is nowhere near enough.

- In September 2026 Guri city councillors asked for detailed plans for **parking, shuttles and the slow exit of cars after events**.
- The city says the extra buses are meant to reduce car use. No temporary car park locations or road closures had been announced.
- **Avoid leaving right after the closing-night fireworks** if you can.

## Crowds and safety

The city says it will run a joint safety inspection with outside experts before the festival. From a visitor's point of view:

- **Closing night (11 October) is likely to be the busiest moment**, with the concert, fireworks and everyone heading for buses and car parks at once.
- Events run late enough that buses go until 23:00. Bring a layer if you plan to stay into the evening.
- The flower beds are not walkways. Take photos from paths and photo zones.

## The park itself

![People riding a canopied four-seat pedal cart along a park path lined with flower pots](/images/guri-cosmos-festival-2026-10/04.webp)

Guri Hangang Park is planted with canola in spring and cosmos in autumn. KTO lists a climbing-hydrangea tunnel, a nature study area, walking paths and a "family healing" campsite, which is first come, first served and open 10:00–18:00 from October to December. Despite the name, **overnight camping and cooking are not allowed**. The spring canola festival is big too: 320,000 visitors in May 2026, according to reports.

![A dirt walking path with benches, lined on both sides by tall trees, in Guri Hangang Park](/images/guri-cosmos-festival-2026-10/02.webp)

A Korean newspaper guide notes that the park has well-kept cycle paths, so you can combine flowers and a bike ride. No information was found on bike restrictions or parking during the festival.

## Nearby

**Donggureung (Nine Eastern Tombs)** — the largest group of Joseon royal tombs, with nine tombs including that of the dynasty's founder, King Taejo. The Joseon royal tombs became a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 2009. Address: 197 Donggureung-ro, Guri; the nearest station is Donggureung on Line 8.

- **Hours (Sep–Oct)** — 06:00–18:00, last entry 17:00 (KTO)
- **Closed** — Mondays, except when Monday is a public holiday
- All three festival days are open days. The admission fee could not be confirmed from an official page this time; check the Royal Tombs website before you go.

**Jangja Lake Park** — a neighbourhood park around a lake that was once badly polluted and has since been cleaned up. It has walking paths, a red-clay barefoot trail, a rose garden and an ecology centre, and is always open. Jangja Lake Park station on Line 8 is one of the festival bus stops, so a morning walk here and an afternoon at the festival works well.

## A short history

Guri City turned neglected riverbank land into canola and cosmos fields and began holding spring and autumn festivals there. Park Young-soon, mayor until December 2015, named this as one of his achievements when he left office. Yonhap calls 2026 the 18th edition, but the first year and any cancelled years could not be confirmed from official records.

| Year | Dates | Edition | Visitors |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2025 | 26–28 Sep | 17th | about 360,000 (city count) |
| 2026 | 9–11 Oct | 18th | — |

In 2025, rain on the last morning raised fears of cancellation, but the city says afternoon events went ahead.

## Practical notes

- **When to go** — for the flowers in daylight, **the morning of 9 October** may be calmer. For the concert and fireworks it is closing night, but plan your way home first.
- **Last buses** — the city says village buses run until 23:00; exact last departures may vary by route.
- **Language** — no foreign-language guidance for this festival was found. Expect festival notices to be in Korean.
- **Other autumn flowers** — the Seoul Silver Grass Festival at Haneul Park runs 17–23 October, so it does not clash.

## What we could not confirm

- **Entry fee** — the park is open all year and a 2018 report called the festival free, but no 2026 notice was found.
- **Opening hours, line-up, ceremony times** — not in reports up to late September. Guri City's website (guri.go.kr) could not be reached at the time of writing.
- **Bus route numbers and stops, temporary car parks, road closures**
- **Current bloom status**
- **The first edition and any cancelled years**
- **Donggureung admission fee**
- **Field size by year** — the 2025 report of 90,000㎡ does not match this year's "11,940㎡ larger than last year".

## Wrapping up

Three things to remember: **9 to 11 October, Guri Hangang Park, and the festival bus from Line 8 stations (09:00–23:00).** If you drive, expect a slow exit on closing night.
