If you are heading to G-STAR, Korea's biggest game show, in Busan this month, the same ticket gets you into a second event next door. **Game Culture Festival 2026** fills BEXCO Exhibition Center 2 from November 19 to 22, alongside G-STAR. Korea's Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism and the Korea Creative Content Agency (KOCCA) run it. Expect hands-on zones built around Korean game franchises, game soundtracks played on traditional Korean instruments, and an esports viewing party.

![Sky-blue BEXCO BUSAN letter sculpture among pine trees in front of the BEXCO building](/images/busan-game-culture-festival-2026-11/01.webp)

## Admission: your G-STAR ticket is the ticket

According to KOCCA's press release, the festival has no ticket of its own. Anyone who has bought a **G-STAR 2026 ticket** can walk in at no extra cost, so the G-STAR ticket is the only thing you need to buy.

- **Prices**: adult ₩18,000 (about $13.50), youth ₩8,000 for those born on or after January 1, 2008 (as of writing)
- **Where to buy**: online only, in advance. There are no on-site sales, and you can buy until midnight the day before your visit
- **Limits**: two tickets per person per day, valid only on the date you choose
- **Free entry**: visitors with disabilities (bring your welfare card), national merit recipients, and preschool children born in 2020 or later. Pick up tickets at the main information desk
- **Saturday**: November 21 is split into sessions. At the time of writing, Session 1 had sold out and only Session 2 was left

The official ticket page gives a few fixed times: doors open at 11:00 on Thursday, November 19, ticket checks end at 17:00, and last entry is 17:30. Check the G-STAR site for opening times on the other days.

## K-Game Land: four franchise zones

The core of the festival is **K-Game Land**, four zones themed on Korean game franchises. Collect stamps as you go round for a souvenir, and catch the daily quiz events on the mini stage.

- **K-FUTURE**: the Greymane Camp from Pearl Abyss's *Crimson Desert*
- **K-GLOBAL**: *Overwatch*, built around its Busan map and D.Va, the Korean hero who fights in a pink mech
- **K-HERITAGE**: Beomgol Village from Netmarble's *Mongil: STAR DIVE*
- **K-STORY**: NEOWIZ's *Goodbye Seoul: Itaewon* and *SANABI: A Haunted Day*. The SANABI booth runs a speedrun challenge

![BEXCO exhibition halls and front plaza with mountains behind](/images/busan-game-culture-festival-2026-11/02.webp)

## K-Sound Stage: game music on Korean instruments

On the afternoon of the opening day, Thursday, November 19, the **K-Sound Stage** puts on a concert. The Busan National Gugak Center, Korea's state traditional-music institution in Busan, will play music from *PUBG*, *AION* and *Lies of P: Overture* on gugak (traditional Korean) instruments, conducted by Kim Yu-won. Folk-crossover group dodree and singer Ahn Ye-eun follow, and dodree is debuting a new arrangement of "Blue Wind" from *Mongil*. The press release does not give a start time yet. If the concert is the reason you are going, book Thursday.

## GCF Esports League and viewing party

The **GCF Esports League** is a *VALORANT* tournament run with the streaming platform SOOP, with a ₩5,000,000 prize pool. Five-player teams aged 15 and over signed up, online qualifiers take place on November 14, and the final is on Saturday, November 21. The final is played at the T1 Base Camp Haeundae arena rather than at BEXCO. The festival hall at BEXCO hosts a viewing party for it, with match-prediction events and snacks.

## Practical notes

- **Getting there**: BEXCO sits right by BEXCO Station on Busan subway [[line:busan2]]. It is a transfer station with the [[line:donghae]] commuter line, which is handy from the Haeundae and Gijang side. Entrances to Exhibition Center 2 can change from event to event, so follow the signs on the day
- **Opening day**: Thursday, November 19 is also the Korean college entrance exam (Suneung), a national event when roads and transit get extra care in the morning. Morning roads may be busier than usual, but doors don't open until 11:00 that day anyway
- **Plan your route**: G-STAR's big publisher booths are mostly in Exhibition Center 1, and this festival is in Exhibition Center 2. Leave time for walking between the two if you want to complete the stamp card
- **Budget**: ₩18,000 for the ticket (₩8,000 youth) plus a meal in Centum City. The Shinsegae and Lotte department stores in Centum City are right by BEXCO
- **Contact**: for tickets, Ticketlink (1588-7890, Korean); for programs, the festival's official site
