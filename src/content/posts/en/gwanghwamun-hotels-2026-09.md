Gwanghwamun, City Hall and Jonggak sit side by side in the middle of Seoul, but the places to sleep in each are **completely different**. Counting Seoul's licensing records gives a short summary:

- **Gwanghwamun and City Hall have big hotels; Jonggak (Gwancheol-dong) has small hostels.** Gwancheol-dong's 24 operating hostels are **the most of any neighbourhood in Seoul**
- **Sejong-no, the neighbourhood that contains Gwanghwamun Square itself, has no registered accommodation at all**

Hotels sorted by budget are in [where to stay in Jongno](/en/posts/jongno-hotels-2026-09/) and [where to stay in Myeongdong](/en/posts/myeongdong-hotels-2026-09/). This post sticks to the data and the airport run.

## How we counted

We took four licensing datasets from the Seoul Open Data Plaza and kept every business whose legal-neighbourhood (*beopjeong-dong*) address is in the list below. Tourist accommodation and general lodging are the 24 September 2026 update; homestays are from 27 September.

| Area | Legal neighbourhoods |
| --- | --- |
| Gwanghwamun | Jongno-gu: Sejong-no, Dangju-dong, Sinmun-no 1-ga and 2-ga, Naesu-dong, Doryeom-dong, Susong-dong, Cheongjin-dong, Junghak-dong, Jeokseon-dong |
| Jonggak | Jongno-gu: Gwancheol-dong, Seorin-dong, Gongpyeong-dong, Jongno 1-ga |
| City Hall | Jung-gu: Taepyeong-ro 1-ga and 2-ga, Mugyo-dong, Da-dong, Seosomun-dong, Jeong-dong |
| Sogong · Bukchang | Jung-gu: Sogong-dong, Bukchang-dong |

Central Myeongdong and Euljiro are left out. Sogong-dong and Bukchang-dong sit between City Hall Station and Myeongdong, so they get their own row.

| Property | Neighbourhood · street address | Type | Registered rooms | Registered |

| Shilla Stay Gwanghwamun | Susong-dong · 71 Sambong-ro | Tourist hotel | **338** | 2015 |

- **Four Seasons Hotel Seoul** also lists 317 rooms on its official site. Airport bus 6002 names its stop after the hotel
- **Somerset Palace Seoul** gives 7 Yulgok-ro 2-gil as its address, where **284 rooms of serviced lodging** (*saenghwal sukbak*) are registered under a company branch name. It is licensed as serviced lodging, not as a tourist hotel
- The other seven are **hostels in Dangju-dong, Cheongjin-dong and Sinmun-no 1-ga**, and **all seven were registered in 2025–2026**. One building at 32-5 Jongno 5-gil in Cheongjin-dong has three different hostels on floors 4, 5 and 6

All ten homestays in the Gwanghwamun area are in the residential streets behind Gyeonghuigung Palace, in **Sinmun-no 2-ga (8) and Naesu-dong (2)**. Under the Tourism Promotion Act enforcement decree, this licence lets **a resident host foreign tourists in the home they live in**, so **Korean nationals generally cannot book them**.

### Cheongjin-dong: 22 closed inns

Cheongjin-dong's records hold **22 closed lodgings (21 inns, 1 tourist hotel)**, most registered between the 1960s and the 1990s. The three hostels above are all that operate there now.

## Jonggak: Seoul's hostel capital

**Gwancheol-dong**, the nightlife blocks southeast of Jonggak Station, has **24 operating tourist-accommodation licences, and every one is a hostel**.

- That is **the highest number of hostels in any neighbourhood in Seoul** (467 citywide). Changsin-dong (19) and Jongno 5-ga (17) come next
- Counting all tourist accommodation, it ranks **second** only to Yeoksam-dong in Gangnam (31)
- **22 of the 24 were registered in 2025–2026** (7 in 2025, 15 in 2026), including two in August 2026 alone
- The 22 with a room count range from **1 to 22 rooms**, and more than half have 10 or fewer

The two older ones are Hostel Chloe (2014, 19 rooms) and Hostel Haru (2016, 14 rooms). A single building at 99 Cheonggyecheon-ro holds two hostels on different floors.

Gwancheol-dong also has more than 20 lodgings outside the tourism list, which is where names can mislead:

- **Lemong Hotel (르몽호텔, 106 rooms), MG Hotel (39) and Sky Hotel (28) are licensed as inns (*yeogwan*)**, Korea's traditional budget category, not as tourist hotels
- Several places trading as "UH Suite" are **serviced lodging or "general hotels"**

**Do not assume "hotel" in a name means tourist-hotel standards**; check booking-site photos and recent reviews. Gwancheol-dong is also a lane of bars and restaurants that stays busy late.

## Weekend rallies and road closures

Sejong-daero, the boulevard from Gwanghwamun to City Hall, **is often closed to traffic for weekend rallies and events**. Two 2026 examples:

- **Saturday 4 April**: an Easter event closed the Gwanghwamun–Sejong intersections section **from midnight to midnight** (Seoul Shinmun, 3 April 2026)
- **Saturday 15 August** (Liberation Day): after a rally between Donghwa Duty Free and Daehanmun, **both directions between the Seoul Police Agency and Salvation Army intersections** were to be closed during the march (Hankyung, 14 August 2026)

Several airport-bus stops below — **Gwanghwamun (Donghwa Duty Free), Gwanghwamun (Koreana Hotel), Gwanghwamun (Press Center) and Seoul City Hall (Deoksugung)** — are on or beside Sejong-daero. If you check out on a weekend, **look at the Seoul Metropolitan Police traffic centre's rally list and the TOPIS closure map the day before**, and plan on the subway rather than a taxi. Whether airport buses move their stops on closure days is something to check with the bus company.

## Which area to pick

- **Big hotel near the palaces** — Four Seasons or Shilla Stay Gwanghwamun, or The Plaza and Koreana at City Hall
- **Low budget, travelling solo** — the Gwancheol-dong hostels in Jonggak. Seoul's largest cluster, but mostly small places that opened after 2025
- **One bus to the airport, early start** — Route 6701 around The Plaza, Koreana and Westin Josun; Route 6002 near Gwanghwamun and Jonggak stations
- **Dawn flight** — N6002 from the Four Seasons stop
- **Late arrival** — last train from T1 at 23:38 or bus 6002 from T1 at 23:29
- **A hotel called "Myeongdong"** — check whether it is actually in Bukchang-dong or Sogong-dong

For sights, see [things to do around Gwanghwamun and City Hall](/en/posts/gwanghwamun-things-to-do-2026-09/); for food, [where to eat in Gwanghwamun, City Hall and Jonggak](/en/posts/gwanghwamun-food-2026-09/).

## What we could not confirm

**Room rates**: there is no official source, so we list none. Prices vary by booking site and date.

**Room counts differ between the two datasets** for some hotels. The general lodging list shows Lotte Hotel at 1,151 rooms, The Plaza at 479 and Koreana at 337, unlike the tourism list. This post uses the tourism list.

**Somerset Palace Seoul**: we confirmed only that its address matches the 284-room registration under a company branch name.

**Why Crown Park Hotel closed and re-registered**, and **what the New Kukje Hotel building is used for now**, could not be confirmed from official sources.

**Whether airport-bus stops move on Sejong-daero closure days** was not in the operators' notices.

**Route 6701's last bus**: the route summary (18:15) and the stop timetables (18:40–19:00) disagree. We also could not confirm the **last Line 1 train from Seoul Station to City Hall and Jonggak**.

## Summary

- The area has **55 tourist-accommodation licences with 4,552 rooms**, more than half of the rooms in **Sogong-dong and Bukchang-dong**.
- **Gwancheol-dong's 24 hostels are the most of any neighbourhood in Seoul**; 22 were registered after 2025.
- **Sejong-no, Seorin-dong and Doryeom-dong have no registered accommodation.**
- Four hotels named "Myeongdong" have **Bukchang-dong or Sogong-dong** addresses.
- **New Kukje Hotel closed in 2023**; Crown Park Hotel re-registered with the same room count in March 2026.
