On a rainy or bitterly cold day in Seoul, Lotte World Tower in Jamsil (잠실) is the one address where you can stay indoors all day and still feel you have seen the city. The same building holds Seoul Sky, an observatory about 500 m up, and Lotte World Aquarium down in the basement, and a combo pass sold at either box office covers both for ₩51,000 (about $38). Everything below comes from the two venues' official price lists, hour calendars and floor guides as of 26 October 2026.

## One building, two attractions

Lotte World Tower is 123 floors and 555 m tall, completed in December 2016. Seoul Sky occupies floors 117 to 123, but its ticket office and entrance are on B1 of the neighbouring Lotte World Mall, in the entertainment wing. From B2 a dedicated elevator called the Sky Shuttle takes you straight to floor 117. Lotte World Aquarium is on B1 and B2 of the same mall, so the two entrances are effectively on the same level.

![Lotte World Tower, Seokchon Lake, Magic Island and Lotte World Adventure in one aerial frame](/images/lotte-world-tower-seoul-sky-aquarium-2026-10/04.webp)

By subway, use Jamsil Station on [[line:2]] (exits 1 and 2) or [[line:8]] (exits 10 and 11); both connect underground to the mall. Lotte World Adventure, the theme park across Seokchon Lake, is served by the same station but sits on the opposite side, so plan your route in the morning if you want the park on the same day.

## Seoul Sky floor by floor

The official attraction list runs like this. B1 "Birth of Korea" is the waiting area before you board, and B2 Sky Platform is an exhibition zone. The Sky Shuttle drops you on floor 117, home to the Sky Show media hall. Floor 118 is the Sky Deck, where you stand on a glass floor. Floor 120 has the open-air Sky Terrace and a media table, and the Sky Mapping Show on floor 121 was marked as not operating at the time of writing. Floor 123, the top, is the 123 Lounge restaurant and bar.

![Lotte World Tower rising from the middle of Jamsil on a clear day, Han River to the left](/images/lotte-world-tower-seoul-sky-aquarium-2026-10/01.webp)

There is a security check at the entrance, and the official visitor guide is specific about what cannot come in. Any wheeled suitcase is out regardless of size, and bags must fit carry-on dimensions (43 cm × 25 cm × 22 cm). Strollers, bicycles, wheeled shoes and other rides are not allowed, tripods and selfie sticks cannot be used, and outside food cannot be brought in or eaten. Families should know in advance that a stroller cannot go up with you.

Children under 36 months enter free with a guardian and proof of age. Standard tickets go on sale online from the 1st of the month of your visit, and timed tickets require you to be at the observatory by the printed time. Screenshots and photocopies of tickets are not accepted.

The official deals page, as of writing, also lists a "SO SWEET NIGHT" ticket for two after 18:00 at ₩49,000 (list price ₩66,000), a student rate of ₩24,500, a senior rate of ₩25,500 and an advance-booking rate of ₩31,500, all running until 31 December. Visitors with a disability and national merit holders get 30% off for themselves and one companion. Discounts cannot be combined, so pick one.

## Sky Bridge tour and 123 Lounge

The Sky Bridge tour climbs about 50 m of stairs from floor 121 to cross a bridge at 541 m. Each run takes 12 people and lasts an hour, and participants must be at least 140 cm tall, under 120 kg and 12 or older; under-14s need a guardian and a legal guardian's consent. Runs leave Monday to Thursday at 13:00, 15:00, 17:00 and 19:00, and Friday to Sunday and holidays every hour on the hour from 13:00 to 19:00, weather permitting. Online booking opens one month ahead and is not available on the day; walk-up sales of leftover slots are at the B1 ticket office or the Sky Station on floor 117. The official winter closure is 30 November 2026 to 28 February 2027, so an autumn visit has to happen before the end of November.

123 Lounge opens Sunday to Thursday 10:30–21:50 and Friday, Saturday and the day before a holiday 10:30–22:50. Some set menus include observatory entry: the weekday 123 Lunch Special for two (10:30–15:00, sold on site) and the Monday-to-Thursday 123 Beverage for one (10:30–16:00, one drink) are listed with free observatory admission. The two- and three-person 123 Special is reservation-only through the CatchTable app. Prices are not published on the site, so check on the spot or call 02-3213-8019.

## Lotte World Aquarium: zones and highlights

![Indoor atrium and escalators of Lotte World Mall](/images/lotte-world-tower-seoul-sky-aquarium-2026-10/07.webp)

According to the official introduction, the aquarium divides the world's oceans into 13 themed zones and walks you from river to coast to open sea. The centrepiece is the main tank, The Ocean, described as Korea's largest habitat tank at 25 m wide, home to sand tiger sharks, black rays and cownose rays. In the order the floor guide lists them:

- Capybara Zone: opened at the end of December 2025, with a feeding "ASMR" talk twice a day.
- Nature Zone and Life Zone: blood parrot cichlids, amphibians and reptiles, electric eels, piranhas and lungfish.
- Rainforest River: arapaima, the Amazon giant that grows to 2–3 m.
- Sea Lion Zone: California sea lions and harbor seals, with a daily talk showing how the sea lions' health checks are done.
- The Ocean, Coral Garden and Play Ocean: the main tank, a reef tank, and a zone where you can follow the breeding of whitespotted bamboo sharks.
- Polar Zone, Freshwater Zone and Sea Turtle Zone: Humboldt penguins, Asian small-clawed otters and an endangered hawksbill sea turtle.

![Moon jellyfish drifting under purple light](/images/lotte-world-tower-seoul-sky-aquarium-2026-10/13.webp)

A new space on B2, the Aqua Gallery "Mulbit Garden", opened on 16 July 2026, and a 100-day anniversary rate of ₩22,000 was running from 19 to 31 October at the time of writing.

![An orange angelfish swimming through water plants](/images/lotte-world-tower-seoul-sky-aquarium-2026-10/12.webp)

## What about Bella the beluga?

In a notice dated 15 January 2026, Lotte World Aquarium said it is preparing to move its beluga to a new sea habitat for her safety and health, and is reviewing three countries as priority candidates in discussion with government agencies and whale experts. The beluga does not appear on the official list of featured animals as of writing, so do not plan a visit around seeing her.

Infants under 36 months are free (groups excluded, proof required). Since each venue stops entry an hour before closing, the roomier order is the aquarium first (it closes at 20:00 Monday to Thursday) and Seoul Sky later (22:00 or 23:00). The venue that sold you the combo also gives one parking discount on the day.

## Autumn only: Sanrio's Mermaid Party and T1

From 4 September to 29 November the aquarium hosts a Sanrio Characters collaboration called "Mermaid Party", with photo zones at the B1 entrance and the B2 marine gallery and sea turtle zone, plus a separate AR mission tour package (₩28,000) and a food package (₩28,500). Over the same weeks (11 September to 29 November) Seoul Sky runs "T1 IN THE SKY", an exhibition with the esports team T1, a T1 collaboration package at ₩33,000 and a 2026 World Championship cheering promotion (20 October to 29 November, ₩23,500). A hanbok pass at ₩21,400 is listed for Hanbok Culture Week, 19 to 31 October, and the "Sowondang" wish-writing event runs to 31 December.

## Practical notes

- Getting there: Jamsil Station on [[line:2]] (exits 1 and 2) or [[line:8]] (exits 10 and 11), connected underground to Lotte World Mall. Trunk buses [[bus:302]] [[bus:303]] [[bus:320]] [[bus:341]] [[bus:422]] and airport buses [[bus:6006]] [[bus:6200]] stop around Jamsil.
- Parking: the Lotte World Mall garage charges ₩600 per 10 minutes on weekdays (10:00–20:00), ₩800 per 10 minutes on weekends and holidays, and ₩200 per 10 minutes from 20:00 to 10:00 the next morning. Pick up a parking voucher at the entry gate and the first four hours cost ₩4,800; beyond four hours the normal rate applies. It is a Hi-pass (electronic toll tag) garage, so if you drive out without paying at a kiosk first, the full rate is charged automatically.
- Last entry: both venues stop ticket sales and entry one hour before closing. Seoul Sky closes at 23:00 on Friday and Saturday and the aquarium at 22:00 Friday to Sunday, so evening plans are easier at the weekend.
- Bag rules: Seoul Sky restricts suitcases, large bags, strollers, tripods and outside food. The aquarium publishes no such list.
- Timing: a combo pass must be used at both venues on one day, so set the aquarium around the talk schedule and go up to Seoul Sky around sunset.
- Prices and hours are from the official sites on 26 October 2026, and most deals end on 31 December, so check again after that.
