Eating in Seongsu-dong tends to go one of two ways. You queue at a **warehouse cafe or bakery on Yeonmujang-gil**, or you sit down to **gamjatang, jokbal or galbi in the older lanes behind Ttukseom and Seongsu stations**. In between are a chicken-soup counter and a taco bar picked by MICHELIN, and a noodle shop where the government publishes the ₩5,500 (about $4) price of a bowl of jajangmyeon.

The short version: **the MICHELIN Guide Seoul 2026 lists five restaurants in Seongdong-gu**, and three of them are within reach of Seongsu: the one-star Tutoiement and the Bib Gourmands Gyewol and Mattdol. For older places there is **Somunnan Seongsu Gamjatang, open since 1983 and around the clock**, and Seongsu Jokbal, which the Korea Tourism Organization (KTO) says is known as one of Seoul's "big three" jokbal houses. Nineteen of the restaurants Seongdong-gu picked for its 2025 "Seongdong Matjip" list are in Seongsu-dong. Every shop below was checked by name and address against MICHELIN, Seongdong-gu, the Ministry of the Interior and Safety, KTO or a dated news report, and anything about flavour is taken from those write-ups.

## What this guide covers

This guide covers **Seongsu-dong 1-ga and 2-ga, Seoul Forest and the streets around Ttukseom Station**. Places just over the line in **Songjeong-dong**, directly north of Seongsu-dong 2-ga, are included and labelled, including the one-star Tutoiement.

**The Konkuk University Station side is Gwangjin-gu and is left out**, which also rules out the Bib Gourmand Jeongmyeon (Neungdong-ro 13-gil, Gwangjin-gu). Majang-dong and Wangsimni are in Seongdong-gu but outside the walking area here, so only their MICHELIN entries get a short mention.

For sights, see [things to do in Seongsu](/en/posts/seongsu-things-to-do-2026-09/). For the whole area, see the [Seongsu travel guide](/en/posts/seongsu-guide-2026-09/).

**Gyewol and Mattdol are both on the Seongdeokjeong-gil streets**, within walking distance of each other. The Bib Gourmand is MICHELIN's mark for a meal at **₩45,000 or less per person**, and **the ₩ symbols are bands, not amounts**: ₩ is the lowest and ₩₩₩₩ the highest.

Tutoiement's neighbourhood comes from looking up the MICHELIN page's coordinates in OpenStreetMap. Songjeong-dong borders Seongsu-dong 2-ga, so it is easy to fit into a Seongsu day, but the address is not Seongsu-dong.

### Gyewol: Bib Gourmand chicken gomtang

![A white bowl of kalguksu noodle soup on a wooden table, four dumplings, minced-meat garnish and sliced spring onion on a clear broth](/images/seongsu-food-2026-09/05.webp)

The photo above is a generic bowl of kalguksu, not food from Gyewol or any noodle shop in this guide.

**Seongdeokjeong 3-gil 8.** MICHELIN describes a specialist in Korean chicken gomtang (a clear soup served with rice): low-temperature-cooked chicken breast over rice, blanched young cabbage and a clear broth with the fat skimmed off. At lunch it adds a spicy chicken salad and chicken suyuk brushed with doenjang (soybean paste) and torched, and in summer there is a cold noodle dish.

KTO data says **the whole shop is an 8-seat counter**, and the signatures are the clear baek-gomtang and the spicy hong-gomtang. DiningCode lists baek-gomtang at ₩11,000, hong-gomtang at ₩12,000, and the lunch-only side dishes at ₩8,000 each.

Hours differ by source. **The MICHELIN page shows 11:00–14:30 daily only**, while KTO data and DiningCode give **11:00–21:00 with a break from 15:00 to 17:00**. Check the shop's Instagram before planning dinner here.

**Seongsu Mangchi Burger shares the address.** KTO lists it at Seongdeokjeong 3-gil 8 too, with the mangchi burger, the spicy bul-mangchi burger and a green-onion bulgogi burger.

### Mattdol: hand-pressed tortillas

![Three corn-tortilla tacos on a white plate, chopped meat heaped with diced onion and coriander, with lime and radish on the side](/images/seongsu-food-2026-09/06.webp)

The photo above shows generic tacos from a US food truck, not food from Mattdol.

**Seongdeokjeong-gil 63.** MICHELIN introduces chef Lee Chang-yoon's Mexican restaurant and says you can often see him **pressing masa by hand into tortillas**, the way he learned in Mexico. It mentions tostadas and tacos built around Korean seasonal produce.

The MICHELIN page shows **Tuesday to Saturday, 18:00–21:00, closed Sunday and Monday**, so it is dinner only. DiningCode lists a six-taco tasting course at ₩39,000 with a "reservation required" tag.

### Tutoiement: one-star French

**Gwangnaru-ro 11-gil 20.** According to MICHELIN, *tutoiement* is the French for addressing someone informally, and chef Kim Do-hyun chose it because he wants to talk to guests comfortably rather than formally. MICHELIN singles out the way each plate is composed with its sauce in mind.

DiningCode lists **dinner from 19:00 to 21:30** with a seasonal course and points to the CatchTable app for bookings. The MICHELIN page does not show hours.

## Pop-up stores and food pop-ups

Seongsu's pop-ups disappear too fast to name, so here are the numbers instead.

- According to Seongdong-gu, **883 pop-up stores ran in and around Seongsu-dong in 2025** (press release, 27 January 2026)
- Citing a report by pop-up agency Sweetspot, Newsis (18 September 2026) put **nationwide pop-ups in the first half of 2026 at 2,130**, up 44.9% on a year earlier (1,470), and said **food and drink, including alcohol and desserts**, is taking a bigger share than in the early fashion-and-beauty days
- But **Seongdong-gu's share of all pop-ups fell from 34.9% in the first half of 2025 to 26.0% in the first half of 2026**, and pop-ups on Yeonmujang-gil fell from 233 to 196, down 15.9% (Hankyung Magazine, 27 July 2026)

**Food pop-ups run for days or a few weeks**, so check the brand's own channels just before you go.

### The co-existence agreements stopped growing after 2021

In 2015 Seongdong-gu passed **what it calls the country's first anti-gentrification ordinance** and promoted **co-existence agreements** in which landlords and tenants voluntarily agree to keep rent rises modest. Its survey published in February 2022 (September–December 2021, 767 businesses) found:

| | With agreement | Without |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Average time in business | 79 months | 52 months |
| Rent increase | 2.49% | 2.85% |
| Rent per pyeong | ₩103,900 | ₩106,600 |

By the end of 2021, **73.3%** of landlords in the Seoul Forest-gil, Bangsongdae-gil and Sangwon-gil zones and **501 buildings** across the district had signed. The gap in time in business is a difference in averages, and it cannot be put down to the agreements alone.

The update comes from Edaily (23 September 2026): a Seongdong-gu official said many joined voluntarily at the start, "but no one has signed up since".

## Good to know

**Some places only do dinner.** Mattdol opens Tuesday to Saturday from 18:00 to 21:00, and Tutoiement's course starts at 19:00 per DiningCode. Eldot, on the other hand, lists weekday lunch only.

**Afternoon breaks are common.** Gyewol, Seongsu Jokbal, Daeseong Galbi, Gajoku and Halmeoni-ui Recipe all close for a break around 15:00. Korean restaurants often shut between lunch and dinner service. Somunnan Seongsu Gamjatang is open 24 hours, so timing matters less there.

**Takeaway.** KTO data says both Somunnan Seongsu Gamjatang and Seongsu Jokbal do takeaway, which is worth knowing if you plan to eat in Seoul Forest.

**Ask about the broth.** Gamjatang, gomtang and noodle soups usually use meat or anchovy stock. If you are vegetarian or have allergies, ask before ordering.

## Wrapping up

- **MICHELIN Guide Seoul 2026**: five entries in Seongdong-gu. Around Seongsu are the Bib Gourmands Gyewol (chicken gomtang) and Mattdol (tacos), and the one-star Tutoiement (Songjeong-dong).
