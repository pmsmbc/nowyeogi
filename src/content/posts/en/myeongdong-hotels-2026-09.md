Search for a hotel in Myeongdong and dozens of names come back with "Myeongdong" in them. Counting 14 neighbourhoods (*dong*) with Seoul's official licensing data gives a clearer picture:

- **69 tourist lodgings with 6,399 registered rooms**: 29 tourist hotels and 40 hostels
- **More than half the rooms (3,543) are in Myeongdong 1·2-ga and Chungmuro 1·2-ga**, between the shopping streets and Myeongdong Station
- **Namsan-dong 2-ga's 16 hostels rank fourth of all Seoul neighbourhoods**, and its 72 homestays are the most in Jung-gu
- Several big "Myeongdong" hotels, including **Lotte City Hotel Myeongdong, Four Points by Sheraton Josun Myeongdong, Nine Tree Myeongdong II and Sotetsu The Splaisir Myeongdong, have addresses outside this area**

In short, Myeongdong's big hotels sit **inside the shopping district and on Toegye-ro by Myeongdong Station**, plus **near Sungnyemun Gate (Namdaemun-ro 4-ga) and Hoehyeon Station**. The slopes towards Namsan, **Namsan-dong 2-ga and Hoehyeon-dong, are hostel and homestay territory**. If you are still choosing between neighbourhoods, [where to stay in Seoul](/en/posts/seoul-where-to-stay-2026-09/) compares them side by side.

## How these were counted

We downloaded four licensing datasets from the Seoul Open Data Plaza (tourist lodging, general lodging, urban homestays for foreign tourists, and hanok stays) and kept only businesses marked as open whose **legal-dong address is one of the 14 below**. The tourist and general lodging files are the 24 September 2026 updates, homestays 27 September and hanok stays 23 September. The data and method are the same as in our [Dongdaemun](/en/posts/dongdaemun-hotels-2026-10/) and [Hongdae](/en/posts/hongdae-hotels-2026-09/) hotel guides.

### How big Myeongdong is within Seoul

As our [Euljiro hotels guide](/en/posts/euljiro-hotels-2026-09/) found, **Jung-gu holds about 25% of all lodging rooms in Seoul**. Recounted with the same tourist lodging dataset:

- Seoul has 814 open tourist lodgings, 56,119 rooms where counts are listed; **Jung-gu accounts for 216 of them and 18,467 rooms**
- Myeongdong's 6,399 rooms are **about 35% of Jung-gu and 11% of Seoul**
- By rooms per legal dong, **Chungmuro 2-ga ranks 6th (1,267), Myeongdong 1-ga 8th (1,050), Namdaemun-ro 4-ga 12th (997) and Hoehyeon-dong 1-ga 14th (932)**. First is Hangangno 3-ga in Yongsan (1,968), second Sogong-dong (1,653)

So no single Myeongdong *dong* is Seoul's biggest hotel neighbourhood, but **taken together the 14 form one of the largest clusters in the city**.

## 150 homestays, all on the Namsan side

The "urban homestay for foreign tourists" licence is defined in the Tourism Promotion Act's enforcement decree as **residents offering rooms in their own home to foreign visitors**, so **Korean nationals generally cannot book them**. The rules are explained in our [Yeonnam-dong hotels guide](/en/posts/yeonnam-hotels-2026-09/).

All 150 homestays in the 14 dong are **in Namsan-dong and Hoehyeon-dong**. **Myeongdong 1·2-ga, Chungmuro 1·2-ga and Namdaemun-ro have none**; they are all shops and main roads.

Namsan-dong and Hoehyeon-dong are **lanes climbing towards Namsan**. Homestays rarely have lifts or a 24-hour desk, so if you arrive late with luggage, get check-in instructions in advance and look at the slope on a map.

## Getting to the airport

Myeongdong has **two airport bus routes sharing one stop**. [[bus:6001]] comes along Toegye-ro from Dongdaemun and continues via Myeongdong Station and the Bank of Korea to Seoul Station; [[bus:6015]] loops through Euljiro and then calls at Myeongdong and Hoehyeon stations before heading to Chungjeongno and Gongdeok. Both cost **₩17,000 for adults (about $13) and ₩12,000 for children**, and the official pages say **first come first served, no online booking, pay by transit card or cash**.

## Which to choose

- **Big hotel right by the airport bus** — L7 Myeongdong, Sejong Hotel or Skypark 3 on Toegye-ro by Myeongdong Station, or the Seoul Prince across the road (Myeongdong Station stop for 6001 and 6015); voco or L'Escape by Hoehyeon Station (6015)
- **In the middle of the shopping streets** — Skypark Central, Solaria Nishitetsu, Royal Hotel, Ibis Ambassador or Aloft in Myeongdong 1·2-ga
- **Near Namdaemun Market and Seoul Station** — Courtyard, Gracery or Fraser Place in Namdaemun-ro 4-ga. Close to the AREX at Seoul Station, a longer walk to the shopping streets
- **Budget, travelling solo** — hostels on Toegye-ro 18-gil and 20-gil in Namsan-dong 2-ga. Across the area 21 of 40 hostels date from 2025 or later, so recent reviews may be thin
- **A foreign visitor who wants a residential lane** — homestays in Namsan-dong 2-ga and Hoehyeon-dong; Koreans generally cannot book them, and the streets are steep
- **Late-evening flight** — last airport buses leave around 20:00, so plan on [[line:4]] to Seoul Station and the airport railway
- **Anything named "Myeongdong"** — check whether the address is actually Jang-gyo-dong, Jeo-dong, Cho-dong, Bukchang-dong or Sogong-dong

## What the earlier version got wrong

The first version of this page (10 September 2026) had errors.

**First, five of the "Myeongdong hotels" it recommended are not in Myeongdong.** Lotte Hotel Seoul and The Westin Josun are in Sogong-dong, The Splaisir Myeongdong in Bukchang-dong, Lotte City Hotel Myeongdong in Jang-gyo-dong, and one of the two Nine Tree Myeongdong hotels (II) in Cho-dong. Both are now registered as "**Nine Tree by Parnas**", not "Nine Tree Premier".

**Second, no lodging is registered as "Loisir Hotel Seoul Myeongdong".** The building known by that name, 115 Toegye-ro, is now licensed as two **general hotels**, Myeongdong Migliore BM Hotel and Mohenic Hotel Seoul Myeongdong. The pool mentioned in the earlier version could not be confirmed from an official source and has been removed.

**Third, there was no basis for "the Namdaemun-ro hotels are older buildings with roomier layouts".** The three big Namdaemun-ro 4-ga hotels were registered in 2013–2018; the old hotels here are **the Savoy (1963), Seoul Prince (1969), Seoul Rex (1970), Sejong (1973) and Pacific (1975)** in Chungmuro, Namsan-dong and Hoehyeon-dong. Licensing data says nothing about room sizes.

**Fourth, the price bands and seasonal mark-ups were not official.** Figures such as "₩60,000–110,000" and "20–40% more in peak season" came from booking-site averages and have been removed.

## Summary

- Myeongdong's 14 dong have **69 tourist lodgings with 6,399 rooms**, 29 hotels and 40 hostels: about 11% of Seoul's tourist lodging rooms.
- The biggest are **voco (576), Courtyard (409), Gracery (336), Sejong (333), Solaria Nishitetsu (312), Skypark Central (312) and Royal (310)**.
- **Namsan-dong 2-ga's 16 hostels rank fourth in Seoul**, and all 150 homestays are in Namsan-dong and Hoehyeon-dong.
- Lotte City, Four Points, Nine Tree Myeongdong II and The Splaisir Myeongdong are **Myeongdong in name only**.
- **Millennium Hilton (2022) and Tmark Grand (2023, now the voco)** are recorded as closed.
- Airport buses **6001 (last 20:07) and 6015 (last 20:03)** share the Myeongdong Station stop; there is no night bus.
- From the airport railway, it is **two stops on [[line:4]] from Seoul Station**.
