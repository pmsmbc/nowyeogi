Seoul's dessert craze this fall comes down to one texture: chewy. It started with queues outside **Hanjeongsun**, a fruit-mochi shop from Seongsu-dong. Then the Belgian chocolatier Godiva launched its own mochi, and people began lining up before dawn. Add the Christmas cake pre-orders that opened in mid-November, and you have the three things Seoul is lining up for right now. This guide focuses on how to actually get your hands on them.

![White mochi cut in half with a whole strawberry inside](/images/seoul-trending-desserts-2026-11/01.webp)

## Hanjeongsun: fruit mochi from Seongsu

Hanjeongsun (한정선, 韓貞仙) opened its first shop in Seongsu in 2023. It makes *chapssaltteok*, the Korean sweet rice cake that is close to Japanese daifuku, stuffed with whole fresh fruit and cream. Cut one in half and you see the fruit in cross-section, which is the photo that spread across Instagram. Each piece comes wrapped in red, yellow, blue or green *hanji* (Korean mulberry paper), and the shops are styled like a hanok with white porcelain on display. Reports say it has become a regular stop for foreign visitors to Seongsu.

- **Flavors**: persimmon, fig, chocolate strawberry, red bean butter, whole tangerine, gold kiwi and a Dubai-style pistachio version, plus newer Greek-yogurt flavors with strawberry, mango or blueberry. The lineup changes with the season
- **Prices**: ₩4,000–6,000 (about $3–4.50) each, with the Dubai-style at ₩6,500 the most expensive. Gift boxes cost ₩32,000 for six and ₩45,000 for nine (as of writing). The boxes have fixed flavors, so you cannot mix your own
- **Storage**: they need refrigeration, and the shop recommends eating them the same day, within two days at most

Shops include the Seongsu flagship (43-1 Yeonmujang-gil), a branch on the 2nd floor of Connect Place inside Seoul Station, and food halls at Lotte World Mall, Shinsegae Gangnam, The Hyundai Seoul and Hyundai Trade Center. The Seongsu shop is mostly takeout and opens 10:30–22:00. Queues are long: one reporter found 141 groups already waiting ten minutes after the Lotte World Mall branch opened, and favorite flavors were sold out.

![Cafe terrace with parasols and trees on Seongsu-dong's cafe street](/images/seoul-trending-desserts-2026-11/02.webp)

If you would rather skip the line, try a convenience store. GS25, one of Korea's big convenience-store chains, launched Hanjeongsun Yogurt Chapssaltteok on September 3 for ₩3,900. It is sold frozen: eat it frozen and it is like sorbet, let it thaw and it turns chewy. It sold about 600,000 units in its first month, and a pistachio version followed in October.

## Godiva mochi: chocolate rice cakes, only in Jamsil

Godiva mochi launched on September 10, when Godiva opened its Lotte World Mall shop in Jamsil. Instead of red bean paste, the rice cake holds chocolate ganache with almond praline, fresh fruit such as strawberry, banana or tangerine, or gelato. Prices are ₩7,500 for praline, ₩8,500 for fresh fruit and ₩9,000 for gelato mochi (as of writing). Lotte Department Store says it is sold only at the Lotte World Mall shop for now.

In the first weeks, people queued from 4 or 5 a.m. and the day's waiting list closed around 6 a.m. Resellers even offered to buy one for a fee. Since October 29, the shop has used **CatchTable bookings** instead of an on-site line. CatchTable is Korea's main restaurant-booking app.

- **When bookings open**: 10:00 every day. Search "고디바 롯데월드몰점" (Godiva Lotte World Mall) in CatchTable. Same-day bookings only
- **Visit slots**: 10:30, 11:30, 12:30 or 13:30, one slot per person, one booking per phone number
- **Limit**: four mochi per person
- **Watch out**: the booking is cancelled if you arrive more than 30 minutes late. Even with a booking, you may wait 30 minutes to an hour to order, and some flavors sell out early

Gelato mochi melts fast. If you have a long trip back, praline or fruit mochi travels better.

## Christmas cakes: pre-orders start mid-November

In Korea, Christmas cake is a big seasonal purchase, and bakery chains and hotels compete with new designs each year. Booking early usually gets you a discount. These are this year's headline cakes.

- **Twosome Place "Cream-top Strawberry Choco" (크초생)**: the chain's best-selling strawberry chocolate cream cake topped with a thick layer of vanilla-bean mascarpone cream, launched November 13. Pre-order in the Twosome Heart app from November 11–18 for a first-come 20% coupon, and pick it up on any day from December 1–31. Not every branch sells it
- **Tous les Jours "Holiday Strawberry Queen"**: a cake that glows in the dark when you turn off the lights. There is also the "Holiday Black Queen" with strawberry sauce and a bear-shaped mousse cake. Booking any of 35 holiday items in the Tous les Jours app until December 22 gets up to 30% off, with pickup December 18–25
- **Conrad Seoul**: five whole cakes from ₩105,000 to ₩135,000, led by the "Signature Tree", shaped after the hotel's spiral staircase and ginkgo leaves. Six mini cakes cost ₩25,000 each
- **Grand Walkerhill Seoul**: the "Lumière Golden Noël", topped with a 0.5 g pure-gold business-card ornament, is this year's priciest at ₩550,000 (about $410). The nougat-and-nut mini cake "Lumière de Noël" costs ₩95,000. Naver bookings until December 4 get 5% off the Golden Noël and 10% off the de Noël. The de Noël is sold December 1–25 at Le Passage on the 1st floor, and the Golden Noël is picked up December 22–25

JW Marriott Hotel Seoul's "Teddy Bear Christmas" (₩450,000) and JW Marriott Dongdaemun Square Seoul's "The Sound of Noël" (₩400,000) are also on the list.

## Practical notes

- **Getting there**: Hanjeongsun's Seongsu flagship is walkable from Seongsu Station on [[line:2]]. Lotte World Mall connects directly to Jamsil Station on [[line:2]] and [[line:8]], so you can visit Hanjeongsun's Jamsil branch and Godiva on one trip
- **Budget**: two or three Hanjeongsun mochi run ₩10,000–20,000. Four Godiva mochi, the maximum, cost about ₩30,000–36,000
- **Queue tips**: reviews mention waiting about 40 minutes even when arriving at opening. The Lotte World Mall branch of Hanjeongsun uses a kiosk waiting list, so you can browse other shops while you wait. For Godiva, have the app open before bookings go live at 10:00
- **Gifts**: the mochi are perishable, so they are best eaten in Seoul rather than packed for a flight home

## Wrapping up

For fruit mochi, go to **Hanjeongsun**. For chocolate mochi, book **Godiva** in Jamsil. For a holiday cake, order inside the pre-order discount window.
