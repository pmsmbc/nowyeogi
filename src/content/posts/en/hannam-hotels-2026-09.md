Itaewon feels like a hotel district, with big names such as Grand Hyatt and Mondrian on the skyline. Seoul's licensing data tells a more lopsided story:

- **Registered hotels and hostels in Itaewon-dong and Hannam-dong: 10.** Only **four** have more than 100 rooms
- **Licensed urban homestays for foreign tourists in the same two neighbourhoods: 194.** Itaewon-dong's 173 put it **seventh among all of Seoul's neighbourhoods**

In short, you are choosing between **a handful of large hotels and a couple of hundred small rooms in people's homes**. If you want hotels sorted by budget, see [where to stay in Itaewon and Hannam-dong by budget](/en/posts/itaewon-hotels-2026-09/). This post sticks to the data and the airport run.

## How we counted

We downloaded four licensing datasets from the Seoul Open Data Plaza and kept every business whose address is in **Itaewon-dong, Hannam-dong, Yongsan-dong 2-ga (Haebangchon) or Bogwang-dong** in Yongsan-gu. The homestay data is the 27 September 2026 update; lodging and tourist accommodation are from 24 September. The two administrative wards Itaewon 1-dong and 2-dong are both "Itaewon-dong" in these addresses.

| Licence type (law) | Itaewon-dong | Hannam-dong | Haebangchon | Bogwang-dong |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Tourist accommodation: tourist hotels, hostels (Tourism Promotion Act) | **8** | **2** | 0 | 0 |
| Inns (yeogwan) under the Public Health Control Act | 6 | 1 | 0 | 2 |
| Hanok stays | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| **Urban homestay for foreign tourists** (Tourism Promotion Act) | **173** | **21** | **52** | **14** |

The big hotels also appear in the general lodging list as "tourist hotels", so adding the two lists would double-count them. The inn row counts only the traditional *yeogwan* that are not in the tourism list.

- **Grand Hyatt Seoul** sits on the southern slope of Namsan on Sowol-ro in Hannam-dong. It is **the largest hotel in the area** by far, and an airport bus stop is named after it

- **Imperial Palace Boutique Hotel** is at 221 Itaewon-ro, between Itaewon and Hangangjin stations

The other six are hostels and small hotels with fewer than 30 rooms, and **three of the hostels were registered in 2024–2025**. Two of them are on the same alley, Bogwang-ro 59-gil.

### Two hotels that are gone

- **Crown Hotel** (140 Noksapyeong-daero, 203 rooms, registered 1980) **closed in June 2022**. Edaily reported on 6 May 2026 that a **25-storey mixed-use block of about 150 homes** was due to break ground on the site in June 2026, with completion planned for October 2029
- **I.T.W Hotel** (11 Usadan-ro 14-gil, 71 rooms) closed in February 2022, and **Nouvelle Seoul Hotel, also 71 rooms, was registered at the same address in September 2023**. It looks like the same building under a new name and operator

### The inns are mostly from the 1960s–80s

Seven of the nine inns were licensed between 1965 and 1982. "Stay Passport Itaewon Hotel & Spa" on Itaewon-ro 17-gil is, on paper, **a four-room inn licensed in 1965**. "Hannam 67 Hotel" (67 Daesagwan-ro) is also registered as an inn, with 35 rooms.

In other words, **"hotel" in a name does not mean a licensed tourist hotel**. Check photos and recent reviews to set expectations. Hannam-dong has five closed inns on record.

### One or two rooms in a house

- Of the 118 Itaewon-dong homestays with a room count, **65 have one room and 43 have two**
- Buildings are **63 multi-household houses, 29 detached houses and 16 multi-unit villas**, plus a single apartment
- The median registered floor area is about 61 m² in Itaewon-dong and 55 m² in Haebangchon

## Homestays are for foreign tourists only

Article 2 of the Enforcement Decree of the Tourism Promotion Act (version in force from 4 August 2026) defines this licence as **a resident of a city** offering lodging **in the home they live in** to **foreign tourists**.

1. **Korean nationals generally cannot stay.** If you are travelling with a Korean citizen, look at the 10 hotels and hostels or the inns instead
2. **The host must live there.** Renting out an empty flat is not legal under this licence

Airbnb required **all existing Korean listings to submit a business registration from 16 October 2025**, and said listings without one **could not take bookings from 1 January 2026**. The platform may not stop a Korean national from booking a homestay, though, so **ask the host what licence they hold** if that applies to you.

### Staying on a residential street

- **Haebangchon is on the steep southern slope of Namsan**, with plenty of stepped alleys. With a big suitcase, ask about the gradient, the floor and whether there is a lift before you book
- **The neighbours are real residents**, and so is your host. Late-night chatter and suitcase wheels carry
- **There is no 24-hour front desk.** Get check-in instructions in advance if you land late

## The Hannam 3 redevelopment

South of Usadan-ro, the **Hannam 3 redevelopment zone** (around 686 Hannam-dong) is being rebuilt. Asia Economy reported on 16 September 2026 that **demolition was 99% complete** and that the zone will hold **5,970 homes** in 123 buildings of up to 21 storeys, with a shaded, planted walkway along Usadan-ro.

If your room is south of Usadan-ro, **check on a map how close it is to the site**. Nouvelle Seoul Hotel and several inns are on lanes that branch off Usadan-ro.

## Getting to and from the airport

**AREX, the airport railway, does not run through Itaewon or Hannam-dong.** Two airport bus routes do.

### Airport buses

Where to board **towards the airport**:

| Route | Stop (number) | Fare | First | Last |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [[bus:6702]] K Limousine | Namsan (Grand Hyatt Seoul) (03680) | 18,000 won (about $13.50) | 04:26 | **20:18** |
| [[bus:6702]] K Limousine | Itaewon 2-dong Community Center (03343) | 18,000 won | 04:28 | **20:20** |
| [[bus:6010]] Airport Limousine | Hannam-dong, Cheongsan Building (03752) | 17,000 won | 04:30 | **20:54** |
| [[bus:6010]] Airport Limousine | Hannam Station (03736) | 17,000 won | 04:32 | **20:56** |

Bus 6702 runs 25 times a day and 6010 runs 19. **Coming from the airport**, 6702 also stops at Grand Hyatt and Itaewon 2-dong Community Center, leaving **T1 from 06:05 to 23:05 and T2 from 05:45 to 22:45**. Fares are adult fares, and both operators say times can change with traffic.

Hannam Station is on the [[line:gyeongui]]. Bus 6010's stops are on the Hannam-daero and Hannam Station side, so it suits rooms in Hannam-dong better than rooms around Itaewon Station.

### AREX with one change

To use [[line:arex]], the simplest route is **to change to [[line:6]] at Gongdeok**. From Gongdeok, Line 6 runs Hyochang Park, Samgakji and Noksapyeong, then Itaewon (fourth stop) and Hangangjin (fifth).

| Journey | Fare (transit card, AREX section) | First | Last |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Gongdeok → Incheon Airport T1 | 4,650 won (about $3.50) | 05:24 | **23:44** |
| Gongdeok → Incheon Airport T2 | 5,250 won | 05:24 | **23:44** |

Weekday and weekend times are the same. **The 23:22, 23:54 and 00:04 trains from Gongdeok terminate at Geomam**, well short of the airport, so do not board them by mistake.

**Coming from the airport**, the last all-stop trains to Seoul Station are **23:38 from T1 and 23:32 from T2**. The 23:56 from T1 only goes as far as Digital Media City. **We could not confirm whether Line 6 is still running when that last train reaches Gongdeok**, so on a late arrival plan for a taxi.

More options are in [how to get from Incheon Airport to Seoul](/en/posts/incheon-airport-to-seoul-2026-09/).

## Which to pick

- **Foreign visitor who wants a neighbourhood feel**: an Itaewon-dong homestay. It has the seventh-highest count in Seoul
- **Newer homestays with Namsan views**: Haebangchon. 38 of 52 opened since 2025, but the slopes are steep
- **Travelling with a Korean national**: one of the 10 hotels and hostels, or an inn
- **One bus to the airport**: near Grand Hyatt or Itaewon 2-dong, take 6702; near Hannam-daero or Hannam Station, take 6010
- **Right by Itaewon Station**: Hamilton Hotel, A1 Hotel and Hostel Fellaz are all around Itaewon-ro

For food, see [where to eat in Itaewon and Hannam-dong](/en/posts/hannam-food-2026-09/).

## What we could not confirm

**Prices.** There is no official source, and hotel and homestay rates vary by site and date.

**Whether all nine inns are really operating.** We only confirmed that the licensing data lists them as active. We also could not confirm **whether the inns on the Usadan-ro lanes fall inside the Hannam 3 demolition area**.

**Whether construction has actually started on the Crown Hotel site.** The Edaily report gave a target date.

**Which earlier hotel Mondrian Seoul Itaewon's 1998 licence came from.**

**Other airport buses through Hannam-dong and Hangangjin, such as route 6030.** Only blog posts say it is suspended; we could not check its status on an operator's page, so we left it out.

**Line 6's last departure from Gongdeok towards Itaewon.** We could not check the official timetable.

## Wrapping up

- **Itaewon-dong has 8 registered hotels and hostels, Hannam-dong 2.** Only Grand Hyatt (620 rooms), Mondrian (297), Hamilton (166) and Imperial Palace (128) have more than 100 rooms.
- **Crown Hotel closed in 2022**, and a 25-storey residential block is planned on the site.
- **Itaewon-dong's 173 homestays rank seventh in Seoul**, and 76 were licensed in 2025 or later.
- **In Haebangchon, 38 of 52 homestays** are from 2025 or later.
- Homestays are **in the host's own home** and **for foreign tourists only**.
- Airport buses: **6702 (last 20:18 at Grand Hyatt, 20:20 at Itaewon 2-dong)** and **6010 (last 20:54 at Hannam-dong, 20:56 at Hannam Station)**.
- By rail, **change to Line 6 at Gongdeok**. The last AREX train from Gongdeok to the airport is **23:44**.
