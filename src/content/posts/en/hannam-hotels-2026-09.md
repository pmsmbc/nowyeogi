Hannam-dong has fewer places to sleep than its reputation suggests. Count it in Seoul's licensing data and you get:

- **Two tourist hotels with 748 rooms** — Grand Hyatt Seoul (620) and Imperial Palace Boutique Hotel (128)
- **One inn** (35 rooms)
- **21 urban homestays for foreign tourists.** Of the 42 records the data keeps for Hannam-dong, half are closed

Bogwang-dong next door adds two inns and 14 homestays. In short, **Hannam-dong is a neighbourhood of two big hotels and very little else.** The hotels and homestays of Itaewon-dong and Haebangchon, west of Itaewon Station, have moved to [Where to stay in Itaewon and Haebangchon](/en/posts/itaewon-hotels-2026-09/). This guide covers Hannam-dong and Bogwang-dong, the Hannam 3 redevelopment and the trip to the airport.

## How these were counted

The counts use four licensing datasets on Seoul Open Data Plaza, keeping only businesses marked "open" whose **legal-dong address is Hannam-dong or Bogwang-dong**. Tourist accommodation and lodging data are the 24 September 2026 update, homestays the 27 September update and hanok stays the 23 September update — the same sources and method as the [Itaewon and Haebangchon](/en/posts/itaewon-hotels-2026-09/) and [Bukchon](/en/posts/bukchon-hotels-2026-09/) guides.

| Dataset (law) | Hannam-dong | Bogwang-dong |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tourist accommodation — tourist hotels, hostels (Tourism Promotion Act) | **2** | 0 |
| Inns (yeogwan) in the lodging data (Public Health Control Act) | 1 | 2 |
| Hanok stays (Tourism Promotion Act) | 0 | 0 |
| **Urban homestays for foreign tourists** (Tourism Promotion Act) | **21** | **14** |

Big hotels appear in the lodging data a second time as "tourist hotels", so adding the two lists would double count. The inn row only counts **yeogwan** that are not in the tourist list.

Neighbouring dongs are split like this:

- **Itaewon-dong (Itaewon 1-dong and 2-dong) and Yongsan-dong 2-ga (Haebangchon)** — 8 hotels and hostels, 6 inns, 225 homestays. [Where to stay in Itaewon and Haebangchon](/en/posts/itaewon-hotels-2026-09/)
- Itaewon-dong and Haebangchon are counted in [where to stay in Itaewon and Haebangchon](/en/posts/itaewon-hotels-2026-09/)

Among all of Seoul's neighbourhoods by homestay count, **Hannam-dong ranks 79th and Bogwang-dong 109th**. Itaewon-dong, in the same district, is 7th with 173.

### Grand Hyatt Seoul

- On Namsan's southern slope at **322 Sowol-ro, Hannam-dong**. It is close to Itaewon 2-dong, but the address is Hannam-dong
- Registered with **620 rooms** in the tourist data and 607 in the lodging data. The Korea Tourism Organization describes it as having **615 rooms**. The sources differ by a few rooms; this guide uses the licensing figure
- According to the Korea Tourism Organization, it has **indoor and outdoor pools, tennis and squash courts, a sauna, an ice rink** and a spa
- **It has the second-largest room count of any tourist hotel in Yongsan-gu**, after the Novotel Ambassador Seoul Yongsan (621) in Hangangno 3-ga
- Airport bus [[bus:6702]] has a stop named after it, "Namsan (Grand Hyatt Seoul)" (see "Getting to the airport")

![The outdoor pool at Grand Hyatt Seoul seen from above, with rows of sun loungers, garden trees and a tennis court at the top left](/images/hannam-hotels-2026-09/07.webp)

### Imperial Palace Boutique Hotel

- At **221 Itaewon-ro**, "between Itaewon and Hangangjin stations" in the words of its official site. The address is Hannam-dong
- Registered with **128 rooms** in the tourist data and 121 (9 Korean-style, 112 Western-style) in the lodging data
- The official site lists room types from Superior and Deluxe to suites, plus a restaurant, a conference room and a fitness room

### One inn, and the inns that closed

The only inn operating in Hannam-dong is **"Hannam 67 Hotel"** (한남67호텔, 67 Daesagwan-ro), licensed in 1982 with **35 rooms** (5 Korean-style, 30 Western-style). The name says hotel, but it is licensed as an inn, not a tourist hotel.

The lodging data keeps **13 closed records** for Hannam-dong, mostly inns licensed between 1966 and 1983. Five still carry a street address (Dokseodang-ro 14-gil, Usadan-ro 14-gil, Daesagwan-ro 30ga-gil, Daesagwan-ro 34-gil, Hannam-daero 21-gil).

## 21 homestays in Hannam-dong

Hannam-dong has **21** urban homestays for foreign tourists. Of its 42 records in the data, **20 are closed and one cancelled** — as many have shut as are open.

| Designated | Hannam-dong | Bogwang-dong |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2012–2020 | 5 | 5 |
| 2021–2024 | 4 | 6 |
| **2025** | 4 | 3 |
| **2026 (to the 27 September update)** | **8** | 0 |
| Total | 21 | 14 |

- **12 of the 21 were designated in 2025 or later.** The oldest is "Stay Khaki" (스테이 카키) on Itaewon-ro 36-gil, from 2012
- **Three units in one building at 10 Hannam-daero 28-gil — numbers 201, 301 and 401 — were all designated on 1 July 2026.** They are called "Next Itaewon", "Nook Itaewon" and "Note Itaewon": **Itaewon in the name, Hannam-dong in the address**
- By lane: Itaewon-ro 54ga-gil (3), Hannam-daero 28-gil (3), Hannam-daero 42ga-gil (2), Itaewon-ro 36-gil and 42-gil (2 each)
- Of the 16 with a room count, **7 have one room and 7 have two**, with one three-room and one four-room
- The median floor area is **about 72 m²**, larger than in [Itaewon-dong](/en/posts/itaewon-hotels-2026-09/) (about 61 m²) or Haebangchon (about 54 m²). The largest is "Zion Guesthouse" (시온게스트하우스) on Hannam-daero 27ga-gil, designated in 2012, at 217 m²

### 14 in Bogwang-dong: the Usadan-ro lanes

Eight of Bogwang-dong's 14 homestays are in Usadan-ro side lanes — **Usadan-ro 4-gil (3), 4na-gil (2), 10-gil (2)** and 2-gil (1) — and two are on Jangmun-ro 17-gil.

- Only three were designated in 2025 or later, so **new ones are appearing more slowly** here than in Hannam-dong or Itaewon-dong
- **Three have "Itaewon" in the name** — "Itaewon Madang", "Lost in Itaewon" and "Itaewon Stay" — but their address is Bogwang-dong
- The median floor area is small, about 45 m². All nine with a room count have one or two rooms

## Homestays are for foreign visitors only

Article 2 of the Enforcement Decree of the Tourism Promotion Act (version in force from 4 August 2026) defines an urban homestay for foreign tourists as a business where **a city resident, in the house they live in**, provides lodging **to foreign tourists**.

1. **Korean nationals cannot, in principle, stay in them.** In Hannam-dong a Korean traveller needs one of the two hotels or the inn
2. **The host has to live there.** Renting out an empty flat is not legal under this licence

Airbnb made **business registration documents mandatory for existing listings from 16 October 2025**, and said listings without them **could not take bookings from 1 January 2026**. The platform may not filter bookings by nationality, so **ask the host about the licence type before you book.**

## The Hannam 3 redevelopment

South of Usadan-ro, the **Hannam 3 redevelopment zone** (around 686 Hannam-dong) is under way. According to Asia Economy (16 September 2026), **demolition is 99% complete**, and 123 buildings of up to 21 storeys with **5,970 homes** are planned. A green pedestrian route is planned along Usadan-ro.

- If your room is in a lane south of Usadan-ro, **check the distance to the construction zone on a map**
- On the north side of Usadan-ro, in Itaewon-dong, the Nuber Seoul Hotel and several inns use Usadan-ro addresses; they are covered in the [Itaewon and Haebangchon guide](/en/posts/itaewon-hotels-2026-09/)

## Just outside the boundary

- **Itaewon-dong** — west of Itaewon Station: Hamilton Hotel (166 rooms), Mondrian Seoul Itaewon (297), four hostels, six inns and 173 homestays (7th in Seoul). Mondrian's **address is Itaewon-dong**, as its name suggests
- **Haebangchon (Yongsan-dong 2-ga)** — 52 homestays, 38 of them designated since 2025

Both are in [Where to stay in Itaewon and Haebangchon](/en/posts/itaewon-hotels-2026-09/).

## Getting to the airport

**The Airport Railroad does not run through Hannam-dong.** Two airport bus routes stop at either end of the neighbourhood instead.

### Airport buses

Where to board **for the airport**:

| Route | Stop (number) | Adult fare | First | Last |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [[bus:6702]] K Limousine | Namsan (Grand Hyatt Seoul) (03680) | 18,000 won | 04:26 | **20:18** |
| [[bus:6010]] Airport Limousine | Hannam The Hill (Egyptian Embassy) (03749) | 17,000 won | 04:29 | 20:53 |
| [[bus:6010]] Airport Limousine | Hannam-dong, Cheongsan Building (03752) | 17,000 won | 04:30 | **20:54** |
| [[bus:6010]] Airport Limousine | Hannam Station (03736) | 17,000 won | 04:32 | **20:56** |

- **Route 6702** runs 25 times a day. After the Grand Hyatt it stops at Itaewon 2-dong Community Center (20:20) and Boseong Girls' School (20:21), covered in the [Itaewon guide](/en/posts/itaewon-hotels-2026-09/). It also **stops at the Grand Hyatt coming from the airport**, leaving **T1 06:05–23:05 and T2 05:45–22:45**. Inbound, the Grand Hyatt is the last of the three stops in the area, so it is the longest ride from the airport
- **Route 6010** runs 19 times a day. It starts at Wangsimni Station and goes via Eungbong, Geumho and Oksu, then Hannam The Hill, Hannam-dong and Hannam Station, then Dongbu Ichon-dong before heading to the airport. It suits rooms on the Hannam-daero and Hannam Station side
- The child fare is 12,000 won on both routes (about $9). Both operators say times can change with traffic

Hannam Station is on [[line:gyeongui]].

### Airport Railroad

To use [[line:arex]], **change at Gongdeok to [[line:6]]**, a single transfer. From Gongdeok the line runs through Hyochang Park, Samgakji and Noksapyeong; Itaewon is the fourth stop and **Hangangjin the fifth**.

| Section | Fare (transit card, AREX part) | First | Last |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Gongdeok → Incheon Airport T1 | 4,650 won | 05:24 | **23:44** |
| Gongdeok → Incheon Airport T2 | 5,250 won | 05:24 | **23:44** |

Weekday and weekend times are the same. The 23:22, 23:54 and 24:04 trains from Gongdeok **terminate at Geomam**, so don't board them for the airport.

**Coming in**, the last AREX to Seoul Station leaves **T1 at 23:38 and T2 at 23:32**. The 23:56 from T1 only runs to Digital Media City. **Whether Line 6 is still running when that last train reaches Gongdeok could not be confirmed**, so plan for a taxi if you land late.

For all the options, see [Incheon Airport to Seoul](/en/posts/incheon-airport-to-seoul-2026-09/).

## Which to choose

- **Korean traveller staying in Hannam-dong** — the Grand Hyatt (620 rooms), Imperial Palace (128) or the "Hannam 67 Hotel" inn. Homestays are off limits in principle
- **Resort-style hotel on the Namsan side** — the Grand Hyatt. Bus 6702 stops at the door
- **Between Itaewon and Hangangjin stations** — Imperial Palace, on Itaewon-ro between the two
- **Foreign visitor who wants a quiet residential street** — Hannam-dong's 21 homestays: roomier than average, but few
- **Near Hannam-daero or Hannam Station** — the 6010 stops at Hannam-dong and Hannam Station, last buses 20:54 and 20:56
- **South of Usadan-ro** — Bogwang-dong's 14 homestays. Check how close the Hannam 3 site is
- **More hotel choice** — Hamilton, Mondrian and four hostels in Itaewon-dong. [Where to stay in Itaewon and Haebangchon](/en/posts/itaewon-hotels-2026-09/)

## Summary

- Hannam-dong has **two tourist hotels with 748 rooms: Grand Hyatt Seoul (620) and Imperial Palace Boutique Hotel (128)**.
- Inns: one in Hannam-dong (Hannam 67 Hotel) and two in Bogwang-dong.
- **Homestays: 21 in Hannam-dong and 14 in Bogwang-dong.** Half of Hannam-dong's 42 records are closed, and its median floor area is a roomy 72 m².
- Homestays are allowed only **in the host's own home** and **for foreign visitors**.
- **Hannam 3**, south of Usadan-ro, is 99% demolished and will hold 5,970 homes.
- Airport buses: **6702 at the Grand Hyatt (last 20:18)** and **6010 at Hannam-dong (20:54) and Hannam Station (20:56)**; by rail, **change at Gongdeok to Line 6** for Hangangjin.
- Itaewon-dong and Haebangchon are covered in [Where to stay in Itaewon and Haebangchon](/en/posts/itaewon-hotels-2026-09/).
