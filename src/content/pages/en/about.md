"Now, Yeogi" (지금 여기는, "right here, right now") gathers hotel, travel, café and restaurant information for cities around the world in one place. We start with Korea (Seoul) and add cities one by one.

## How the guides are made

- Guides are **compiled from public sources** such as official listings, visitor reviews and travel videos. They are not first-hand visit reports. The sources used are listed at the end of each guide.
- Prices and opening hours are as of the time of writing and may change. Please check official channels before you go.
- Posts may include ads and affiliate links. They do not influence which places we cover. Sponsored posts are labeled as such.

<h2 id="editor">Editor</h2>

**Jin the Collector** (수집하는 진) writes and maintains the guides on Now, Yeogi. As the pen name suggests, the job is gathering scattered information and turning it into something you can actually use.

- Official sources come first: city and county notices, the Korea Tourism Organization, festival offices and open government data. Visitor reviews and videos add a sense of the place. When sources disagree, the official one wins, and anything that cannot be confirmed is left out.
- Dates, fares and timetables change, so each guide states when its facts were checked. When an event ends or an official notice changes, the guide is updated and the revision date is shown.
- Corrections are welcome and are checked and applied. Send them through the [contact](/en/contact/) page.

If you spot an error, let us know through the [contact](/en/contact/) page and we will correct it.
